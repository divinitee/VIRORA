## What Claude checks afterwards (read-only)

- **CoachProfile**: one row per account. A: `everyday`, 15 min, trial / vira / velvet@2. B: `vocabulary`, 10, free / vira / vira@2.
- **PlanLog**: A day 1 `today:0`: `daily_initial` → `time_changed` → `evidence_changed` (3 words left) → `evidence_changed` (empty), with no extra rows from the reopen and refresh. `continuation:1`: `continuation_initial` (third-person-s + 6 words) → `evidence_changed` (empty), and **no `continuation:2`**. Every row's minutes add up to no more than its budget. No queue ever holds a word missing English, Uzbek or Russian; "score" (`6a42659782466c80f8ae0caa`) never appears.
- **ItemEvidence**: one row per item per round, `coach_session`, the right `session_key`, `server_graded`, `weight 0.6`. Scores as answered (continuous now 2/5, missed word 0/1, …).
- **LearnerItem**: no duplicates; every row matches a fresh offline replay of its evidence (ts-fsrs 5.4.2). Reviews are due +8 days for perfect items and +1 day for the misses.
- **Function logs** (coachApi, progressApi) for your time windows: only `POST → 200`. No errors, no `coach applyRound failed`, no 5xx. A 409 `nothing_to_continue` is fine; it only appears if a stale "Keep going" was tapped.
