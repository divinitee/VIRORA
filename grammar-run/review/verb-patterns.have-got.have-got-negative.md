## transform (15)

1. _Make the sentence negative._  
   She has got a **dog** called **Max**.  
   → She ______ a **dog** called **Max**.  
   key: hasn't got  
   why: She → hasn't got.

2. _Make the sentence negative._  
   We have got some **bread** at home, **Mum**.  
   → We ______ any **bread** at home, **Mum**.  
   key: haven't got  
   why: We → haven't got. In negatives, some becomes any.

3. _Make the sentence negative._  
   My **aunt** has got a **blue** car.  
   → My **aunt** ______ a **blue** car.  
   key: hasn't got  
   why: One person → hasn't got.

4. _Make the sentence negative._  
   I have got a **book** for you, **Olga**.  
   → I ______ a **book** for you, **Olga**.  
   key: haven't got  
   why: I → haven't got.

5. _Make the sentence negative._  
   Our **old house** has got a **balcony**.  
   → Our **old house** ______ a **balcony**.  
   key: hasn't got  
   why: Our flat or house is one thing → hasn't got.

6. _Correct the mistake._  
   She haven't got a **phone**, **Timur**.  
   → She ______ a **phone**, **Timur**.  
   key: hasn't got  
   why: She → hasn't got, not haven't got.

7. _Correct the mistake._  
   I don't got a **ruler**, **Aziz**.  
   → I ______ a **ruler**, **Aziz**.  
   key: haven't got  
   why: With got, the negative is haven't got, not don't got.

8. _Correct the mistake._  
   **Lola** hasn't get any **sisters**.  
   → **Lola** ______ any **sisters**.  
   key: hasn't got  
   why: Got never changes: hasn't got, not hasn't get.

9. _Correct the mistake._  
   They hasn't got a **rabbit** in their **room**.  
   → They ______ a **rabbit** in their **room**.  
   key: haven't got  
   why: They → haven't got, not hasn't got.

10. _Correct the mistake._  
   We haven't have any **bread**, **Mum**.  
   → We ______ any **bread**, **Mum**.  
   key: haven't got  
   why: The negative is haven't got, not haven't have.

11. _Rewrite with haven't got or hasn't got._  
   I don't have a **watch**, **Lola**.  
   → I ______ a **watch**, **Lola**.  
   key: haven't got  
   why: I don't have = I haven't got.
