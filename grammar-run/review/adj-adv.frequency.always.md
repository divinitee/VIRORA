## transform (15)

1. _Rewrite the sentence with always instead of the words every day or every time._  
   I drink **juice** after **swimming** every time.  
   → I ______ **juice** after **swimming**.  
   key: always drink  
   why: Every time, with no exceptions, is what always means. Always goes before the main verb or after be.

## express (5)

1. Describe your morning routine. What do you always do before you leave home? Write three or four sentences.  
   target: always for routines · look for: always with a routine · always before the main verb · at least two different actions

2. Describe a family tradition. What does your family always do at Navruz, New Year or a birthday? Write three or four sentences.  
   target: always for traditions · look for: always with a tradition · a holiday or event named · present simple verbs

3. Describe a friend or family member. What are they always like? What do they always do? Write three or four sentences.  
   target: always with be and with main verbs · look for: is always + adjective · always + main verb · he or she with the -s form

4. Compare things you always do with things you usually do at the weekend. Write three or four sentences.  
   target: always vs usually · look for: always for no exceptions · usually for most times · at the weekend or at weekends

5. Describe a place you know well, such as a bazaar, a park or your school. What is always true about it? Write three or four sentences.  
   target: always for facts that do not change · look for: always used two or more times · a place named · be or a main verb after always as needed
