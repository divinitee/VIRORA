## transform (15)

1. _Rewrite the second sentence with a pronoun instead of the repeated words._  
   This is Aziz. Aziz is from **Nukus** and likes **football**.  
   → This is Aziz. ______ is from **Nukus** and likes **football**.  
   key: He  
   why: Aziz is one boy and the subject, so use He instead of repeating his name.

2. _Rewrite the second sentence with a pronoun instead of the repeated words._  
   My mum is a teacher. My mum teaches **maths** at a school in **Fergana**.  
   → My mum is a teacher. ______ teaches **maths** at a school in **Fergana**.  
   key: She  
   why: My mum is one woman and the subject, so use She.

3. _Rewrite the second sentence with a pronoun instead of the repeated words._  
   My parents have a car. My parents drive to **work** on **Saturdays**.  
   → My parents have a car. ______ drive to **work** on **Saturdays**.  
   key: They  
   why: My parents are two people and the subject, so use They.

4. _Rewrite the second sentence with a pronoun instead of the repeated words._  
   Our school is big. Our school has a **garden** and a **computer room**.  
   → Our school is big. ______ has a **garden** and a **computer room**.  
   key: It  
   why: Our school is one thing and the subject, so use It.

5. _Rewrite the second sentence with a pronoun instead of the repeated words._  
   Malika and I like books. Malika and I read **comics** before **lessons**.  
   → Malika and I like books. ______ read **comics** before **lessons**.  
   key: We  
   why: Malika and I means me and another person. As the subject, use We.

6. _Correct the pronoun._

## create (8)

1. Write one sentence about yourself: your name and where you are from.  
   must use: I + verb · exactly one sentence · must start with I · must name a city or town

2. Write two sentences about your best friend. Use their name in the first sentence and a pronoun in the second.  
   must use: he / she as subject · exactly two sentences · name in sentence one · he or she starts sentence two

3. Write one sentence about you and your family. Start with We.  
   must use: We + verb · exactly one sentence · must start with We · must say where you live or what you do

4. Write two sentences about your grandparents. Use They in the second sentence.  
   must use: They as subject · exactly two sentences · They in sentence two · no them or their as the subject

5. Write two sentences about your phone or your bag. Use It in the second sentence.  
   must use: It as subject · exactly two sentences · It in sentence two · not Its as the subject

6. Write one sentence about a teacher at your school. Start with He or She.  
   must use: He / She + verb · exactly one sentence · must start with He or She · must say what the teacher teaches or does

7. Write one sentence about what you and your friends do after school.  
   must use: We + verb · exactly one sentence · must use We as the subject · must name an activity

8. Write three short sentences: one with I, one with he or she, one with they.  
   must use: I / he / she / they as subjects · exactly three sentences · a different subject pronoun in each · no me, him, her or them as a subject

## express (5)

1. Introduce yourself to a new classmate. Write three or four sentences about you and your family.  
   target: subject pronouns I / we / he / she · look for: I am … · a family member, then he or she · we for you and your family

2. Describe two people in your family. Say who they are, where they work or study and what they like.  
   target: he / she / they as subjects · look for: he or she instead of repeating the name · they for two people · present simple verbs

3. Write about your best friend: who they are, where they live and what you do together.  
   target: he / she / we · look for: he or she for the friend · we for you and your friend · no him / her as a subject

4. Describe your home or your school in three or four sentences.  
   target: it / they as subjects · look for: it for one thing · they for more than one · is / has / are

5. Write a short message to a pen friend about your town and the people in it.  
   target: I / we / they / it · look for: I and we about yourself · it for the town · they for people
