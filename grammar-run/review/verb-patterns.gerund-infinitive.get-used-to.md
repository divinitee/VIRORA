# verb-patterns.gerund-infinitive.get-used-to — review sample (B1)

Each item shows one random filler combination (in bold). Mark anything a teacher wouldn't accept.

## choose (20)

1. It took me a few weeks to ______ waking up at **five**, **Feruza**.  
   options: ✔ get used to · used to · get use to · use to  
   why: "It took me a few weeks" = a slow change, so get used to; then -ing.

2. The food in **Korea** was strange, but after a month I ______ it, **Dilnoza**.  
   options: used to · got use to · was use to · ✔ got used to  
   why: After a month the food stopped feeling strange: got used to + noun.

3. I'm still ______ my new **laptop**. The buttons are different, **Malika**.  
   options: get used to · got used to · ✔ getting used to · getting use to  
   why: "Still" = the change is happening now: am getting used to.

4. You'll soon ______ the noise from the **street**, **Otabek**.  
   options: use to · ✔ get used to · used to · get use to  
   why: "Soon" = in the future the noise will feel normal: get used to.

5. My grandma ______ her hearing aid quickly, **Malika**. Now she hears everything at the **wedding**.  
   options: ✔ got used to · used to · got use to · was use to  
   why: She became comfortable with it quickly, in the past: got used to.

6. Our new **teacher** is ______ the cold winters here, **Otabek**.  
   options: getting use to · get used to · got used to · ✔ getting used to  
   why: It is happening now, little by little: is getting used to.

7. I can't ______ driving on the left in **Australia**, **Zarina**.  
   options: get use to · use to · ✔ get used to · used to  
   why: "Can't" + the base form get; driving on the left still feels strange.

8. At first the uniform felt strange, but the **nurses** soon ______ wearing it.  
   options: was use to · ✔ got used to · used to · got use to  
   why: "Soon" in a past story: got used to + -ing.

9. Are you ______ your new **job** yet, **Feruza**?  
   options: ✔ getting used to · getting use to · get used to · got used to  
   why: "Yet" asks about a change that is still happening: getting used to.

10. It's hard to ______ a new **city** at my age, **Malika**.  
   options: used to · get use to · use to · ✔ get used to  
   why: After "to" (it's hard to) comes the base form get: get used to.

11. Did you get used to ______ in **Seoul** in the end, **Dilnoza**?  
   options: lived · to live · ✔ living · live  
   why: The "to" in get used to is a preposition, so the verb takes -ing: living.

12. I'm getting used to ______ English at work, **Zarina**.  
   options: to speak · ✔ speaking · speak · spoke  
   why: get used to + -ing: speaking, not speak.

13. The baby still can't get used to ______ in her own **bedroom**.  
   options: ✔ sleeping · sleep · slept · to sleep  
   why: after get used to, the verb is -ing: sleeping.

14. My dad got used to ______ at night when he was a **guard**.  
   options: work · worked · to work · ✔ working  
   why: got used to + -ing: working.

15. **My brother** is slowly getting used to ______ for the whole family, **Sardor**.  
   options: cooked · to cook · ✔ cooking · cook  
   why: getting used to + -ing: cooking.

16. I'll never get used to ______ the **minibus** at six in the morning, **Zarina**.  
   options: to take · ✔ taking · take · took  
   why: never get used to + -ing: taking.

17. It took her a year to get used to ______ **a uniform**, **Feruza**.  
   options: ✔ wearing · wear · wore · to wear  
   why: get used to + -ing: wearing.

18. We got used to ______ dinner at **half past nine** in **Italy**.  
   options: eat · ate · to eat · ✔ eating  
   why: got used to + -ing: eating.

19. You'll get used to ______ up early for the **new job**, **Ulugbek**.  
   options: got · to get · ✔ getting · get  
   why: get used to + -ing: getting up.

20. My legs hurt, but I'm getting used to ______ **an hour** a day, **Dilnoza**.  
   options: to walk · ✔ walking · walk · walked  
   why: getting used to + -ing: walking.
