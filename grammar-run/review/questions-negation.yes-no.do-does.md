## transform (15)

1. _Make a yes/no question. Write one word in each gap._  
   You play **chess** on **Monday**.  
   → ______ you ______ **chess** on **Monday**?  
   key: Do + play  
   why: The subject is you or they, so use Do, then the base verb play.

2. _Make a yes/no question. Write one word in each gap._  
   They live near the **park**, **Olga**.

   key: Do + live  
   why: The subject is you or they, so use Do, then the base verb live.
