## choose (20)

   options: ✔ an · a · – · the

   target: a / an · look for: a + consonant sound · an + vowel sound · There is

   target: a / an + job · look for: a + job · an + job · is

   target: a / an with tricky sounds · look for: an hour · a university or a uniform · three or four sentences

   target: a / an · look for: a + place · an old or an empty · There is
