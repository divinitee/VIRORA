## transform (15)

1. _Answer the question. Use in and the mountains._  
   Where does your **cousin** live, **Timur**?  
   → He lives ______ mountains, **Timur**.  
   key: in the  
   why: The mountains are an area: in the mountains.

2. _Answer the question. Use in and the yard._  
   Where is the **puppy**, **Aziz**?  
   → It's ______ yard, **Aziz**.  
   key: in the  
   why: The yard is an area with a fence around it: in the yard.

3. _Answer the question. Use in and the forest._  
   Where do the **squirrels** live, **Olga**?  
   → They live ______ forest, **Olga**.  
   key: in the  
   why: The forest is an area: in the forest.

4. _Answer the question. Use in and the countryside._  
   Where does your **grandma** have a house, **Olga**?  
   → She has a house ______ countryside, **Olga**.  
   key: in the  
   why: We say in the countryside.

5. _Answer the question. Use in and the sky._  
   Where are the **clouds**, **Timur**?  
   → They're ______ sky, **Timur**. Look up!  
   key: in the  
   why: We say in the sky, not on the sky.

6. _Correct the mistake._  
   My aunt lives at **Bukhara**, **Lola**.

## create (8)

1. Write one sentence about the town or city where you live.  
   must use: I live in … · exactly one sentence · in + a town or city · a place name

2. Write one sentence about a relative who lives in another town or village.  
   must use: My … lives in … · exactly one sentence · in · a family word

3. Write one sentence about something you can see in the sky at night.  
   must use: There are … in the sky. · exactly one sentence · in the sky · a thing word

4. Write one sentence about a good place in your town.  
   must use: There's a … in our town. · exactly one sentence · in our town · a place word

5. Write one sentence about the mountains, the countryside or the sea.  
   must use: … in the mountains / in the sea · exactly one sentence · in the · a nature word

6. Write one sentence about what children do in the yard of your building or house.  
   must use: The children … in the yard. · exactly one sentence · in the yard · an action word

7. Write one sentence about a country you want to visit and why.  
   must use: I want to see … in … · exactly one sentence · in + a country · a reason

8. Write two sentences: one about your town and one about your country.  
   must use: … in … / … in Uzbekistan · exactly two sentences · in twice · two place names
