## transform (15)

1. _Rewrite the sentence with a possessive pronoun._  
   This is my **pen**, **Aziz**.  
   → This **pen** is ______, **Aziz**.  
   key: mine  
   why: My + noun → mine: This is my phone = This phone is mine.

2. _Rewrite the sentence with a possessive pronoun._  
   Is this your **pen**, **Timur**?  
   → Is this **pen** ______, **Timur**?  
   key: yours  
   why: Your + noun → yours: Is this phone yours?

3. _Rewrite the sentence with a possessive pronoun._  
   These are our **bags**, **Olga**.  
   → These **bags** are ______, **Olga**.  
   key: ours  
   why: Our + noun → ours: These books are ours.

4. _Rewrite the sentence with a possessive pronoun._  
   That is **Rustam**'s **phone**.  
   → That **phone** is ______.  
   key: his  
   why: Aziz's thing → his: That phone is his.

5. _Rewrite the sentence with a possessive pronoun._  
   Those are their **pens**, **Mum**.  
   → Those **pens** are ______, **Mum**.  
   key: theirs  
   why: Their + noun → theirs: Those books are theirs.

   target: Mine is … , but his / hers is … · look for: mine · his or hers · three or four sentences

   target: Mine is … , and yours is … · look for: mine · yours · three or four sentences
