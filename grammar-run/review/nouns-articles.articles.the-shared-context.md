## create (8)

1. Write two sentences about a pet. Use a in the first sentence and the in the second.  
   must use: a ... the (second mention) · exactly two sentences · first uses a · second uses the

2. Ask someone at home to close or open something. Use the.  
   must use: the (only one here) · exactly one sentence · must use the · must use close or open

3. Ask where something in your kitchen is. Use the.  
   must use: the (shared) · exactly one question · must use the · must start with Where

4. Write two sentences about something you bought. Use a, then the.  
   must use: a ... the · exactly two sentences · first uses a · second uses the

5. Ask someone at the table to pass you something.  
   must use: the (shared) · exactly one question · must use the · must use pass

6. Write one sentence about the lights or the windows in your room.  
   must use: the + plural · exactly one sentence · must use the · must use lights or windows

7. Write two sentences about a film you saw. Use a, then the.  
   must use: a ... the · exactly two sentences · first uses a · second uses the

8. Write one sentence about the water or the milk in your fridge.  
   must use: the + uncountable · exactly one sentence · must use the water or the milk

## express (5)

1. Describe your home: there is a..., and then say more about it with the. Write three or four sentences.  
   target: a ... the · look for: a + first mention · the + second mention · There is

2. Write about a trip: you stayed at a hotel and ate at a café. Write three or four sentences.  
   target: a ... the · look for: a hotel or a café · the hotel or the café · was

3. Write a short note to your family about things in the kitchen. Write three or four sentences.  
   target: the (shared) · look for: the + kitchen things · please · where

4. Write about a present you got. Write three or four sentences.  
   target: a ... the · look for: a + present · the + present · a colour or size

5. Write about your pets or a friend's pets. Write three or four sentences.  
   target: a ... the · look for: a + animal · the + animal · likes
