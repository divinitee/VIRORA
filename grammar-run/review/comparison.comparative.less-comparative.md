## transform (15)

1. _Say the same thing with less ... than._  
   The bus is not as expensive as the **plane**, **Timur**.  
   → The bus is ______ ______ than the **plane**, **Timur**.  
   key: less + expensive  
   why: Not as expensive as means the same as less expensive than.

2. _Say the same thing with less ... than._  
   For me, **English** is not as difficult as **physics**.  
   → For me, **English** is ______ ______ than **physics**.  
   key: less + difficult  
   why: Not as difficult as means the same as less difficult than.

3. _Say the same thing with less ... than._  
   The **library** is not as crowded as the **market** today.  
   → The **library** is ______ ______ than the **market** today.  
   key: less + crowded  
   why: Not as crowded as means the same as less crowded than.

4. _Say the same thing with less ... than._  
   This **book** is not as useful as that one, **Timur**.  
   → This **book** is ______ ______ than that one, **Timur**.  
   key: less + useful  
   why: Not as useful as means the same as less useful than.

5. _Say the same thing with less ... than._  
   My **grandmother** is not as strict as my **uncle**.  
   → My **grandmother** is ______ ______ than my **uncle**.  
   key: less + strict  
   why: Not as strict as means the same as less strict than.

6. _Say the same thing from the other side. Use less._  
   The red **lamp** is more expensive than the blue one, **Mum**.  
   → The blue **lamp** is ______ ______ than the red one, **Mum**.  
   key: less + expensive  
   why: If one thing is more expensive, the other is less expensive: less + the plain adjective.

7. _Say the same thing from the other side. Use less._  
   At our school, **basketball** is more popular than **boxing**.  
   → At our school, **boxing** is ______ ______ than **basketball**.  
   key: less + popular  
   why: If one thing is more popular, the other is less popular: less + the plain adjective.

8. _Say the same thing from the other side. Use less._  
   The first **lesson** was more interesting than the second, **Aziz**.  
   → The second **lesson** was ______ ______ than the first, **Aziz**.  
   key: less + interesting  
   why: If one thing is more interesting, the other is less interesting: less + the plain adjective.

9. _Say the same thing from the other side. Use less._  
   In **autumn**, city life is more tiring than village life for my **father**.  
   → In **autumn**, village life is ______ ______ than city life for my **father**.  
   key: less + tiring

10. _Say the same thing from the other side. Use less._  
   The **park** at night is more dangerous than in the daytime, **Timur**.  
   → The **park** in the daytime is ______ ______ than at night, **Timur**.  
   key: less + dangerous  
   why: If one thing is more dangerous, the other is less dangerous: less + the plain adjective.

11. _Correct the mistake._  
   This **watch** is less expensiver than that one, **Aziz**.  
   → This **watch** is ______ ______ than that one, **Aziz**.  
   key: less + expensive  
   why: Use less + the plain adjective: less expensive. Less already makes the comparison, so do not add -er.

12. _Correct the mistake._  
   The **lesson** was fewer difficult than the last one, **Olga**.  
   → The **lesson** was ______ ______ than the last one, **Olga**.  
   key: less + difficult  
   why: Before an adjective use less. Fewer is only for things you can count.

13. _Correct the mistake._  
   Tea is less sweet then **cola**, **Aziz**.  
   → Tea is less sweet ______ **cola**, **Aziz**.  
   key: than  
   why: After less + adjective we use than, not then. Then is about time.

14. _Correct the mistake._  
   The **park** is little crowded than the market, **Timur**.  
   → The **park** is ______ ______ than the market, **Timur**.  
   key: less + crowded  
   why: To compare, use less + adjective + than. Little does not compare two things.

15. _Correct the mistake._  
   This **cartoon** is less interesting from the book, **Aziz**.  
   → This **cartoon** is less interesting ______ the book, **Aziz**.  
   key: than  
   why: In English the second thing comes after than, not from.
