## transform (15)

1. _Don't say the name or the noun again. Use a pronoun._  
   Aziz is my friend. I often play **basketball** with Aziz at the **yard**.  
   → Aziz is my friend. I often play **basketball** with ______ at the **yard**.  
   key: him  
   why: Aziz is one boy, after with: him.

2. _Don't say the name or the noun again. Use a pronoun._  
   My aunt is a **teacher**. Everybody in our **town** knows my aunt.  
   → My aunt is a **teacher**. Everybody in our **town** knows ______.  
   key: her  
   why: My aunt is one woman, after knows: her.

3. _Don't say the name or the noun again. Use a pronoun._  
   These **socks** are dirty. Please wash the **socks**, **Aziz**.  
   → These **socks** are dirty. Please wash ______, **Aziz**.  
   key: them  
   why: The things are more than one, after wash: them.

4. _Don't say the name or the noun again. Use a pronoun._  
   Mum and I are in the **garden**. **Grandma** is looking for Mum and me.  
   → Mum and I are in the **garden**. **Grandma** is looking for ______.  
   key: us  
   why: Mum and me = us after for.

5. _Don't say the name or the noun again. Use a pronoun._  
   **Mum**, I have a new **bag**. I take the **bag** to school every day.  
   → **Mum**, I have a new **bag**. I take ______ to school every day.  
   key: it  
   why: One thing, after take: it.

6. _Correct the mistake._  
   Mr Karimov is my neighbour. I see he at the **gate** every morning.  
   → Mr Karimov is my neighbour. I see ______ at the **gate** every morning.  
   key: him  
   why: After see we use the object form: him, not he.

7. _Correct the mistake._  
   Madina is in my **team**. I sit next to she, **Dad**.  
   → Madina is in my **team**. I sit next to ______, **Dad**.  
   key: her  
   why: After next to we use the object form: her, not she.

8. _Correct the mistake._  
   Please help we, **Uncle Bobur**! The **box** is very heavy.  
   → Please help ______, **Uncle Bobur**! The **box** is very heavy.  
   key: us  
   why: After help we use the object form: us, not we.

9. _Correct the mistake._  
   My **cats** are in the **street**. Can you see they?  
   → My **cats** are in the **street**. Can you see ______?  
   key: them  
   why: After see we use the object form: them, not they.

10. _Correct the mistake._  
   **Olga**, can you lend I your **pen**?  
   → **Olga**, can you lend ______ your **pen**?  
   key: me  
   why: After lend we use the object form: me, not I.

11. _Change grandfather to grandparents. Then change the pronoun._  
   We visit our grandfather on Sundays. We bring him **flowers**, **Dad**.  
   → We visit our grandparents on Sundays. We bring ______ **flowers**, **Dad**.  
   key: them  
   why: Grandparents are more than one person: him → them.

12. _Change Aziz to Lola. Then change the pronoun._  
   Aziz is late. Let's wait for him at the **cafe**, **Olga**.  
   → Lola is late. Let's wait for ______ at the **cafe**, **Olga**.  
   key: her  
   why: Lola is a girl: him → her.

13. _Change friend to friends. Then change the pronoun._  
   My friend is at the **gate**. Can you let him in, **Mum**?  
   → My friends are at the **gate**. Can you let ______ in, **Mum**?  
   key: them  
   why: Friends are more than one person: him → them.

14. _Change this bag to these bags. Then change the pronoun._  
   I like this **green** bag, **Aunt Lola**. I want to buy it.  
   → I like these **green** bags, **Aunt Lola**. I want to buy ______.  
   key: them  
   why: Bags are more than one thing: it → them.

15. _Change Dad to Mum. Then change the pronoun._  
   Dad is in the **garden**. Please take this **tea** to him.  
   → Mum is in the **garden**. Please take this **tea** to ______.  
   key: her  
   why: Mum is a woman: him → her.

## create (8)

1. Write one sentence about a friend and what you do with him or her.  
   must use: with him / with her · exactly one sentence · must use him or her · a friend's name

2. Write one sentence about a person in your family who helps you.  
   must use: helps me · exactly one sentence · must use me · a family word

3. Write one sentence about your family and a person who visits you.  
   must use: visits us · exactly one sentence · must use us · about your family

4. Write one sentence about two or more people or animals you like.  
   must use: like them · exactly one sentence · must use them · about you

5. Write one sentence about a thing you use every day and why you like it.  
   must use: like it / use it · exactly one sentence · must use it as an object · about you

6. Write one question to a friend: ask them to help you with something.  
   must use: Can you help me …? · exactly one question · must use me · must end with a question mark

7. Write one sentence about a teacher and what the students think of him or her.  
   must use: like him / like her · exactly one sentence · must use him or her · a teacher

8. Write two sentences: name a person in the first, then use him or her in the second.  
   must use: name … him / her · exactly two sentences · a name in the first · him or her in the second

## express (5)

1. Tell a new friend about your best friend: what you do with them and why you like them.  
   target: him / her / them · look for: with him / with her · I like him / her · three or four sentences

2. Describe a person in your family who looks after you. What do they do for you?  
   target: me / us · look for: helps me / for us · a family word · three or four sentences

3. Write a message to your grandparents. Tell them you miss them and want to visit them.  
   target: them · look for: miss them · visit them · three or four sentences

4. Tell a friend about your favourite thing at home and what you do with it.  
   target: it · look for: use it / like it · what you do · three or four sentences

5. Write about a teacher you like. Say what the teacher does and why you like him or her.  
   target: him / her · look for: him or her · what the teacher does · three or four sentences
