## express (5)

1. Describe what you did last weekend. Write three or four sentences.  
   target: irregular past simple · look for: went, saw, ate or bought · a past time marker · at least three irregular verbs

2. Write about a holiday you remember. Where did you go and what did you do? Write three or four sentences.  
   target: irregular past simple · look for: went · took, saw or swam · a finished time

3. Tell a friend about a busy day yesterday. Write three or four sentences.  
   target: irregular past simple · look for: woke, got or left · a finished time · at least three irregular verbs

4. Describe a birthday or a family celebration. Who came, what did people bring and what did you eat? Write three or four sentences.  
   target: irregular past simple · look for: came · brought or gave · ate or drank

5. Write about a time you lost something and found it again. Write three or four sentences.  
   target: irregular past simple · look for: lost · found · thought, saw or went
