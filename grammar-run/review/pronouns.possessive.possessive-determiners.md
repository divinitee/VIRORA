# pronouns.possessive.possessive-determiners — review sample (A1)

Each item shows one random filler combination (in bold). Mark anything a teacher wouldn't accept.

## choose (20)

1. This is ______ **phone**. I got it **last month**.  
   options: ✔ my · me · I · mine

## transform (15)

1. _Correct the mistake._  
   This is me **watch**. I **chose** it yesterday.  
   → This is ______ **watch**. I **chose** it yesterday.  
   key: my  
   why: Before a noun, use my, not me.

2. _Correct the mistake._  
   This is my cousin. He name is **Ali**, and he lives in **Nukus**.  
   → This is my cousin. ______ name is **Ali**, and he lives in **Nukus**.  
   key: His  
   why: Before a noun, use his, not he.

3. _Correct the mistake._  
   My neighbours are nice. Them house has a **nice yard** and a **garage**.  
   → My neighbours are nice. ______ house has a **nice yard** and a **garage**.  
   key: Their  
   why: Before a noun, use their, not them.

4. _Correct the mistake._  
   The cat drinks it's milk in the **hall** every **day**.  
   → The cat drinks ______ milk in the **hall** every **day**.  
   key: its  
   why: It's means it is. For something that belongs to it, use its.

5. _Correct the mistake._  
   We like us **neighbour**. She is very **funny**.  
   → We like ______ **neighbour**. She is very **funny**.  
   key: our  
   why: Before a noun, use our, not us.

6. _Use a possessive word (my, your, his, her, its, our, their) instead of the name with 's._  
   This is Aziz's **camera**. He uses it **every day**.  
   → This is ______ **camera**. He uses it **every day**.  
   key: his  
   why: Aziz is a boy, so Aziz's becomes his.

7. _Use a possessive word (my, your, his, her, its, our, their) instead of the name with 's._  
   Lola is my friend. Lola's **father** is a **teacher**.  
   → Lola is my friend. ______ **father** is a **teacher**.  
   key: Her  
   why: Lola is a girl, so Lola's becomes her.

8. _Use a possessive word (my, your, his, her, its, our, their) instead of the name with 's._  
   My grandparents have a dog. My grandparents' dog is **old** and **brown**.  
   → My grandparents have a dog. ______ dog is **old** and **brown**.  
   key: Their  
   why: My grandparents are two people, so my grandparents' becomes their.

9. _Use a possessive word (my, your, his, her, its, our, their) instead of the name with 's._  
   Our school has a new gym. The school's gym is **clean** and has **big windows**.  
   → Our school has a new gym. ______ gym is **clean** and has **big windows**.  
   key: Its  
   why: The school is a thing, so the school's becomes its.

10. _Use a possessive word (my, your, his, her, its, our, their) instead of the name with 's._  
   We are the Karimov family. The Karimov family's flat is on the **eighth** floor, and it has **four** rooms.  
   → We are the Karimov family. ______ flat is on the **eighth** floor, and it has **four** rooms.  
   key: Our  
   why: We are the Karimov family, so the Karimov family's becomes our.

11. _Change I to She._  
   I love my **dog**. I play with it every **day**.  
   → She loves ______ **dog**. She plays with it every **day**.  
   key: her  
   why: The owner is now she, so my changes to her.

12. _Change I to They._  
   I do my homework in the **garden** **on Sundays**.  
   → They do ______ homework in the **garden** **on Sundays**.  
   key: their  
   why: The owner is now they, so my changes to their.

13. _Change She to He._  
   She washes her **bike** every **Sunday**.  
   → He washes ______ **bike** every **Sunday**.  
   key: his  
   why: The owner is now he, so her changes to his.

14. _Change He to We._  
   He visits his **grandparents** in **Kokand** every summer.  
   → We visit ______ **grandparents** in **Kokand** every summer.  
   key: our  
   why: The owner is now we, so his changes to our.

15. _Change They to I._  
   They call their **uncle** every **day**.  
   → I call ______ **uncle** every **day**.  
   key: my  
   why: The owner is now I, so their changes to my.

## create (8)

1. Write one sentence about your name and your age.  
   must use: my name / my age · exactly one sentence · must use my · my before a noun

2. Write one sentence about a friend's family. Use his or her.  
   must use: his / her + noun · exactly one sentence · must use his or her · a family word after it

3. Write one sentence about your family's home. Use our.  
   must use: our + noun · exactly one sentence · must use our · must say something about the home

4. Write one sentence about your neighbours or your grandparents. Use their.  
   must use: their + noun · exactly one sentence · must use their · a noun after their

5. Write one sentence about an animal and something that belongs to it. Use its.  
   must use: its + noun · exactly one sentence · must use its, not it's · must name an animal

6. Ask a new classmate one question with your.  
   must use: your + noun · exactly one question · must use your · must end with a question mark

7. Write one sentence about your favourite thing at home.  
   must use: my favourite + noun · exactly one sentence · must use my · must name the thing

8. Write three short sentences about three people in your family and one thing each of them has.  
   must use: my / his / her / their · exactly three sentences · a possessive word in each · no me, him or them before a noun

## express (5)

1. Introduce your family: say each person's name, age and job.  
   target: my / his / her + noun · look for: my mother / my father · his name / her name · a possessive before every noun it owns

2. Describe your room: what is in it and whose things are they?  
   target: my / our / his / her · look for: my + noun · a brother's or sister's things with his / her · no me / him before a noun

3. Write about your best friend and their family in three or four sentences.  
   target: his / her / their · look for: his or her for the friend · their for the family · names of family members

4. Describe your school or your town and the things it has.  
   target: our / its · look for: our school / our town · its + noun · its, not it's

5. Write a short message to a pen friend about your home and your family.  
   target: possessive determiners · look for: my and our · his / her for family members · three or more possessive words
