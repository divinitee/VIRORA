## transform (15)

1. _Correct the mistake._  
   My parents want that I study **economics**, **Malika**.  
   → My parents want ______ **economics**, **Malika**.  
   key: me to study  
   why: want + me + to + base, not "want that I …".

2. _Correct the mistake._  
   I want that you come for dinner on **Sunday**, **Jamshid**.  
   → I want ______ for dinner on **Sunday**, **Jamshid**.  
   key: you to come  
   why: want + you + to + base: you to come.

3. _Correct the mistake._  
   The teacher wants us reading more **books**, **Ulugbek**.  
   → The teacher wants ______ more **books**, **Ulugbek**.  
   key: us to read  
   why: want + us + to + base, not -ing.

4. _Correct the mistake._  
   Mum wanted me help her in the **shop**.  
   → Mum wanted ______ her in the **shop**, **Dilnoza**.  
   key: me to help  
   why: want + me + to + base: don't forget "to".

5. _Correct the mistake._  
   Our boss wants we finish the **project** today, **Otabek**.  
   → Our boss wants ______ the **project** today, **Otabek**.  
   key: us to finish  
   why: After want, the person is an object: us, not we.

6. _Rewrite with want / wanted + person + to._  
   My dad said: 'Please wash the **car**.' He asked me.  
   → My dad wanted ______ the **car**, **Jamshid**.  
   key: me to wash  
   why: Dad's request to me: wanted me to wash.

7. _Rewrite with want / wanted + person + to._  
   The coach said to the boys: 'Run **ten laps** every morning.'  
   → The coach wants ______ **ten laps** every morning, **Jamshid**.  
   key: them to run  
   why: The coach's wish for the boys: them to run.

8. _Rewrite with want / wanted + person + to._  
   Grandma always says to us: 'Eat more **fruit**!', **Sardor**.  
   → Grandma wants ______ more **fruit**, **Sardor**.  
   key: us to eat  
   why: Grandma's wish for us: us to eat.

9. _Rewrite with want / wanted + person + to._

10. _Rewrite with want / wanted + person + to._

## create (8)

1. Write one sentence about what your parents want you to do.  
   must use: My parents want me to … · exactly one sentence · want + me + to · base verb

2. Write one sentence about what you want a friend to do.  
   must use: I want you / him / her to … · exactly one sentence · want + person + to · base verb

3. Write one sentence with would like + person + to.  
   must use: I'd like you to … · exactly one sentence · would like · person + to + base

4. Write one sentence about what a teacher wants the class to do.  
   must use: The teacher wants us to … · exactly one sentence · wants us to · base verb

5. Write one sentence with doesn't want + person + to.  
   must use: … doesn't want me to … · exactly one sentence · doesn't want · person + to + base

6. Write one sentence with want to (yourself) and one with want + person + to.  
   must use: want to vs want someone to · exactly two sentences · want to + base · want + person + to + base

7. Write one question: Do you want me to …?  
   must use: Do you want me to …? · exactly one question · want + me + to · a question mark

8. Write one sentence about what a doctor wanted someone to do.  
   must use: The doctor wanted him / her to … · exactly one sentence · wanted · person + to + base

## express (5)

1. Write three or four sentences about what your family wants you to do in the future.  
   target: want + person + to · look for: want me to · would like me to · three or four sentences

2. You are a teacher. Write three or four sentences about what you want your students to do.  
   target: want + person + to · look for: want you to · would like · three or four sentences

3. Write three or four sentences about a party you are planning: what do you want your friends to do?  
   target: want / would like + person + to · look for: want them to · would like · three or four sentences

4. Write three or four sentences about rules at home: what do your parents want and not want you to do?  
   target: (don't) want + person + to · look for: want me to · don't want me to · three or four sentences

5. Write a short message (three or four sentences) to a new classmate with Do you want me to …?  
   target: Do you want me to …? · look for: want me to · a question · three or four sentences
