1. _Write the verb in brackets in the right form. Use statement order: no do / does / did._  
   Can you tell me where the station ______ (be), **Olga**? I'm near the **bazaar**.  
   key: is  
   why: Subject before verb: the station is.

2. _Write the verb in brackets in the right form. Use statement order: no do / does / did._  
   Do you know how much a ticket to **Samarkand** ______ (cost) **this month**?  
   key: costs  
   why: No does, so the verb takes -s: costs.

   why: Where does she live → where she lives.

## create (8)

1. Ask a stranger politely where the nearest bus stop is.  
   must use: Could you tell me where … is? · exactly one question · where + subject + is · no question order after where

2. Ask a shop worker politely how much something costs.  
   must use: Can you tell me how much … costs? · exactly one question · how much + subject + verb · no does

3. Ask a friend politely what time the match starts.  
   must use: Do you know what time … starts? · exactly one question · what time + subject + verb · no does

4. Write a sentence saying you don't know where a friend lives.  
   must use: I don't know where … lives. · exactly one sentence · where + subject + verb · lives with -s

5. Write a sentence saying you can't remember what you did last weekend.  
   must use: I can't remember what I did … · exactly one sentence · what + subject + past verb · no did after what

6. Ask a teacher politely why a lesson is cancelled.  
   must use: Could you tell me why … is …? · exactly one question · why + subject + is · statement order

7. Ask a hotel worker politely how you can get to the old town.  
   must use: Could you tell me how I can get to …? · exactly one question · how + I + can · statement order

8. Write two polite questions for a new classmate: one with where and one with what.  
   must use: Can I ask where you … / Do you know what …? · exactly two questions · two different wh-words · statement order in both

## express (5)

1. You are a tourist in a new city. Write polite questions to ask at the hotel desk.  
   target: Could you tell me where … is / how much … costs? · look for: wh-word + statement order · different wh-words · three or four questions

2. Write a message to a friend who missed a class: say what you don't know about the homework and ask them to find out.  
   target: I don't know what … / Can you ask when …? · look for: wh-word + statement order · no do / does inside · three or four sentences

3. You lost your bag. Write what you can't remember and ask a worker for help.  
   target: I can't remember where I … / Do you know where …? · look for: wh-word + statement order · past verbs · three or four sentences

4. Write polite questions to ask a doctor or nurse.  
   target: Could you tell me how often I … / why …? · look for: wh-word + statement order · different wh-words · three or four questions

5. You are planning a trip with your class. Write what you need to find out.  
   target: We need to know how much … costs / when … leaves. · look for: wh-word + statement order · no question order inside · three or four sentences
