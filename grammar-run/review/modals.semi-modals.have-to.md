## transform (15)

1. _Rewrite the sentence with have to or has to._  
   Students must wear a uniform at **school** **for exams**.  
   → Students ______ (wear) a uniform at **school** **for exams**.  
   key: have to wear  
   why: Students is plural, so must becomes have to + base verb.

2. _Rewrite the sentence with have to or has to._  
   Drivers must stop at the **border** **in the city**.  
   → Drivers ______ (stop) at the **border** **in the city**.  
   key: have to stop  
   why: Drivers is plural: have to stop.

3. _Rewrite the sentence with have to or has to._  
   Aziz must finish the **project** **by Friday**.  
   → Aziz ______ (finish) the **project** **by Friday**.  
   key: has to finish  
   why: Aziz is one person (he), so must becomes has to.

4. _Rewrite the sentence with have to or has to._  
   Passengers must show their **passports** at the **gate**.  
   → Passengers ______ (show) their **passports** at the **gate**.  
   key: have to show  
   why: Passengers is plural: have to show.

5. _Rewrite the sentence with have to or has to._  
   My sister must take her **pills** **twice a day**.  
   → My sister ______ (take) her **pills** **twice a day**.  
   key: has to take  
   why: My sister is she, so use has to.

6. _Rewrite the sentence about the new person._  
   I have to get up at **six**, **Lola**.  
   → My dad ______ (get) up at **six**, **Lola**.  
   key: has to get  
   why: My dad is he, so have to becomes has to.

7. _Rewrite the sentence about the new person._  
   We have to wear **a uniform** at **school**.  
   → Olga ______ (wear) **a uniform** at **school**.  
   key: has to wear  
   why: Olga is she, so have to becomes has to.

8. _Rewrite the sentence about the new person._  
   I have to study for the **interview** **this week**.  
   → Aziz ______ (study) for the **interview** **this week**.  
   key: has to study  
   why: Aziz is he, so use has to.

9. _Rewrite the sentence about the new person._  
   They have to help their parents in the **shop** **in summer**.  
   → Bobur ______ (help) his parents in the **shop** **in summer**.  
   key: has to help  
   why: Bobur is he, so use has to.

10. _Rewrite the sentence about the new person._  
   You have to pay for the **room** **every month**.  
   → She ______ (pay) for the **room** **every month**.  
   key: has to pay  
   why: With she, use has to.

11. _Make a question with do or does + have to._  
   Aziz has to work on **Friday** **again**.  
   → ______ Aziz ______ (work) on **Friday** **again**?  
   key: Does + have to work  
   why: Question: Does + he + have to + verb. After does, has becomes have.

12. _Make a question with do or does + have to._  
   You have to bring a **dictionary** **every day**.  
   → ______ you ______ (bring) a **dictionary** **every day**?  
   key: Do + have to bring  
   why: Question: Do + you + have to + verb.

13. _Make a question with do or does + have to._  
   Lola has to wear a **suit** at **work**.  
   → ______ Lola ______ (wear) a **suit** at **work**?  
   key: Does + have to wear  
   why: Question: Does + she + have to + verb (have, not has).

14. _Make a question with do or does + have to._  
   We have to leave at **six** **tomorrow**.  
   → ______ we ______ (leave) at **six** **tomorrow**?  
   key: Do + have to leave  
   why: Question: Do + we + have to + verb.

15. _Make a question with do or does + have to._

## create (8)

1. Write one sentence about something you have to do every morning.  
   must use: I have to + base verb · exactly one sentence · must use have to · must include every morning

2. Write one sentence about something a family member has to do at work.  
   must use: has to + base verb · exactly one sentence · must name the person · must use has to

3. Write one sentence about a rule at your school or university.  
   must use: have to + base verb · exactly one sentence · must use have to · must mention school or university

4. Ask a friend a question about their job or studies with have to.  
   must use: Do you have to … ? · exactly one question · must start with Do you have to · must end with a question mark

5. Write one sentence about something you don't have to do at the weekend.  
   must use: don't have to + base verb · exactly one sentence · must use don't have to · must include at the weekend

6. Write one sentence about what tourists have to show at the airport.  
   must use: have to show · exactly one sentence · must use have to · must name a document

7. Write one sentence about something a pet has to do or have every day.  
   must use: has to + base verb · exactly one sentence · must name the animal · must use has to

8. Write two sentences: one thing you have to do this week and one thing your friend has to do.  
   must use: have to / has to · exactly two sentences · have to in one, has to in the other · base verb in both

## express (5)

1. Describe a normal school or work day. What do you have to do? Write three or four sentences.  
   target: have to for duties · look for: I have to + base verb · time words · three different verbs

2. What does a doctor, a teacher or a driver have to do at work? Choose one job and write three or four sentences.  
   target: has to for a job · look for: he / she has to + base verb · don't forget to · no must to

3. Write about the rules in your home. What do you and your brothers or sisters have to do?  
   target: have to / has to · look for: I have to · my brother / sister has to · don't have to for one thing

4. A friend is coming to study at your school. Write what students have to do there.  
   target: have to for rules · look for: students have to · you have to · a question with Do you have to

5. Interview a friend about their week: write four questions with have to and their answers.  
   target: Do you have to … ? questions and answers · look for: Do you have to … ? · Yes, I do / No, I don't · has to in one answer
