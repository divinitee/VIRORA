# tenses.perfect.perfect-vs-past — review sample (B1)

Each item shows one random filler combination (in bold). Mark anything a teacher wouldn't accept.

## choose (20)

1. I ______ that film **last week**, **Zarina**. It was great.  
   options: ✔ saw · have seen · see · am seeing  
   why: A finished time word at the end: past simple saw.

2. We ______ to **Samarkand** in **2019**.  
   options: have gone · go · am going · ✔ went  
   why: "in" + a year is finished time: past simple went.

   options: buys · is buying · ✔ bought · has bought  
   why: "three days ago" is finished time: past simple bought.

   options: am meeting · ✔ met · have met · meet  
   why: A finished time word: past simple met.

   options: ✔ lost · has lost · loses · is losing  
   why: A finished time word: past simple lost.

6. Your **teacher** ______ you an hour ago, **Sherzod**.  
   options: has called · calls · is calling · ✔ called  
   why: "an hour ago" is finished time: past simple called.

7. I ______ to swim when I was **eight**, **Nodira**.  
   options: learn · am learning · ✔ learned · have learned  
   why: "when I was …" is finished time: past simple learned.

   why: "last season" is finished time: past simple won.

   I have seen that film **last week**, **Nodira**.  
   → I ______ that film **last week**, **Nodira**.  
   key: saw  
   why: A finished time word cannot go with have seen: saw.

   We have visited **Bukhara** in **2022**.

   why: "in" + a year needs the past simple: visited.

   **Zarina** has bought a new **bag** two days ago.
