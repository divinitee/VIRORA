# prep-phrasal.movement.across-through-along — review sample (B1)

Each item shows one random filler combination (in bold). Mark anything a teacher wouldn't accept.

## choose (20)

1. The train went ______ a long dark tunnel under the **hill** and came out into the sunlight, **Zarina**.  
   options: across · ✔ through · along · between  
   why: In one end of the tunnel and out the other: through.

2. We pushed our way ______ the crowd to the **stage**, **Nodira**.  
   options: ✔ through · along · between · across  
   why: People all around us: through the crowd.

3. The **bird** came in ______ the open window, **Zarina**.  
   options: along · between · across · ✔ through  
   why: In one side of the window and out the other: through.

4. Go ______ the gate and turn left to find the **car park**, **Otabek**.  
   options: between · across · ✔ through · along  
   why: You pass the gate from one side to the other side of the wall: through.

5. We walked ______ the forest for an hour. There were **tall trees** all around us, **Jamshid**.  
   options: across · ✔ through · along · between  
   why: … all around you: through the forest.

6. The little boy looked ______ the keyhole into the **classroom**, **Otabek**.  
   options: ✔ through · along · between · across  
   why: Your eye goes into the hole and out the other side: through.

7. The ball flew ______ the open **gate** and into the house, **Malika**.  
   options: along · between · across · ✔ through  
   why: In one side of the … and out the other: through.

8. Look both ways before you walk ______ the **street**, **Nodira**.  
   options: between · ✔ across · through · along  
   why: "Look both ways" = you go to the other side: across.

9. My cousin swam ______ the **canal** to the other side, **Malika**.  
   options: ✔ across · through · along · between  
   why: "to the other side": across.

10. We took a boat ______ the **sea** to the little island, **Sardor**.  
   options: through · along · between · ✔ across  
   why: From this side to the island on the other side: across.

11. The ball rolled ______ the street and into the **garden** on the other side, **Malika**.  
   options: along · between · ✔ across · through  
   why: "on the other side": across.

12. He jumped ______ the stream without getting his **trousers** wet, **Otabek**.  
   options: between · ✔ across · through · along  
   why: Over to the other bank in one jump: across.

13. My friend lives just ______ the road from me, so I can see her **window**, **Jamshid**.  
   options: ✔ across · through · along · between  
   why: On the other side of the road: across.

14. It took three days to sail ______ the **big lake** from one coast to the other, **Nodira**.  
   options: through · along · between · ✔ across  
   why: "from one coast to the other": across.

15. We walked ______ the beach for two hours, looking at the **boats**, **Zarina**.  
   options: ✔ along · between · across · through  
   why: For two hours beside the … = following the beach: along.

16. There are cafés all ______ **Navoi Street**, from one end to the other, **Zarina**.  
   options: between · across · through · ✔ along  
   why: Following the whole length of the street: along.

17. Drive ______ this road for two kilometres, then turn left at the **petrol station**, **Ulugbek**.  
   options: across · through · ✔ along · between  
   why: "for two kilometres" = following the road: along.

18. We cycled ______ the river bank to the next **town**, **Nodira**.  
   options: through · ✔ along · between · across  
   why: Following the line of the river bank: along.

19. Tall trees grow ______ both sides of the **street**, **Jamshid**.  
   options: ✔ along · between · across · through  
   why: In a line beside the …: along.

20. We walked slowly ______ the river and watched the **boats** go by, **Otabek**.  
   options: between · across · through · ✔ along  
   why: Following the river and watching it: along.

## transform (15)

1. _Rewrite with across._  
   We swam from one side of the **canal** to the other, **Malika**.  
   → We swam ______ the **canal**, **Malika**.  
   key: across  
   why: from one side to the other = across.

2. _Rewrite with across._  
   The dog ran from one side of the road to the other, **Sardor**. A **bus** had to stop.  
   → The dog ran ______ the road, **Sardor**. A **bus** had to stop.  
   key: across  
   why: from one side to the other = across.

3. _Rewrite with across._  
   We sailed from one side of the lake to the other in an hour, **Jamshid**. The **wind** was perfect.

   key: across  
   why: from one side to the other = across.

## create (8)

1. Write one sentence about crossing a road or a river. Use across.  
   must use: … across the road / river · exactly one sentence · across · to the other side

2. Write one sentence about going through a tunnel, a forest or a crowd.  
   must use: … through the tunnel · exactly one sentence · through · a space around you

3. Write one sentence about walking beside a river or a beach. Use along.  
   must use: … along the river · exactly one sentence · along · a long line

4. Write directions to your home with along and across.  
   must use: Go along … then across … · exactly one sentence · along · across

5. Write one sentence about light or sound coming through something.  
   must use: … through the window / wall · exactly one sentence · through · light or sound

6. Write one sentence about a place on the other side of the street from your home.  
   must use: … across the street from … · exactly one sentence · across the street · a place

7. Write one sentence about things in a line beside a road or river.  
   must use: … along the road · exactly one sentence · along · a line of things

8. Write one sentence about a journey through mountains or a desert.  
   must use: … through the mountains · exactly one sentence · through · past

## express (5)

1. Give directions from your home to a shop or school. Write three or four sentences with across, through and along.  
   target: across / through / along · look for: across · along · three or four sentences

2. Describe a walk you enjoyed. Where did you go? Write three or four sentences.  
   target: along the river / through the forest · look for: along · through · three or four sentences

3. Describe a long car or train journey. Write three or four sentences.  
   target: through tunnels / across rivers / along the coast · look for: through · across · three or four sentences

4. Describe your street or a street you like. Write three or four sentences.  
   target: along the street / across the street from · look for: along · across · three or four sentences

5. Write three or four sentences of a story about an animal that got into a house.  
   target: through the window / across the room · look for: through · across · three or four sentences
