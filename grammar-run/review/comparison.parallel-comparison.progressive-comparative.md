## transform (15)

1. _Rewrite with … and … (the comparative twice)._  
   It gets a bit hotter every day in **August**, **Dilnoza**.  
   → It gets ______ in **August**, **Dilnoza**.  
   key: hotter and hotter  
   why: "a bit hotter every day" = hotter and hotter.

2. _Rewrite with … and … (the comparative twice)._  
   My **grandmother** gets a little weaker every year, **Sardor**.  
   → My **grandmother** is getting ______, **Sardor**.  
   key: weaker and weaker  
   why: "a little weaker every year" = weaker and weaker.

3. _Rewrite with … and … (the comparative twice)._  
   The **test** questions got a bit harder each time, **Sardor**.  
   → The **test** questions got ______, **Sardor**.  
   key: harder and harder  
   why: "a bit harder each time" = harder and harder.

4. _Rewrite with … and … (the comparative twice)._  
   The **Bukhara** roads get a little busier every year, **Feruza**.  
   → The **Bukhara** roads are getting ______, **Feruza**.  
   key: busier and busier (also: more and more busy)  
   why: "a little busier every year" = busier and busier.

5. _Rewrite with … and … (the comparative twice)._  
   **Flats** get a bit more expensive every year, **Otabek**.  
   → **Flats** are getting ______, **Otabek**.  
   key: more and more expensive  
   why: "a bit more expensive every year" = more and more expensive.

6. _Correct the mistake._  
   It's getting more and more cold in **Moscow**, **Sardor**.  
   → It's getting ______ in **Moscow**, **Sardor**.  
   key: colder and colder  
   why: cold is short: colder and colder, not more and more cold.

7. _Correct the mistake._  
   The music got loud and loud at the **concert**, **Sardor**.  
   → The music got ______ at the **concert**, **Sardor**.  
   key: louder and louder  
   why: Repeat the -er form: louder and louder.

8. _Correct the mistake._  
   I felt more nervous and nervous before the **exam**, **Otabek**.  
   → I felt ______ before the **exam**, **Otabek**.  
   key: more and more nervous  
   why: Repeat more, not the adjective: more and more nervous.

9. _Correct the mistake._  
   My little **kitten** is getting more bigger and bigger, **Otabek**.  
   → My little **kitten** is getting ______, **Otabek**.  
   key: bigger and bigger

   → This **game** is becoming ______, **Sardor**.  
   key: more and more popular
