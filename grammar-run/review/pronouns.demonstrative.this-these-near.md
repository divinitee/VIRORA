## transform (15)

1. _Make it plural (more than one)._  
   This book is **funny**. I read it **on the bus**.  
   → ______ books are **funny**. I read them **on the bus**.  
   key: These  
   why: Books is more than one, so this changes to these.

2. _Make it plural (more than one)._  
   This cup on my **desk** is **full**.  
   → ______ cups on my **desk** are **full**.  
   key: These  
   why: Cups is more than one, so this changes to these.

3. _Make it plural (more than one)._  
   This apple in my hand is **sour**. I got it from **Grandma**.  
   → ______ apples in my hand are **sour**. I got them from **Grandma**.  
   key: These  
   why: Apples is more than one, so this changes to these.

## create (8)

1. You are holding your phone. Write one sentence about it with This.  
   must use: This + is · exactly one sentence · must start with This · must say something about the phone

2. You are wearing your shoes. Write one sentence about them with These.  
   must use: These + are · exactly one sentence · must start with These · are, not is

3. Show a friend a photo of your family. Write one sentence about one person in it.  
   must use: This is my … · exactly one sentence · must use This is · a family word

4. Write one sentence about the food on your plate at dinner.  
   must use: this + noun · exactly one sentence · must use this · must name the food

5. You have some books on your desk. Write one sentence about them.  
   must use: these + plural noun · exactly one sentence · must use these · a plural noun after these

6. A friend visits your room. Write one sentence to show them something in it.  
   must use: This is / These are · exactly one sentence · must use this or these · must name the thing

7. Write one sentence about the room you are in now.  
   must use: This room … · exactly one sentence · must use this · is, not are

8. Write two sentences about things in your bag: one with this, one with these.  
   must use: this + singular / these + plural · exactly two sentences · one this, one these · is after this, are after these

## express (5)

1. You are showing a new friend around your room. Write what you say about four things in it.  
   target: this / these for near things · look for: This is … · These are … · is after this, are after these

2. You are looking at photos of your family on your phone. Describe three of them.  
   target: This is … / These are … · look for: This is my … · These are my … · family words

3. You are at the market with a friend, holding some fruit. Write a short conversation about it.  
   target: this / these + noun · look for: these + plural fruit · this + one thing · a question with this or these

4. Describe the things on your desk or table right now.  
   target: this / these · look for: this + singular noun · these + plural noun · near words: here, on my desk

5. Write a short message to a pen friend with a photo of your school. What is in the photo?  
   target: This is / These are · look for: This is our school · These are my friends / classmates · three or four sentences
