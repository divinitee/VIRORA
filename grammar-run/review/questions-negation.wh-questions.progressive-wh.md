   must use: Who is … -ing · exactly one question · must start with Who · must use -ing

   must use: Why are … -ing · exactly one question · must start with Why · must use -ing

   must use: What is … -ing · exactly one question · must start with What · must use -ing

## express (5)

1. You call a friend. Write four questions about what they are doing now.  
   target: wh + am/is/are + -ing · look for: What are you doing · Where · -ing

2. You hear a noise in the next room. Write questions to ask your family.  
   target: wh + am/is/are + -ing · look for: What is · Who is · Why

3. Write a short phone dialogue with your mum. She is at the market.  
   target: wh + am/is/are + -ing · look for: What are you buying · Where · now

4. Look at a busy street. Write three or four questions about the people.  
   target: wh + am/is/are + -ing · look for: Where is · What are · -ing

5. Your friend is at a wedding. Write questions about it.  
   target: wh + am/is/are + -ing · look for: What are they · Who is · wearing
