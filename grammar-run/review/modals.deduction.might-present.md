## transform (15)

   Perhaps she is busy at the **bank**, **Feruza**.

   key: might be  
   why: Perhaps she is → she might be.

2. _Rewrite with might (or might not)._  
   Maybe he knows the way to the **museum**, **Otabek**.  
   → He ______ the way to the **museum**, **Otabek**.  
   key: might know  
   why: Maybe he knows → he might know.

3. _Rewrite with might (or might not)._  
   Perhaps the **bakery** is closed, **Otabek**.  
   → The **bakery** ______ closed, **Otabek**.  
   key: might be  
   why: Perhaps it is → it might be.
