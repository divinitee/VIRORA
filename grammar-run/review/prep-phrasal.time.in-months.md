## create (8)

1. Write one sentence about the month of your birthday.  
   must use: My birthday is in … · exactly one sentence · must use in + a month · about you

2. Write one sentence about a family member's birthday.  
   must use: in + month · exactly one sentence · must use in + a month · a family word

3. Write one sentence about a trip you want to take and the month.  
   must use: in + month · exactly one sentence · must use in + a month · a place

4. Write one question to a friend about the month of their birthday.  
   must use: Is your birthday in …? · exactly one question · must use in + a month · must end with a question mark

5. Write one sentence about what your family does in March for Navruz.  
   must use: in March · exactly one sentence · must use in March · about your family

6. Write one sentence about the month you like best and why.  
   must use: in + month · exactly one sentence · must use in + a month · must say why

7. Write one sentence about a test, exam or event at your school and its month.  
   must use: in + month · exactly one sentence · must use in + a month · about school or work

8. Write two sentences about two different months: what you do in each.  
   must use: in + month (×2) · exactly two sentences · in in both sentences · two different months
