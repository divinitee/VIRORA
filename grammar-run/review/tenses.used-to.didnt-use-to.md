## transform (15)

1. _Make the sentence negative with didn't use to._  
   When I was **seven**, I used to like **cheese**.  
   → When I was **seven**, I ______ **cheese**.  
   key: didn't use to like  
   why: Make the old habit negative: used to like → didn't use to like.

2. _Make the sentence negative with didn't use to._  
   My **grandparents** used to go out on **weekdays**.  
   → My **grandparents** ______ out on **weekdays**.  
   key: didn't use to go  
   why: Negative of used to go: didn't use to go (no -d after did).

3. _Make the sentence negative with didn't use to._  
   We used to have a **big fridge** in our **kitchen**.  
   → We ______ a **big fridge** in our **kitchen**.  
   key: didn't use to have  
   why: Negative of used to have: didn't use to have.

4. _Make the sentence negative with didn't use to._  
   **Olga** used to eat **fast food**.  
   → **Olga** ______ **fast food**.  
   key: didn't use to eat  
   why: Negative of used to eat: didn't use to eat.

5. _Make the sentence negative with didn't use to._  
   My **father** used to work on **Saturdays**.  
   → My **father** ______ on **Saturdays**.  
   key: didn't use to work  
   why: Negative of used to work: didn't use to work.

6. _This sentence has a mistake. Rewrite it correctly with didn't use to._  
   I didn't used to like **tea**, but now I **do a lot**.  
   → I ______ **tea**, but now I **do a lot**.  
   key: didn't use to like  
   why: After did, write use with no -d: didn't use to like.

7. _This sentence has a mistake. Rewrite it correctly with didn't use to._  
   **Nilufar** wasn't used to drink **green tea**, but now she has it every **morning**.  
   → **Nilufar** ______ **green tea**, but now she has it every **morning**.  
   key: didn't use to drink  
   why: Not "wasn't used to drink": a past habit is didn't use to drink.

8. _This sentence has a mistake. Rewrite it correctly with didn't use to._  
   We don't use to visit the **museum** as children, but now we go every **summer**.  
   → We ______ the **museum** as children, but now we go every **summer**.  
   key: didn't use to visit  
   why: "as children" is the past, so did, not do: didn't use to visit.

9. _This sentence has a mistake. Rewrite it correctly with didn't use to._  
   My **brother** didn't use cook at home, but now he cooks every **weekend**.  
   → My **brother** ______ at home, but now he cooks every **weekend**.  
   key: didn't use to cook  
   why: to is missing: didn't use to cook.

10. _This sentence has a mistake. Rewrite it correctly with didn't use to._  
   They didn't use to living in **Tashkent**, but they moved there last **year**.  
   → They ______ in **Tashkent**, but they moved there last **year**.  
   key: didn't use to live  
   why: After use to comes the base verb, not -ing: didn't use to live.

## express (5)

1. How are you different from five years ago? Write three or four sentences with things you didn't use to do.  
   target: didn't use to for changes · look for: didn't use to + base form · at least two verbs · now for the present

2. Describe how your street or town has changed. What didn't there use to be? Write three or four sentences.  
   target: didn't use to / there didn't use to be · look for: didn't use to · a place named · now for the present

3. Describe a family member who has changed a lot. Write three or four sentences.  
   target: he / she didn't use to · look for: didn't use to · used to or now · no -d after did

4. Write about a food, sport or hobby you didn't use to like. Why do you like it now? Write three or four sentences.  
   target: didn't use to like · look for: didn't use to like or enjoy · a reason · present simple for now

5. Describe your first year at school or college compared with now. Write three or four sentences.  
   target: used to and didn't use to · look for: didn't use to · used to · school words
