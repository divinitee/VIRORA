## transform (15)

1. _Ask a How often question about this. Write one word in each gap._  
   Zarina goes to the **library** twice a week, **Timur**.  
   → How often ______ Zarina ______ to the **library**, **Timur**?  
   key: does + go  
   why: He or she takes does, then the base verb: goes becomes does … go. The frequency is the answer, so it leaves the question.
