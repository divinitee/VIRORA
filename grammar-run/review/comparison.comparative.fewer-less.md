# comparison.comparative.fewer-less — review sample (B1)

Each item shows one random filler combination (in bold). Mark anything a teacher wouldn't accept.

## choose (20)

1. There were ______ people at the **festival** than last year, **Malika**.  
   options: ✔ fewer · less · little · lesser  
   why: people is plural and countable: fewer people.

2. I spend ______ money on **clothes** than my **sister**.  
   options: fewer · little · lesser · ✔ less  
   why: money is uncountable: less money.

3. Our **yard** has ______ trees than the park near the **bazaar**.  
   options: little · lesser · ✔ fewer · less  
   why: trees can be counted: fewer trees.

4. This **fridge** uses ______ electricity than our old one, **Ulugbek**.  
   options: lesser · ✔ less · fewer · little  
   why: electricity can't be counted: less electricity.

5. My brother made ______ mistakes in the **dictation** than me, **Jamshid**.  
   options: ✔ fewer · less · little · lesser  
   why: mistakes can be counted: fewer mistakes.

6. We have ______ homework this **month** than last **month**, **Ulugbek**.  
   options: fewer · little · lesser · ✔ less  
   why: homework is uncountable: less homework.

7. There are ______ cars on the road on **Saturdays** than on weekdays, **Gulnora**.  
   options: little · lesser · ✔ fewer · less  
   why: cars can be counted: fewer cars.

8. My **father** drinks ______ **coffee** than he did last year.  
   options: lesser · ✔ less · fewer · little  
   why: The drink is uncountable here: less.

9. **Sevara** has ______ friends in **Tashkent** than in her old town.  
   options: ✔ fewer · less · little · lesser  
   why: friends can be counted: fewer friends.

10. It takes ______ time by **car** than by bus, **Jamshid**.  
   options: fewer · little · lesser · ✔ less  
   why: time is uncountable: less time.

   why: problems can be counted: fewer problems.

12. There was ______ **sunshine** this **autumn** than last **autumn**.  
   options: lesser · ✔ less · fewer · little

13. We sold ______ **melons** at the bazaar today than yesterday, **Jamshid**.  
   options: ✔ fewer · less · little · lesser  
   why: The goods are plural, so: fewer.

14. You should eat ______ sugar and more **salad**, **Gulnora**.  
   options: fewer · little · lesser · ✔ less  
   why: sugar is uncountable: less sugar.

15. There are ______ students in our **class** than in yours, **Malika**.  
   options: little · lesser · ✔ fewer · less  
   why: students can be counted: fewer students.

16. My new job gives me ______ free time than my old **course**, **Jamshid**.  
   options: lesser · ✔ less · fewer · little  
   why: free time is uncountable: less free time.

17. Our team scored ______ goals this **season** than last **season**, **Ulugbek**.  
   options: ✔ fewer · less · little · lesser  
   why: goals can be counted: fewer goals.

18. The **new district** is quieter, **Gulnora**: there is ______ noise than in the centre.  
   options: fewer · little · lesser · ✔ less  
   why: noise is uncountable: less noise.

19. I got ______ messages **this month than last month**, **Jamshid**.  
   options: little · lesser · ✔ fewer · less

## transform (15)

1. _Compare the two numbers. Use fewer or less … than._  
   Last year our **club** had 30 students. This year it has **25**.  
   → This year our **club** has ______ students than last year.  
   key: fewer  
   why: 30 students to fewer: students are countable.

2. _Compare the two numbers. Use fewer or less … than._  
   I slept eight hours on Monday and **five** hours on Tuesday, **Jamshid**.  
   → I slept for ______ on Tuesday than on Monday, **Jamshid**.  
   key: fewer hours  
   why: Hours can be counted: fewer hours.

3. _Compare the two numbers. Use fewer or less … than._  
   The old phone cost 3 million soum. The new one costs **2** million, **Jamshid**.  
   → The new phone costs ______ money than the old one, **Jamshid**.  
   key: less  
   why: money can't be counted: less.

4. _Compare the two numbers. Use fewer or less … than._  
   We had 40 guests at my sister's wedding and **35** at mine, **Malika**.  
   → We had ______ guests at my wedding than at my sister's, **Malika**.  
   key: fewer  
   why: guests can be counted: fewer.

5. _Compare the two numbers. Use fewer or less … than._  
   Our old car used 10 litres of petrol a day. The new one uses **7**.  
   → The new car uses ______ petrol than the old one, **Ulugbek**.  
   key: less  
   why: petrol can't be counted: less.

6. _Correct the mistake._  
   I have less friends in **Tashkent** than in my village, **Jamshid**.  
   → I have ______ friends in **Tashkent** than in my village, **Jamshid**.  
   key: fewer  
   why: friends are countable, so not less: fewer.

7. _Correct the mistake._  
   There is fewer traffic on **Friday** than on Monday, **Jamshid**.  
   → There is ______ traffic on **Friday** than on Monday, **Jamshid**.  
   key: less  
   why: traffic is uncountable, so not fewer: less.

8. _Correct the mistake._  
   We bought lesser **apples** this week than last week, **Ulugbek**.  
   → We bought ______ **apples** this week than last week, **Ulugbek**.  
   key: fewer  
   why: Not lesser: goods are countable: fewer.

9. _Correct the mistake._  
   My **mother** drinks fewer coffee than before, **Ulugbek**.  
   → My **mother** drinks ______ coffee than before, **Ulugbek**.  
   key: less  
   why: coffee is uncountable, so not fewer: less.

10. _Correct the mistake._

   target: fewer / less … than · look for: fewer · less · two places

   target: fewer problems / less money · look for: fewer · less · than
