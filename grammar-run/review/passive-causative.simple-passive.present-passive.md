## transform (15)

1. _Correct the mistake._  
   This **phone** is make in **Korea**, **Timur**.  
   → This **phone** ______ in **Korea**, **Timur**.  
   key: is made  
   why: After is, use the past participle: made.

2. _Correct the mistake._  
   **French** are spoken in many countries, **Olga**.  
   → **French** ______ in many countries, **Olga**.  
   key: is spoken  
   why: One language → is spoken.

3. _Correct the mistake._  
   These **spices** are sell at the bazaar, **Aziz**.  
   → These **spices** ______ at the bazaar, **Aziz**.  
   key: are sold  
   why: After are, use the past participle: sold.

4. _Correct the mistake._  
   The rooms is cleaned every **day**, **Timur**.  
   → The rooms ______ every **day**, **Timur**.  
   key: are cleaned  
   why: More than one room → are cleaned.

5. _Correct the mistake._  
   **Chinese** teaches at our school, **Aziz**.  
   → **Chinese** ______ at our school, **Aziz**.  
   key: is taught  
   why: A subject doesn't teach; it is taught.
