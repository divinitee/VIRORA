## transform (15)

1. _Rewrite the sentence so it says the same thing from the other side. Use the comparative form of the word in brackets._  
   In the **photo**, my brother is shorter than my **father**.  
   → In the **photo**, my **father** is ______ (tall) than my brother.  
   key: taller  
   why: Short and tall are opposites. Tall is a short adjective, so it becomes taller.

2. _Rewrite the sentence so it says the same thing from the other side. Use the comparative form of the word in brackets._  
   The neighbours' **cat** is older than our **cat**.  
   → Our **cat** is ______ (young) than the neighbours' **cat**.  
   key: younger  
   why: Old and young are opposites. Young is a short adjective, so it becomes younger.

3. _Rewrite the sentence so it says the same thing from the other side. Use the comparative form of the word in brackets._  
   The bus to **Bukhara** is slower than the **express train**.

   key: faster  
   why: Slow and fast are opposites. Fast is a short adjective, so it becomes faster, not more fast.
