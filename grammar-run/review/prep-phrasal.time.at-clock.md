## create (8)

1. Write one sentence about the time you get up on a school day or a work day.  
   must use: at + clock time · exactly one sentence · must use at + a clock time · about you

2. Write one sentence about the time your first lesson starts.  
   must use: at + clock time · exactly one sentence · must use at · a clock time like 8:30 or nine o'clock

3. Write one sentence about the time your family has dinner.  
   must use: at + clock time · exactly one sentence · must use at · about your family

4. Invite a friend to meet you. Say the place and the clock time.  
   must use: Let's meet at … · exactly one sentence · must use at + a clock time · a place in your town

5. Write one question to a friend about the time of something.  
   must use: What time …? · exactly one question · must start with What time · must end with a question mark

6. Answer this question about yourself: What time do you go to bed?  
   must use: I go to bed at … · exactly one sentence · must use at · a clock time

7. Write one sentence about a shop or market near your home and the time it opens or closes.  
   must use: opens / closes at … · exactly one sentence · must use at + a clock time · opens or closes

8. Write two sentences about your day: one thing in the morning and one in the evening, each with a clock time.  
   must use: at + clock time (×2) · exactly two sentences · at in both sentences · two different clock times

## express (5)

1. Describe a normal school day or work day for you, from morning to evening. Give the times.  
   target: at + clock times · look for: I get up at … · at + four or five clock times · starts / finishes / goes

2. A new student asks about your school timetable. Tell them when lessons start, have breaks and finish.  
   target: at + clock times · look for: starts at … · finishes at … · at + half past / o'clock

3. Write a message to a friend. Plan a trip to the park or the bazaar on Saturday and say what time you will meet.  
   target: at + clock time · look for: Let's meet at … · a place · a clock time

4. Tell a friend about your family's evening: when people come home, eat and go to bed.  
   target: at + clock times · look for: comes home at … · have dinner at … · goes to bed at …

5. Write about your weekend day. What time do you get up, eat and go out?  
   target: at + clock times · look for: at + clock times · three or four sentences · about you
