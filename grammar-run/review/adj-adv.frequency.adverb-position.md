## express (5)

1. Describe a normal school or work day for you, from morning to evening. Use always, usually, often, sometimes and never. Write three or four sentences.  
   target: frequency adverbs before the main verb · look for: adverb before the main verb · no adverb between the verb and its object · at least three different adverbs

2. Describe your weekends. What do you usually do, and what do you never do? Write three or four sentences.  
   target: frequency adverbs in habits · look for: usually and never before the main verb · no be before a main verb · at weekends or at the weekend

3. Describe how you feel at different times of the week. Are you often tired? Are you always happy on Fridays? Write three or four sentences.  
   target: frequency adverbs after be · look for: am, is or are before the adverb · an adjective after the adverb · no missing be

4. Describe someone in your family and their habits. Write three or four sentences.  
   target: frequency adverbs with be and with main verbs · look for: adverb after be · adverb before the main verb · he or she with the -s form

5. Describe what you eat and drink in a normal week. Write three or four sentences.  
   target: frequency adverbs before main verbs about food · look for: often, sometimes or never before eat or drink · no adverb after the verb · food and drink named
