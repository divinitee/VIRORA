## transform (15)

1. _Correct the mistake._  
   I went to home at **half past six**, **Aziz**.  
   → I ______ at **half past six**, **Aziz**.  
   key: went home  
   why: No to before home: went home.

2. _Correct the mistake._  
   **Olga** comes at home late on **Mondays**.  
   → **Olga** ______ late on **Mondays**.  
   key: comes home  
   why: After come, no at before home: comes home.

3. _Correct the mistake._  
   We got to home at midnight after the **wedding**, **Olga**.  
   → We ______ at midnight after the **wedding**, **Olga**.  
   key: got home  
   why: No to before home: got home.

4. _Correct the mistake._  
   Let's go to the home now, **Timur**. It's **cold**.  
   → Let's ______ now, **Timur**. It's **cold**.  
   key: go home  
   why: No to the before home: go home.

5. _Correct the mistake._  
   **Olga** walked in home in the **cold**.  
   → **Olga** ______ in the **cold**.  
   key: walked home  
   why: No in before home: walked home.

6. _Rewrite the sentence with home._  
   I went to my house after **school**, **Olga**.  
   → I ______ after **school**, **Olga**.  
   key: went home  
   why: Your own house or flat = home: went home.

7. _Rewrite the sentence with home._
