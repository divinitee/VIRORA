## express (5)

1. Describe tomorrow: what will you be doing at 8 a.m., at noon and at 8 p.m.? Write three or four sentences.  
   target: future continuous at clock times · look for: will be + -ing · a time in each sentence · no will + base for actions in progress

2. Imagine your perfect holiday next summer. Describe what you will be doing at this time on a normal day. Write three or four sentences.  
   target: future continuous for a scene · look for: will be + -ing · a place named · at least three actions

3. A friend wants to visit you on Saturday. Explain when you can't meet and what you will be doing. Write three or four sentences.  
   target: future continuous for unavailability · look for: will be + -ing · clock times or parts of the day · a time when you are free

4. Describe your life ten years from now. Where will you be living, and what will you be doing? Write three or four sentences.  
   target: future continuous for the long term · look for: will be living / working · this time in ten years · no will be + base

5. Describe what different people in your family will be doing at seven o'clock tomorrow evening. Write three or four sentences.  
   target: future continuous with different subjects · look for: will be + -ing · at least three people · at seven o'clock
