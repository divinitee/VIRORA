## transform (15)

1. _Correct the mistake._  
   **Timur** has a **piano lesson** in Friday.  
   → **Timur** has a **piano lesson** ______ Friday.  
   key: on  
   why: Friday is a day, so use on, not in.

2. _Correct the mistake._  
   The **match** starts on eight o'clock, **Dad**.  
   → The **match** starts ______ eight o'clock, **Dad**.  
   key: at  
   why: Eight o'clock is a clock time, so use at, not on.

3. _Correct the mistake._  
   My **uncle** goes to **Kazan** at June.  
   → My **uncle** goes to **Kazan** ______ June.  
   key: in  
   why: June is a month, so use in, not at.

4. _Correct the mistake._  
   We drink **black tea** on the evening, **Timur**.  
   → We drink **black tea** ______ the evening, **Timur**.  
   key: in  
   why: The evening is a part of the day, so use in, not on.

5. _Correct the mistake._  
   **Aunt Lola**'s birthday party is in 20 August, **Olga**.  
   → **Aunt Lola**'s birthday party is ______ 20 August, **Olga**.  
   key: on  
   why: 20 August is a date, so use on, not in.

6. _Answer the question. The time is: 7:30._  
   **Aziz**, what time does the **train** leave?  
   → It leaves ______ 7:30.  
   key: at  
   why: 7:30 is a clock time, so the answer uses at.

7. _Answer the question. The time is: Thursday._  
   When is your **history test**, **Olga**?  
   → It is ______ Thursday.  
   key: on  
   why: Thursday is a day, so the answer uses on.

8. _Answer the question. The time is: 2015._  
   When did your family move to **Tashkent**, **Lola**?  
   → We moved there ______ 2015.  
   key: in  
   why: 2015 is a year, so the answer uses in.

9. _Answer the question. The time is: the afternoon._  
   When do you play **tennis**, **Lola**?  
   → I play it ______ the afternoon.  
   key: in  
   why: The afternoon is a part of the day, so the answer uses in.

10. _Answer the question. The time is: midnight._  
   When does **Mr Karimov** come home from **the bakery**?  
   → He comes home ______ midnight.  
   key: at  
   why: Midnight takes at, like a clock time.

11. _Change tomorrow to Saturday. Rewrite the sentence._  
   We are going to the **zoo** tomorrow, **Olga**.

   → **Madina** is calling **Uncle Bobur** ______ ten o'clock.  
   key: at

13. _Change this month to August. Rewrite the sentence._

   → My **aunt** is visiting us ______ August, **Aziz**.  
   key: in  
   why: August is a month, so it takes in. This month has no preposition.

14. _Change today to 4 October. Rewrite the sentence._  
   **My uncle** is flying to **Almaty** today.  
   → **My uncle** is flying to **Almaty** ______ 4 October.  
   key: on  
   why: 4 October is a date, so it takes on. Today has no preposition.

15. _Change after dinner to half past nine. Rewrite the sentence._  
   **Dad** reads to **my little brother** after dinner.  
   → **Dad** reads to **my little brother** ______ half past nine.  
   key: at  
   why: Half past nine is a clock time, so it takes at.

## create (8)

1. Write one sentence about when you get up on a school day. Use a clock time.  
   must use: at + clock time · exactly one sentence · must use at + a time · about you

2. Write one sentence about your favourite day of the week and what you do then.  
   must use: on + day · exactly one sentence · must use on + a day · about you

3. Write one sentence about the month of your birthday.  
   must use: in + month · exactly one sentence · must use in + a month · about you

4. Write one sentence about what your family does in the evening.  
   must use: in the evening · exactly one sentence · must use in the evening · a family word

5. Write one sentence about one of your lessons. Say the day and the time.  
   must use: on + day … at + time · exactly one sentence · on + a day · at + a clock time

6. Write one sentence about the year you were born or the year you started school.  
   must use: in + year · exactly one sentence · must use in + a year · about you

7. Write one question to a friend about their weekend plans. Use a day.  
   must use: on + day · exactly one question · on + a day · must end with a question mark

8. Write two sentences about your day: one with in the morning, one with at night.  
   must use: in the morning / at night · exactly two sentences · in the morning in one · at night in the other

## express (5)

1. Describe a normal school day: when you get up, when your lessons start and what you do in the evening.  
   target: at / in · look for: at + clock time · in the morning / in the evening · three or four sentences

2. Tell a new friend about your week: what you do on different days.  
   target: on + days · look for: on Monday … · on Saturdays … · three or four sentences

3. Write about your year: what your family does in different months or seasons.  
   target: in + months / seasons · look for: in + a month · in summer / in winter · three or four sentences

4. Invite a friend to your birthday party. Say the date, the day and the time.  
   target: on + date / at + time · look for: on + date or day · at + clock time · a friendly invitation

5. Tell a visitor about a special day in your family: when it is and what you do.  
   target: in / on / at · look for: at least two of in, on, at · when it is · what you do
