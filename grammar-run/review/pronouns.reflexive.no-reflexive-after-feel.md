# pronouns.reflexive.no-reflexive-after-feel — review sample (B1)

Each item shows one random filler combination (in bold). Mark anything a teacher wouldn't accept.

## choose (20)

1. I ______ much better today, **Olga**. Thanks for the **medicine**.  
   options: ✔ feel · feel myself · feel me · myself feel  
   why: Saying how you are now: feel, no myself.

2. Do you ______ OK, **Aziz**? You look **pale**.  
   options: feel yourself · feel you · yourself feel · ✔ feel  
   why: "You look …": asking about now: feel.

3. After the **trip**, I ______ very tired, **Timur**.  
   options: felt me · was felt · ✔ felt · felt myself  
   why: "After the …": past: felt, no myself.

4. My grandma ______ sick **yesterday**, so she stayed in **her flat**.  
   options: herself felt · ✔ felt · felt herself · felt her  
   why: Past ("so she stayed"): felt.

5. I always ______ nervous before an **operation**, **Olga**.  
   options: ✔ feel · feel myself · feel me · am feel  
   why: "always": habit: feel, no myself.

6. How do you ______ **now**, **Lola**?  
   options: feel yourself · feel you · yourself feel · ✔ feel  
   why: Asking how someone is: feel.

7. Sit down and ______, **Timur**. The **film** starts in an hour.  
   options: relax you · yourself relax · ✔ relax · relax yourself  
   why: "Sit down and …": relax, no yourself.

8. On **Saturday** we ______ at home and watched **films**.  
   options: ourselves relaxed · ✔ relaxed · relaxed ourselves · relaxed us  
   why: "On … we": past: relaxed, no ourselves.

9. I can't ______ in this noisy **hotel**, **Aziz**.  
   options: ✔ relax · relax myself · relax me · myself relax  
   why: "this noisy …": can't + relax.

10. I can't ______ on my **reading** when the TV is on, **Olga**.  
   options: concentrate myself · concentrate me · myself concentrate · ✔ concentrate  
   why: "when the TV is on": can't + concentrate, no myself.

11. Please ______ on the **test**, **Aziz**.  
   options: concentrate you · yourself concentrate · ✔ concentrate · concentrate yourself  
   why: Asking someone to focus: concentrate on.

12. In the **maths test** I ______ hard and did well, **Lola**.  
   options: was concentrated · ✔ concentrated · concentrated myself · concentrated me  
   why: Past ("did well"): concentrated.

13. Let's ______ at the **café** at **seven**.  
   options: ✔ meet · meet ourselves · meet us · ourselves meet  
   why: "Let's": meet, no ourselves.

14. **Lola** and I first ______ at a **wedding** **two years ago**.  
   options: met ourselves · met us · ourselves met · ✔ met  
   why: "first … at a …": past: met.

15. The **concert** ______ at seven, **Timur**.  
   options: starts it · itself starts · ✔ starts · starts itself  
   why: Timetable: starts, no itself.

16. The **bazaar** ______ at nine and closes at **five**.  
   options: itself opens · ✔ opens · opens itself · opens it  
   why: Timetable: opens, no itself.

17. The **concert** ______ at **eleven** **yesterday**.  
   options: ✔ finished · finished itself · finished it · itself finished  
   why: Past: finished, no itself.

18. Suddenly the **office** door ______ and **the teacher** came in.  
   options: opened itself · opened it · itself opened · ✔ opened  
   why: "Suddenly … came in": past: opened.

19. I ______ from **Samarkand** **last week**, **Olga**.  
   options: returned myself · came back me · ✔ came back · came myself back  
   why: Past: came back, no myself.

20. We must ______, **Olga**. The **bus** leaves in five minutes.  
   options: ourselves hurry · ✔ hurry · hurry ourselves · hurry us  
   why: "leaves in five minutes": we must hurry.

## transform (15)

1. _Correct the mistake._  
   I feel myself very tired after the **walk**, **Aziz**.  
   → I ______ very tired after the **walk**, **Aziz**.  
   key: feel (also: am feeling, felt)  
   why: feel needs no myself: I feel tired.

2. _Correct the mistake._  
   Do you feel yourself better **now**, **Timur**?  
   → Do you ______ better **now**, **Timur**?  
   key: feel  
   why: feel needs no yourself: Do you feel better?

3. _Correct the mistake._  
   We relaxed ourselves at the **park** **last night**.  
   → We ______ at the **park** **last night**.  
   key: relaxed (also: were relaxing)  
   why: relax needs no ourselves: We relaxed.

4. _Correct the mistake._  
   I can't concentrate myself on the **game**, **Aziz**.  
   → I can't ______ on the **game**, **Aziz**.  
   key: concentrate  
   why: concentrate needs no myself: concentrate on.

5. _Correct the mistake._  
   Let's meet ourselves at the **bazaar** at **eight**.  
   → Let's ______ at the **bazaar** at **eight**.

   key: started

14. _Make it past._  
   We meet at the **café**, **Aziz**.  
   → We ______ at the **café** **last week**, **Aziz**.  
   key: met

15. _Make it past._

   → I ______ well in the **exam** **yesterday**, **Aziz**.  
   key: concentrated

## create (8)

1. Write one sentence about how you feel today.  
   must use: I feel … today. · exactly one sentence · feel with no myself · an adjective after feel

2. Write one sentence about how you relax at the weekend.  
   must use: At the weekend I relax by … · exactly one sentence · relax with no myself · a present verb

3. Write one sentence about where you can't concentrate.  
   must use: I can't concentrate when … · exactly one sentence · concentrate with no myself · can't

4. Write one sentence inviting a friend to meet you.  
   must use: Let's meet at … at … · exactly one sentence · meet with no ourselves · a place and a time

5. Write one sentence about when a film or lesson starts.  
   must use: The … starts at … · exactly one sentence · starts with no itself · a time

6. Write one sentence about how you felt after a long day.  
   must use: After …, I felt … · exactly one sentence · felt with no myself · a past time

7. Write one sentence about when a shop near you opens and closes.  
   must use: The … opens at … and closes at … · exactly one sentence · opens / closes with no itself · two times

8. Write one sentence telling a friend to hurry.  
   must use: Hurry up! The … is leaving. · one or two short sentences · hurry with no yourself · a reason
