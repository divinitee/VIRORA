## transform (15)

1. _Correct the mistake._  
   You must to wear a **seatbelt** here, **Lola**.  
   → You ______ a **seatbelt** here, **Lola**.  
   key: must wear  
   why: After must, there is no to: must wear.

2. _Correct the mistake._  
   My little **cousin** musts go to bed at nine.  
   → My little **cousin** ______ to bed at nine.  
   key: must go  
   why: Must never takes -s: she must go, he must go.

3. _Correct the mistake._  
   **Bobur** must calls his **aunt** tonight.  
   → **Bobur** ______ his **aunt** tonight.  
   key: must call  
   why: After must, use the base verb with no -s: must call.

4. _Correct the mistake._  
   We must going home now, **Aziz**. It's **dark**.  
   → We ______ home now, **Aziz**. It's **dark**.  
   key: must go  
   why: After must, use the base verb, not -ing: must go.

5. _Correct the mistake._  
   I must finished my **project** today, **Aziz**.  
   → I ______ my **project** today, **Aziz**.  
   key: must finish  
   why: After must, use the base verb, not the past form: must finish.

6. _Change the order into a rule. Use You must._
