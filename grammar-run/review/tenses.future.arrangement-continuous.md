## create (8)

1. Write one sentence about someone you are meeting this week. Say when.  
   must use: present continuous for an arrangement · exactly one sentence · a day or time · am / is / are + -ing

2. Write one sentence about a trip your family is taking soon, and how you know it is fixed.  
   must use: arrangement + evidence · one or two sentences · a place named · present continuous

3. Write one sentence about an appointment you have this month.  
   must use: am seeing / am going · exactly one sentence · a day named · present continuous

4. Write one sentence about what a friend is doing on Saturday evening.  
   must use: is + -ing with he or she · exactly one sentence · must name the friend · is + -ing

5. Write one sentence saying no to an invitation because you have another plan.  
   must use: present continuous as a reason · one or two sentences · Sorry, I can't ... · I am + -ing

6. Write one sentence about a family event next weekend.  
   must use: are + -ing for a family plan · exactly one sentence · next weekend · present continuous

7. Write one sentence about who is coming to your home for dinner this week.  
   must use: is / are coming · exactly one sentence · a day named · coming

8. Write two sentences from your diary for next week: two different arrangements.  
   must use: two arrangements · exactly two sentences · two different days · present continuous in both

## express (5)

1. Describe your plans for next weekend. Who are you meeting, and where are you going? Write three or four sentences.  
   target: present continuous for arrangements · look for: am / is / are + -ing · days or times · people named

2. A friend invites you to the cinema on Friday, but you are busy. Write a message: say no and explain your arrangements. Write three or four sentences.  
   target: present continuous to explain plans · look for: I'm + -ing · a time · polite refusal

3. Describe your family's plans for a holiday such as Navruz or New Year. Write three or four sentences.  
   target: present continuous for family arrangements · look for: we are + -ing · who is coming · where you are going

4. Look at your week. Describe three appointments or meetings you have. Write three or four sentences.  
   target: present continuous with fixed times · look for: am + -ing · a day in each sentence · no will for arrangements

5. Describe a trip that someone in your family is taking soon. Write three or four sentences.  
   target: is / are + -ing for travel plans · look for: is + -ing · a place and a day · how you know it is booked
