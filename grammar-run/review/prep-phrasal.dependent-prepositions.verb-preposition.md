# prep-phrasal.dependent-prepositions.verb-preposition — review sample (B1)

Each item shows one random filler combination (in bold). Mark anything a teacher wouldn't accept.

## choose (20)

1. Whether we go to the **lake** or not ______ the weather, **Gulnora**.  
   options: ✔ depends on · depends of · depends from · depends  
   why: depend on: it depends on the weather.

2. We need to ______ the new **timetable** at the meeting, **Sardor**.  
   options: discuss about · discuss on · discuss of · ✔ discuss  
   why: discuss takes its object straight away: discuss the timetable, no preposition.

3. My grandpa is ______ the old **music** on the radio, **Gulnora**.  
   options: listening on · listening of · ✔ listening to · listening  
   why: listen to + what you hear.

4. We've been ______ the **bus** for twenty minutes, **Malika**.  
   options: waiting of · ✔ waiting for · waiting · waiting to  
   why: wait for + the person or thing you expect.

5. Everyone stood up when the **bride** ______ the room, **Akmal**.  
   options: ✔ entered · entered in · entered to · entered at  
   why: enter takes its object straight away: entered the room.

6. This **phone** ______ my sister, **Malika**. Please don't take it.  
   options: belongs · belongs of · belongs for · ✔ belongs to  
   why: belong to + the owner.

7. I ______ you, **Nodira**. The **flat** is too expensive.  
   options: agree at · agree of · ✔ agree with · agree  
   why: agree with + a person.

8. My cousin ______ a teacher from **Namangan** last summer, **Sardor**.  
   options: married on · ✔ married · married with · married to  
   why: marry takes its object straight away: she married a teacher.

9. Can I ______ the **cake** with a card, **Nodira**?  
   options: ✔ pay for · pay · pay of · pay on  
   why: pay for + the thing you buy.

10. We ______ the bill after dinner at the **chaikhana**, **Zarina**.  
   options: asked · asked of · asked on · ✔ asked for  
   why: ask for + the thing you want.

11. We finally ______ **Samarkand** at midnight, **Nodira**.  
   options: reached in · reached on · ✔ reached · reached at  
   why: reach takes its object straight away: reached Samarkand.

12. The test ______ three parts: **two essays and an interview**, **Malika**.  
   options: consists · ✔ consists of · consists from · consists with  
   why: consist of + the parts.

13. Everybody ______ my funny **hat**, **Akmal**.  
   options: ✔ laughed at · laughed · laughed on · laughed to  
   why: laugh at + the thing that is funny.

14. Please ______ the **hotel** if you have any questions, **Sardor**.  
   options: contact with · contact to · contact at · ✔ contact  
   why: contact takes its object straight away: contact the teacher.

15. You can always ______ your **best friend**, **Gulnora**.  
   options: rely to · rely · ✔ rely on · rely of  
   why: rely on + the person you trust.

16. We had to ______ our **tickets** for an hour, **Zarina**.  
   options: search to · ✔ search for · search of · search on  
   why: search for + the thing you want to find.

17. I ______ my brother's **bag** for the trip, **Ulugbek**.  
   options: ✔ borrowed · borrowed with · borrowed to · borrowed by  
   why: borrow takes the thing straight away: borrowed his bag.

18. The baby ______ me and I **smiled back**, **Sardor**.  
   options: smiled · smiled to · smiled of · ✔ smiled at  
   why: smile at + the person.

19. The **managers** ______ the problem for two hours, **Akmal**.  
   options: discussed on · discussed of · ✔ discussed · discussed about  
   why: discuss + object, no preposition.

20. My uncle ______ terrible headaches in the **summer**, **Malika**.  
   options: suffers · ✔ suffers from · suffers of · suffers on  
   why: suffer from + the problem.

## transform (15)

1. _Correct the mistake._  
   It depends of the price of the **flat**, **Nodira**.  
   → It depends ______ the price of the **flat**, **Nodira**.  
   key: on (also: upon)  
   why: depend on, not depend of.

2. _Correct the mistake._  
   I listen music every day on the **metro**, **Zarina**.  
   → I listen ______ music every day on the **metro**, **Zarina**.  
   key: to  
   why: listen needs to before its object.

3. _Correct the mistake._  
   We're waiting the **bus**, **Akmal**.  
   → We're waiting ______ the **bus**, **Akmal**.  
   key: for  
   why: wait needs for before its object.

4. _Correct the mistake._  
   I agree to you about the **hotel**, **Otabek**.  
   → I agree ______ you about the **hotel**, **Otabek**.  
   key: with  
   why: agree with a person.

5. _Correct the mistake._  
   Can you explain me the **game**, **Ulugbek**?  
   → Can you explain the **game** ______ me, **Ulugbek**?  
   key: to  
   why: explain something to someone, not explain me.

6. _Rewrite with the verb in brackets._  
   (belong) Whose **bag** is this, **Zarina**?  
   → Who does this **bag** belong ______, **Zarina**?  
   key: to  
   why: belong to; in a question the preposition goes at the end.

7. _Rewrite with the verb in brackets._  
   (ask) I'm phoning because I need help from the **doctor**, **Akmal**.  
   → I'm phoning to ask the **doctor** ______ help, **Akmal**.  
   key: for  
   why: ask someone for something.

8. _Rewrite with the verb in brackets._  
   (consist) The **course** has three parts, **Malika**.  
   → The **course** consists ______ three parts, **Malika**.  
   key: of  
   why: consist of + the parts.

9. _Rewrite with the verb in brackets._  
   (rely) Can I trust the **builders**, **Ulugbek**?  
   → Can I rely ______ the **builders**, **Ulugbek**?  
   key: on (also: upon)  
   why: rely on = trust.

10. _Rewrite with the verb in brackets._  
   (suffer) My aunt often has headaches in the **spring**, **Zarina**.

   key: from (also: with)  
   why: suffer from + the problem.

11. _Rewrite with the verb in brackets._

   → The baby smiled ______ me, **Ulugbek**. It was in the **park**.  
   key: at

12. _Rewrite with the verb in brackets._  
   (borrow) My brother gave me some money. I'll give it back on **Thursday**, **Zarina**.  
   → I borrowed some money ______ my brother, **Zarina**. I'll give it back on **Thursday**.  
   key: from

13. _Rewrite with the verb in brackets._

   → Don't worry, **Nodira**. I'll pay ______ the **taxi**.  
   key: for

14. _Rewrite with the verb in brackets._

   → Everyone laughed ______ my **joke**, **Otabek**.  
   key: at

   target: rely on / ask for / listen to · look for: rely on · another verb + preposition · three or four sentences

4. Write three or four sentences about what you spend your money on.  
   target: spend on / pay for · look for: spend on · pay for · three or four sentences

5. Write three or four sentences about a time you borrowed something and something happened to it.
