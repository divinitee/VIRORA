# sentence-structure.relative-clauses.which-vs-what — review sample (B1)

Each item shows one random filler combination (in bold). Mark anything a teacher wouldn't accept.

## choose (20)

1. I couldn't find the **email** ______ you sent me, **Timur**.  
   options: ✔ which · what · who · where  
   why: The noun comes first (the …): which.

2. The **phone** ______ I bought **yesterday** has already broken.  
   options: what · who · where · ✔ which  
   why: After a noun (the …): which.

3. Do you still have the **scarf** ______ Grandma gave you, **Timur**?  
   options: who · where · ✔ which · what  
   why: After a noun: the … which Grandma gave.

4. This is the **soup** ______ my mum makes every **holiday**.  
   options: where · ✔ which · what · who  
   why: After a noun: the … which my mum makes.

5. We watched a **video** ______ made everyone cry, **Olga**.  
   options: ✔ which · what · who · where  
   why: After a noun (a …): which.

6. The bus ______ goes to **Bukhara** leaves at **nine**.  
   options: what · who · where · ✔ which  
   why: After a noun (The bus): which.

7. I've lost the **book** ______ you lent me, **Aziz**. I'm so sorry!  
   options: who · where · ✔ which · what  
   why: After a noun: the … which you lent.

8. The **café** ______ opened near us sells **great coffee**.  
   options: where · ✔ which · what · who  
   why: After a noun: the … which opened.

9. Is this the **game** ______ you wanted, **Aziz**?  
   options: ✔ which · what · who · where  
   why: After a noun: the … which you wanted.

10. **Bekzod** passed the **driving test**, ______ surprised everyone.  
   options: what · who · where · ✔ which  
   why: After a comma, about the whole idea: which.

11. It rained all **day**, ______ meant we stayed at home, **Aziz**.  
   options: who · where · ✔ which · what  
   why: After a comma, about the rain all …: which.

12. My brother cooked dinner **last week**, ______ was a nice surprise.  
   options: where · ✔ which · what · who  
   why: After a comma, about him cooking: which.

13. I didn't hear ______ the **teacher** said, **Lola**.  
   options: ✔ what · which · that · who  
   why: Nothing before the gap but hear: what = the words that.

14. Thank you for ______ you did for us **last night**, **Lola**.  
   options: which · that · who · ✔ what  
   why: No noun before it: what = the things that.

15. ______ I need **today** is a long **holiday**.  
   options: That · Who · ✔ What · Which  
   why: No noun before it: What = the thing that.

16. That's not ______ I ordered, **Timur**. I asked for **a salad**.  
   options: who · ✔ what · which · that  
   why: "not … I ordered", no noun: what = the thing that.

17. This **bike** is exactly ______ I wanted, **Aziz**!  
   options: ✔ what · which · that · who  
   why: After exactly, no noun: what.

18. I can't **forget** ______ he did at the **meeting**.  
   options: which · that · who · ✔ what  
   why: "I can't … " + no noun after it: what he did.

19. Did you understand ______ she said in **Russian**, **Timur**?  
   options: that · who · ✔ what · which  
   why: After understand, no noun: what.

20. ______ we **ate** in **Bukhara** was amazing.  
   options: Who · ✔ What · Which · That  
   why: No noun before it: What = the food that.

## transform (15)

1. _Correct the mistake._  
   The **parcel** what you sent me is broken, **Timur**.  
   → The **parcel** ______ you sent me is broken, **Timur**.  
   key: which (also: that)  
   why: After a noun, never what: which.

2. _Correct the mistake._  
   I liked the **plov** what your mum cooked **last night**.  
   → I liked the **plov** ______ your mum cooked **last night**.  
   key: which (also: that)  
   why: After a noun (the …): which, not what.

3. _Correct the mistake._  
   This is the **café** what I told you about, **Aziz**.  
   → This is the **café** ______ I told you about, **Aziz**.  
   key: which (also: that)  
   why: "the … I told you about" has a noun first: which.

4. _Correct the mistake._  
   **Timur** lost his **wallet**, what was a big problem.  
   → **Timur** lost his **wallet**, ______ was a big problem.  
   key: which  
   why: After a comma about the whole idea: which, not what.

5. _Correct the mistake._  
   It snowed all **morning**, what was beautiful, **Olga**.  
   → It snowed all **morning**, ______ was beautiful, **Olga**.  
   key: which  
   why: After a comma about the snow: which, not what.

6. _Correct the mistake._  
   I don't understand that you said **at lunch**, **Aziz**.  
   → I don't understand ______ you said **at lunch**, **Aziz**.  
   key: what  
   why: No noun: what, not that.

7. _Correct the mistake._  
   Which I need is a new **laptop**, **Timur**.  
   → ______ I need is a new **laptop**, **Timur**.  
   key: What  
   why: At the start, no noun: What, not Which.

8. _Correct the mistake._  
   Thank you for that you did **last night**, **Aziz**.  
   → Thank you for ______ you did **last night**, **Aziz**.  
   key: what  
   why: No noun after for: what, not that.

9. _Correct the mistake._  
   That's exactly which I wanted for my **kitchen**, **Olga**!  
   → That's exactly ______ I wanted for my **kitchen**, **Olga**!  
   key: what  
   why: No noun after exactly: what, not which.

10. _Correct the mistake._  
   Tell me which happened in **Termez**, **Timur**.  
   → Tell me ______ happened in **Termez**, **Timur**.  
   key: what  
   why: No noun after Tell me: what, not which.

11. _Rewrite with what._  
   The thing that I need is a long **sleep**, **Aziz**.  
   → ______ I need is a long **sleep**, **Aziz**.  
   key: What  
   why: The thing that → What.

12. _Rewrite with what._  
   I didn't hear the thing that you said **at lunch**, **Lola**.  
   → I didn't hear ______ you said **at lunch**, **Lola**.  
   key: what  
   why: the thing that → what.

13. _Rewrite with what._  
   The things that we saw in **Bukhara** were amazing, **Aziz**.  
   → ______ we saw in **Bukhara** was amazing, **Aziz**.  
   key: What  
   why: The things that → What (with was).

14. _Rewrite with what._  
   Show me the things that you bought at the **bazaar**, **Timur**.  
   → Show me ______ you bought at the **bazaar**, **Timur**.  
   key: what  
   why: the things that → what.

15. _Rewrite with what._  
   The thing that I like about my **room** is the view, **Timur**.  
   → ______ I like about my **room** is the view, **Timur**.

## create (8)

1. Write one sentence about a present you got, with which after the noun.  
   must use: The … which my … gave me is … · exactly one sentence · noun + which · no what after the noun

2. Write one sentence that starts with What I need.  
   must use: What I need is … · exactly one sentence · What at the start · no noun before what

3. Write one sentence about a film, with which after the word film.  
   must use: The film which we saw … · exactly one sentence · film + which · a past verb

4. Write one sentence saying you didn't hear something.  
   must use: I didn't hear what … · exactly one sentence · what + subject + verb · no that / which

5. Write one sentence about some good news, then comment on it with , which.  
   must use: … passed the exam, which was great. · exactly one sentence · a comma before which · which talks about the whole idea

6. Write one sentence thanking someone for their help, with what.  
   must use: Thank you for what you … · exactly one sentence · for what · a past verb

7. Write one sentence about a shop near you, with which.  
   must use: The shop which … sells … · exactly one sentence · noun + which · present simple

8. Write two sentences: one with which after a noun and one with what.  
   must use: The book which … / What I like … · exactly two sentences · which after a noun · what with no noun before it

## express (5)

1. Write about your favourite day of the year: what you like about it and the food you eat.  
   target: What I like most is … The food which my mum makes … · look for: which after a noun · what with no noun · three or four sentences

2. Write a short review of a café or shop.  
   target: The coffee which they sell … What I didn't like was … · look for: which and what · present or past verbs · three or four sentences

3. Describe a surprise that happened to you.  
   target: My friend came home, which … I didn't know what … · look for: which after a comma · what · three or four sentences

4. Write a message to a friend thanking them for a present.  
   target: Thank you for what you sent. The scarf which … · look for: which and what · past verbs · three or four sentences

5. Describe a trip and the things you saw.  
   target: What we saw was … The museum which we visited … · look for: which after a noun · what at the start of a sentence · three or four sentences
