   must use: … is a shop where I buy … · exactly one sentence · where · a thing word

5. Write one sentence about a place where you meet your friends.  
   must use: … is the place where we meet. · exactly one sentence · where · a time word

6. Write one sentence about the school where you study or studied.  
   must use: The school where I … · exactly one sentence · where · a past or present verb
