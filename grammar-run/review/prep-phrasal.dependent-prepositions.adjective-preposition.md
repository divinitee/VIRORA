## create (8)

1. Write one sentence about something you are good at.  
   must use: good at + noun or -ing · exactly one sentence · must be about you · must use good at

2. Write one sentence about something you are afraid of, or were afraid of as a child.  
   must use: afraid of + noun · exactly one sentence · must use afraid of · must not use afraid from

3. Write one sentence about a person or a team you are proud of.  
   must use: proud of + noun · exactly one sentence · must use proud of · must name the person or team

4. Write one sentence about something your town or village is famous for.  
   must use: famous for + noun · exactly one sentence · must use famous for · must name the place

5. Write one sentence about something you are worried about this week.  
   must use: worried about + noun · exactly one sentence · must use worried about · must be about this week

6. Write one sentence about a person who is kind to you.  
   must use: kind to + person · exactly one sentence · must use kind to · must name the person

7. Write two sentences: one thing you are good at and one thing you are bad at.  
   must use: good at and bad at · exactly two sentences · must use good at · must use bad at

8. A friend wrote: "I am afraid from dogs and I am not good in sport." Write the sentence correctly.  
   must use: afraid of and good at · exactly one sentence · must use afraid of · must use good at

## express (5)

1. What are you good at and bad at? Write three or four sentences about school subjects, sport or hobbies.  
   target: good at / bad at · look for: good at · bad at · at before a noun or -ing form

2. What were you afraid of when you were a child? Are you still afraid of it? Write three or four sentences.  
   target: afraid of / scared of · look for: afraid of or scared of · no afraid from · past and present

3. Describe your best friend. What is he or she good at, proud of or worried about? Write three or four sentences.  
   target: adjective + its preposition · look for: good at · proud of or worried about · the right preposition each time

4. Tell a visitor about your town or region. What is it famous for and what are people proud of? Write three or four sentences.  
   target: famous for / proud of · look for: famous for · proud of · the right preposition each time

5. Are you ready for your next exam or lesson? What are you worried about? Write three or four sentences.  
   target: ready for / worried about · look for: ready for · worried about · the right preposition each time
