# questions-negation.negation.no-vs-not — review sample (B1)

Each item shows one random filler combination (in bold). Mark anything a teacher wouldn't accept.

## transform (15)

1. _Rewrite with no. Keep the meaning._  
   There isn't any milk in the **bottle**, **Timur**.  
   → There is ______ milk in the **bottle**, **Timur**.  
   key: no  
   why: isn't any = is no.

2. _Rewrite with no. Keep the meaning._  
   We don't have any lessons on **Saturday**, **Aziz**.  
   → We have ______ lessons on **Saturday**, **Aziz**.  
   key: no  
   why: don't have any = have no.

3. _Rewrite with no. Keep the meaning._  
   I haven't got any money for the **trip**, **Olga**.  
   → I have got ______ money for the **trip**, **Olga**.  
   key: no  
   why: haven't got any = have got no.

4. _Rewrite with no. Keep the meaning._  
   There aren't any **cafés** in our **village**.  
   → There are ______ **cafés** in our **village**.  
   key: no  
   why: aren't any = are no.

5. _Rewrite with no. Keep the meaning._  
   **Timur** doesn't have any time **before the exam**.  
   → **Timur** has ______ time **before the exam**.  
   key: no  
   why: doesn't have any = has no.

6. _Rewrite with not … any. Keep the meaning._  
   There is no sugar in my **coffee**, **Lola**.  
   → There is ______ ______ sugar in my **coffee**, **Lola**.  
   key: not + any  
   why: is no = is not any.

7. _Rewrite with not … any. Keep the meaning._  
   We have no bread for **breakfast**, **Olga**.  
   → We do ______ have ______ bread for **breakfast**, **Olga**.  
   key: not + any  
   why: have no = do not have any.

8. _Rewrite with not … any. Keep the meaning._  
   There were no **buses** at the **bus stop**.  
   → There were ______ ______ **buses** at the **bus stop**.  
   key: not + any  
   why: were no = were not any.

9. _Rewrite with not … any. Keep the meaning._  
   **Aziz** has no homework **for Monday**.  
   → **Aziz** does ______ have ______ homework **for Monday**.  
   key: not + any  
   why: has no = does not have any.

10. _Rewrite with not … any. Keep the meaning._  
   I have no plans for the **holidays**, **Lola**.  
   → I do ______ have ______ plans for the **holidays**, **Lola**.  
   key: not + any  
   why: I have no plans = I do not have any plans.

11. _Correct the mistake._  
   There is no any milk in the **kitchen**, **Olga**.  
   → There is ______ any milk in the **kitchen**, **Olga**.  
   key: not  
   why: Never no any: not any.

12. _Correct the mistake._  
   I have not money for the **ticket**, **Lola**.  
   → I have ______ money for the **ticket**, **Lola**.  
   key: no  
   why: Before a noun (money): no, not not.

13. _Correct the mistake._

   → The shop is ______ open on **Navruz**, **Olga**.  
   key: not  
   why: Before an adjective: not, not no.

14. _Correct the mistake._

   → We have ______ time for **breakfast**, **Aziz**.  
   key: no

15. _Correct the mistake._

## create (8)

1. Write one sentence about something your home has none of.  
   must use: There is / are no … · exactly one sentence · no + noun · a positive verb

2. Write one sentence about something you are not today.  
   must use: I'm not … today. · exactly one sentence · not + adjective · today

3. Write one sentence about food you don't have at home now. Use not … any.  
   must use: We don't have any … · exactly one sentence · not + any · a food word

4. Write one sentence about a free day: what you have no time for, or no lessons.  
   must use: I have no … on … · exactly one sentence · no + noun · a day word

5. Write one sentence about a place that is not open or not near.  
   must use: The … is not … · exactly one sentence · not + adjective or place · a place word

6. Write one sentence about your town using There are no …  
   must use: There are no … in my town. · exactly one sentence · no + plural noun · There are

7. Write one sentence about a person who is not at home now.  
   must use: My … is not at home. · exactly one sentence · not at home · a person word

8. Write two sentences: one with no and one with not.  
   must use: There is no … / It is not … · exactly two sentences · no + noun · not + adjective or verb

## express (5)

1. Describe a small village: what does it have and what does it not have?  
   target: There is no … / It is not … · look for: no + noun · not + adjective · three or four sentences

2. You can't come to a friend's party. Write a message explaining why.  
   target: I'm not … / I have no time … · look for: no and not · a reason · three or four sentences

3. Describe a bad day at a hotel or hostel.  
   target: There was no hot water … The room was not … · look for: no + noun · not + adjective · three or four sentences

4. Describe your fridge or kitchen before shopping day.  
   target: There is no … / There isn't any … · look for: no + noun · not … any · three or four sentences

5. Describe a holiday when you had no lessons or work.  
   target: We had no … / I was not … · look for: no and not · past verbs · three or four sentences
