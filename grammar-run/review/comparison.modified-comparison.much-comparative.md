## transform (15)

   The **coach** takes ten hours. The plane takes one hour, **Feruza**.  
   → The plane is ______ than the **coach**, **Feruza**.  
   key: much faster  
   why: Ten hours against one: much faster.

   My phone cost 2 million soum. **Otabek**'s phone cost **12** million.  
   → **Otabek**'s phone was ______ than mine.  
   key: much more expensive  
   why: A few million more: much more expensive.

3. _The difference is big. Rewrite with much and a comparative._
