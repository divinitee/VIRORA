## transform (15)

1. _Make a yes/no question. Write one word in each gap._  
   You are waiting for the **taxi**, **Timur**.  
   → ______ you ______ for the **taxi**, **Timur**?  
   key: Are + waiting  
   why: Move are before you; the verb stays in the -ing form.
