# prep-phrasal.phrasal-verbs.inseparable — review sample (B1)

Each item shows one random filler combination (in bold). Mark anything a teacher wouldn't accept.

## choose (20)

1. Who ______ while you were in **Moscow**, **Dilnoza**?  
   options: ✔ looked after your cat · looked your cat after · looked your cat after it · looked after for your cat  
   why: look after is inseparable: looked after your cat.

2. Grandma is ill. Can you ______ this **weekend**, **Zarina**?  
   options: look her after · look after she · look after for her · ✔ look after her  
   why: Even with a pronoun, look after stays together: look after her.

3. I've lost my **glasses**. I've been ______ all morning, **Dilnoza**.  
   options: looking for they · looking for to them · ✔ looking for them · looking them for  
   why: look for is inseparable: looking for them.

4. The **tap** is broken, but don't worry. We can ______, **Otabek**.  
   options: deal with for it · ✔ deal with it · deal it with · deal it with it  
   why: deal with is inseparable: deal with it.

5. These are your **texts** for the exam. Let's ______ together, **Sardor**.  
   options: ✔ go through them · go them through · go through they · go through for them  
   why: go through (= check one by one) is inseparable: go through them.

6. I ______ at the **airport** yesterday. I hadn't seen him for years, **Otabek**!  
   options: ran him into · ran into he · ran into for him · ✔ ran into him  
   why: run into (= meet by chance) is inseparable: ran into him.

7. I was cleaning the **attic** and I ______ under the bed, **Feruza**. An old photo!  
   options: came it across it · came across for it · ✔ came across it · came it across  
   why: come across (= find by chance) is inseparable: came across it.

8. My sister really ______. They look the same as our **grandma**, **Zarina**.  
   options: takes after for her · ✔ takes after her · takes her after · takes after she  
   why: take after (= look or act like) is inseparable: takes after her.

9. He had a bad cold, but he'll ______ soon, **Feruza**. He's **resting**.  
   options: ✔ get over it · get it over · get it over it · get over for it  
   why: get over (= recover from) is inseparable: get over it.

10. Don't worry about the **food**, **Akmal**. You can ______.  
   options: count me on · count on I · count on for me · ✔ count on me  
   why: count on (= trust) is inseparable: count on me.

11. The **bank** said the problem was strange and they are ______ now, **Feruza**.  
   options: looking it into it · looking into for it · ✔ looking into it · looking it into  
   why: look into (= study a problem) is inseparable: looking into it.

12. The manager got three **letters** and ______ quickly, **Otabek**.  
   options: dealt with for them · ✔ dealt with them · dealt them with · dealt with they  
   why: deal with is inseparable: dealt with them.

13. It was a hard time for the **family**, but they ______ together, **Sardor**.  
   options: ✔ went through it · went it through · went it through it · went through for it  
   why: go through (= live through) is inseparable: went through it.

14. Who will ______ when you're at the **airport**, **Zarina**?  
   options: look the children after · look the children after it · look after for the children · ✔ look after the children  
   why: look after + noun: the noun goes after the particle too.

15. Have you seen my little brother? Mum is ______ in the **park**, **Dilnoza**.  
   options: looking for he · looking for to him · ✔ looking for him · looking him for  
   why: look for is inseparable: looking for him.

16. Excuse me, where should I ______ for the **station**, **Akmal**?  
   options: get off for the bus · ✔ get off the bus · get the bus off · get the bus off it  
   why: get off + the bus.

17. We ______ outside the **cinema**. Our old neighbours, **Jamshid**!  
   options: ✔ ran into them · ran them into · ran into they · ran into for them  
   why: run into is inseparable: ran into them.

18. I ______ on the internet. They are very cheap **books**, **Zarina**.  
   options: came them across · came across they · came across for them · ✔ came across them  
   why: come across is inseparable: came across them.

19. My son ______. He loves football like his **grandpa**, **Sardor**.  
   options: takes after he · takes after for him · ✔ takes after him · takes him after  
   why: take after is inseparable: takes after him.

20. You can always ______, **Otabek**. We're your **friends**.  
   options: count on for us · ✔ count on us · count us on · count on we  
   why: count on is inseparable: count on us.

## transform (15)

1. _Rewrite with the pronoun in brackets instead of the noun. One word in each gap._  
   (her) I ran into my old teacher at the **bank**, **Feruza**.  
   → I ran ______ ______ at the **bank**, **Feruza**.  
   key: into + her  
   why: ran into her: the pronoun goes after the particle.

2. _Rewrite with the pronoun in brackets instead of the noun. One word in each gap._  
   (it) Who looks after your grandparents' **cow**, **Akmal**?  
   → Who looks ______ ______, **Akmal**?  
   key: after + it  
   why: look after stays together: after it, never look it after.

3. _Rewrite with the pronoun in brackets instead of the noun. One word in each gap._  
   (it) We got on the last **bus** just in time, **Sardor**.  
   → We got ______ ______ just in time, **Sardor**.  
   key: on + it  
   why: got on it: inseparable.

4. _Rewrite with the pronoun in brackets instead of the noun. One word in each gap._  
   (them) I came across these **coins** in a box, **Gulnora**.  
   → I came ______ ______ in a box, **Gulnora**.  
   key: across + them  
   why: come across stays together: across them, never came them across.

5. _Rewrite with the pronoun in brackets instead of the noun. One word in each gap._  
   (him) Are you still looking for your **son**, **Jamshid**?  
   → Are you still looking ______ ______, **Jamshid**?

11. _Say it with the phrasal verb. One word in each gap._

   key: into + my

   → She came ______ ______ old **album** in the cupboard, **Feruza**.
