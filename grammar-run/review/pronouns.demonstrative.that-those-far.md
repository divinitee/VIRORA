## create (8)

1. Look out of your window. Write one sentence about one thing you can see far away. Use That.  
   must use: That + is · exactly one sentence · must start with That · is, not are

2. Look out of your window. Write one sentence about some things you can see far away. Use Those.  
   must use: Those + are · exactly one sentence · must start with Those · a plural noun or are

3. You are in a shop. Ask about the price of one thing on a shelf far from you.  
   must use: How much is that …? · exactly one question · must use that · must end with a question mark

4. You are in the street with a friend. Ask who some people over there are.  
   must use: Who are those …? · exactly one question · must use those · must end with a question mark

5. Write one sentence about a building in your town that you can see from your home.  
   must use: that + noun · exactly one sentence · must use that · must name the building

6. A friend shows you two bags: one in their hand, some over there. Say which you like.  
   must use: this one / those · exactly one sentence · must use those · must say what you like

7. Write one sentence about the mountains, hills or trees far from your town.  
   must use: those + plural noun · exactly one sentence · must use those · a far word: far away, over there

8. Write two sentences about things you can see from your school: one with that, one with those.  
   must use: that + singular / those + plural · exactly two sentences · one that, one those · is after that, are after those

## express (5)

1. You are on a high building in your town with a visitor. Tell them what they can see far away.  
   target: that / those for far things · look for: That is … · Those are … · over there / far away

2. You are at the market. Ask the seller about some things that are far from you, and say what you want.  
   target: that / those + noun · look for: How much is that …? · those + plural noun · I want …

3. Describe what you can see from your window: near things and far things.  
   target: this / these and that / those · look for: this or these for near things · that or those for far things · is / are after each one

4. You are in the school yard with a new student. Tell them who the people over there are.  
   target: That is … / Those are … · look for: That is my … · Those are my … · names and family or school words

5. Write a short message about a photo of a place in Uzbekistan you like. Point out things far away in it.  
   target: that / those · look for: that + one thing · those + plural · three or four sentences
