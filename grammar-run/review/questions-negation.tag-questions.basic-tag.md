## create (8)

1. Write one sentence with a tag to check that a friend likes football.  
   must use: You like football, don't you? · exactly one sentence · positive statement · negative tag

2. Write one sentence with a tag to check that the shop isn't open today.  
   must use: The shop isn't open today, is it? · exactly one sentence · negative statement · positive tag

3. Write one sentence with a tag about something a friend did yesterday.  
   must use: You went to …, didn't you? · exactly one sentence · a past verb · didn't + pronoun

4. Write one sentence with a tag about what someone can do.  
   must use: … can …, can't she / he? · exactly one sentence · can · can't in the tag

5. Write one sentence with a tag about the weather.  
   must use: It's cold today, isn't it? · exactly one sentence · be · a matching tag

6. Write one sentence with a tag about a plan for tomorrow.  
   must use: We'll meet tomorrow, won't we? · exactly one sentence · will · won't in the tag

7. Write one sentence with a tag about something your family doesn't do.  
   must use: My dad doesn't …, does he? · exactly one sentence · doesn't · does + pronoun

8. Write two sentences with tags: one positive statement and one negative statement.  
   must use: … , don't you? / … , is it? · exactly two sentences · a negative tag after the positive · a positive tag after the negative
