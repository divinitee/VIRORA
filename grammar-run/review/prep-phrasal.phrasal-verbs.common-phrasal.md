# prep-phrasal.phrasal-verbs.common-phrasal — review sample (A2)

Each item shows one random filler combination (in bold). Mark anything a teacher wouldn't accept.

## choose (20)

1. **Lola** wakes ______ when the **rain** starts.  
   options: ✔ up · on · off · out  
   why: Wake up = stop sleeping.

2. It's cold outside, **Aziz**. Put ______ your **warm coat**.  
   options: off · ✔ on · in · up  
   why: Put on = start wearing clothes. Put off does not mean this.

3. Please take ______ your **boots** before you come in, **Timur**.  
   options: on · down · ✔ off · up  
   why: Take off = remove clothes or shoes.

4. It's dark in the **kitchen**. Can you turn ______ the light, **Olga**?  
   options: in · of · ✔ on · at  
   why: Turn on = start a light or a machine.

5. Turn ______ the **tablet** and go to bed, **Olga**.  
   options: of · ✔ off · at · from  
   why: Turn off = stop a machine. Of is a different word.

6. Come in and sit ______, **Olga**. The **dinner** is ready.  
   options: ✔ down · on · off · to  
   why: Sit down = take a seat.

7. Please stand ______ when **the head teacher** comes into the **room**.  
   options: on · ✔ up · off · at  
   why: Stand up = get on your feet.

8. I'm looking ______ my **wallet**, **Mum**. I can't find it.  
   options: ✔ for · to · on · of  
   why: Look for = try to find something.

9. **Our neighbour** looks ______ my little **sister** when Mum is at work.  
   options: of · ✔ after · on · in  
   why: Look after = take care of someone.

10. **Aunt Lola** is coming ______ from **Kazan** next Monday.  
   options: on · off · ✔ back · to  
   why: Come back = return to a place.

11. We often go ______ for dinner with **our cousins**.  
   options: off · ✔ out · on · to  
   why: Go out = leave home to do something, for example eat at a cafe.

12. Pick ______ your **clothes** from the floor, **Lola**.  
   options: ✔ up · on · in · to  
   why: Pick up = take something from the floor or a table.

13. This is our stop, **Olga**. Let's get ______ the **bus**.  
   options: from · out · ✔ off · down  
   why: Get off = leave a bus or a train. Get out needs of (get out of a car).

14. My **father** grew ______ in a small village near **Samarkand**.  
   options: ✔ up · on · out · off  
   why: Grow up = change from a child to an adult.

15. Can I try ______ this **jacket**, **Dad**? It looks nice.  
   options: in · ✔ on · up · at  
   why: Try on = put on clothes to see if they are right for you.

16. I want to lie ______ for an hour, **Aziz**. I'm very **ill**.  
   options: ✔ down · up · off · to  
   why: Lie down = put your body flat on a bed or sofa to rest.

17. Can you take ______ the **bins**, **Aziz**?  
   options: off · ✔ out · up · on  
   why: Take out = carry something out of the house.

18. I want to find ______ the time of the **lesson**, **Dad**.  
   options: about · up · ✔ out · on  
   why: Find out = learn a fact. We don't say find about.

19. Don't give ______, **Aziz**! This **game** is not so hard.  
   options: ✔ up · on · off · away  
   why: Give up = stop trying.

20. Slow ______, **Olga**! The **road** is wet.  
   options: off · ✔ down · out · on  
   why: Slow down = go less fast.

## transform (15)

1. _Correct the mistake._  
   Put off your **scarf**, **Timur**. It's cold.  
   → Put ______ your **scarf**, **Timur**. It's cold.  
   key: on  
   why: Put on = start wearing clothes. Put off is not right here.

2. _Correct the mistake._  
   **My brother** wakes on at seven and **makes the bed**.  
   → **My brother** wakes ______ at seven and **makes the bed**.  
   key: up  
   why: We say wake up, not wake on.

3. _Correct the mistake._  
   Turn of the **computer** and go to sleep, **Olga**.  
   → Turn ______ the **computer** and go to sleep, **Olga**.  
   key: off  
   why: Turn off has two f's. Of is a different word.

4. _Correct the mistake._  
   I'm looking after my **pen**, **Mum**. I can't find it.  
   → I'm looking ______ my **pen**, **Mum**. I can't find it.  
   key: for  
   why: You can't find it, so you are trying to find it: look for.

5. _Correct the mistake._  
   Let's get down the **tram** here, **Aziz**. This is our stop.  
   → Let's get ______ the **tram** here, **Aziz**. This is our stop.  
   key: off  
   why: We get off a bus or a train, not get down.

6. _Say it with come + a small word._  
   My **brother** returns from **Seoul** on Friday.  
   → My **brother** comes ______ from **Seoul** on Friday.  
   key: back  
   why: Return = come back.

7. _Say it with look + a small word._  
   **Grandpa** takes care of my little **sister** when Mum is at work.  
   → **Grandpa** looks ______ my little **sister** when Mum is at work.  
   key: after  
   why: Take care of = look after.

8. _Say it with find + a small word._  
   Can you check the time of the **film**, **Aziz**?  
   → Can you find ______ the time of the **film**, **Aziz**?  
   key: out  
   why: To learn a fact = find out.

9. _Say it with take + a small word._  
   Please remove your **boots** here, **Aziz**.  
   → Please take ______ your **boots** here, **Aziz**.  
   key: off  
   why: Remove clothes or shoes = take off.

10. _Say it with get + a small word._  
   **My mum** gets out of bed at six and **feeds the chickens**.  
   → **My mum** gets ______ at six and **feeds the chickens**.  
   key: up  
   why: Get out of bed = get up.

11. _Say the opposite._  
   Put on your **gloves**, **Timur**.  
   → Take ______ your **gloves**, **Timur**.  
   key: off  
   why: The opposite of put on is take off.

12. _Say the opposite._  
   Turn on the **radio**, **Aziz**.  
   → Turn ______ the **radio**, **Aziz**.  
   key: off  
   why: The opposite of turn on is turn off.

13. _Say the opposite._  
   Please stand up and **write the date**, **Lola**.  
   → Please sit ______ and **write the date**, **Lola**.  
   key: down  
   why: The opposite of stand up is sit down.

14. _Say the opposite._  
   **Lola** gets on the bus at the **market**.  
   → **Lola** gets ______ the bus at the **market**.  
   key: off  
   why: The opposite of get on a bus is get off.

15. _Say the opposite._  
   Get in the **car**, **Timur**.  
   → Get ______ of the **car**, **Timur**.  
   key: out  
   why: The opposite of get in a car is get out of it.

## create (8)

1. Write one sentence about what time you wake up and get up on a school day.  
   must use: wake up / get up · exactly one sentence · must use wake up or get up · about you

2. Write one sentence about what you put on when it is cold.  
   must use: put on · exactly one sentence · must use put on · a clothes word

3. Write one sentence about what you take off when you come home.  
   must use: take off · exactly one sentence · must use take off · about you

4. Write one sentence about a person who looks after you or someone in your family.  
   must use: look after · exactly one sentence · must use look after · a family word

5. Write one sentence about something you often look for at home.  
   must use: look for · exactly one sentence · must use look for · about you

6. Write one sentence about where you or a person in your family grew up.  
   must use: grow up · exactly one sentence · must use grew up · a place

7. Write one question to a friend about going out at the weekend.  
   must use: go out · exactly one question · must use go out · must end with a question mark

8. Write two sentences for a younger brother or sister: one with turn off, one with put on.  
   must use: turn off / put on · exactly two sentences · turn off in one · put on in the other

## express (5)

1. Describe your morning, from waking up to leaving home.  
   target: wake up / get up / put on · look for: wake up or get up · put on · three or four sentences

2. Describe what you do in the evening when you come back home.  
   target: come back / take off / turn on · look for: come back · take off or turn on · three or four sentences

3. Write a message to a friend who wants to stop learning English. Tell them not to give up.  
   target: give up · look for: don't give up · a reason · three or four sentences

4. Tell a visitor some rules in your home: shoes, the TV, the lights.  
   target: take off / turn off · look for: take off · turn off · please / don't

5. Tell a friend about a person in your family who looks after you or helps you.  
   target: look after · look for: look after · what they do · three or four sentences
