## create (8)

1. Write one sentence about a city you want to go to.  
   must use: I want to go to … · exactly one sentence · go to · a city name

2. Write one sentence about where a relative lives.  
   must use: My … lives in … · exactly one sentence · lives in · a family word

3. Write one sentence about a trip: where you went and where you stayed.  
   must use: We went to … and stayed in … · exactly one sentence · to once · in once

4. Write one sentence about someone who moved to another city or country.  
   must use: … moved to … · exactly one sentence · moved to · a place name

5. Write one sentence about where you spend the summer holidays.  
   must use: We spend the summer in … · exactly one sentence · in · a place word

6. Write one question to a friend about a trip. Use go to.  
   must use: Are you going to … ? · exactly one question · go to · a place name

7. Write one sentence about where you would like to live one day.  
   must use: I'd like to live in … · exactly one sentence · live in · a city or country

8. Write two sentences: one about where you live and one about where you often go.  
   must use: I live in … / I often go to … · exactly two sentences · in once · to once
