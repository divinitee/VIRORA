## transform (15)

1. _Correct the mistake with a or the._  
   We met a boy and a girl at **our picnic**. A girl was **my friend**'s sister.  
   → We met a boy and a girl at **our picnic**. ______ girl was **my friend**'s sister.  
   key: The  
   why: The girl was mentioned before: The, not A.

2. _Correct the mistake with a or the._  
   I bought a shirt and a belt **last week**. Belt was **too long**.  
   → I bought a shirt and a belt **last week**. ______ belt was **too long**.  
   key: The  
   why: Singular noun needs an article: The belt.

## create (8)

1. Write two sentences about something you bought. Use a the first time and the the second time.  
   must use: I bought a … The … was … · exactly two sentences · a on first mention · the on second mention

2. Write two sentences about a person you saw on the way to school or work.  
   must use: I saw a … The … was … · exactly two sentences · a then the · a past verb

3. Write two sentences about an animal you know.  
   must use: My … has a … The … is … · exactly two sentences · a then the · an animal word

4. Write two sentences about a film or a book you liked.  
   must use: I watched / read a … The … was about … · exactly two sentences · a then the · about

5. Write two sentences about a place near your home.  
   must use: There is a … near my home. The … is … · exactly two sentences · There is a … · the on second mention

6. Write two sentences about a present you got.  
   must use: I got a … The … is … · exactly two sentences · a then the · an adjective

7. Write two sentences about two things on your desk, then say something about one of them.  
   must use: There's a … and a … The … is … · exactly two sentences · two things with a · one of them with the

8. Write two sentences about a meal you had in a café.  
   must use: I ordered a … The … was … · exactly two sentences · a then the · a food word

## express (5)

1. Tell a short story about something strange you saw in your street.  
   target: a … (first time) / the … (again) · look for: a on first mention · the for the same thing again · three or four sentences

2. Describe your room: name some things in it, then say more about two of them.  
   target: There is a … The … is … · look for: a for new things · the for things already named · three or four sentences

3. Describe a visit to the bazaar: what did you buy, and what was it like?  
   target: I bought a … The … was … · look for: a then the · past verbs · three or four sentences

4. Describe a new person in your class, group or job.  
   target: We have a new … The … is from … · look for: a then the · a place word · three or four sentences

5. Describe a trip: where did you stay, and what was it like?  
   target: We stayed in a … The … had … · look for: a then the · past verbs · three or four sentences
