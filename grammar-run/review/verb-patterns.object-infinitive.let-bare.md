## transform (15)

1. _Rewrite the sentence with let. Keep the same tense._  
   My parents allow me to stay out until **half past nine**.  
   → My parents ______ out until **half past nine**.  
   key: let me stay  
   why: allow + person + to + verb → let + person + base verb: let me stay.

2. _Rewrite the sentence with let. Keep the same tense._  
   **Aunt Lola** allows us to watch TV after **lunch**.  
   → **Aunt Lola** ______ TV after **lunch**.  
   key: lets us watch  
   why: allows us to watch → lets us watch (he / she + lets, then the base verb).

3. _Rewrite the sentence with let. Keep the same tense._  
   The **PE teacher** allowed him to go home. He felt **unwell**.  
   → The **PE teacher** ______ home. He felt **unwell**.  
   key: let him go  
   why: The past of let is let: allowed him to go → let him go.

4. _Rewrite the sentence with let. Keep the same tense._  
   Our neighbours allowed us to use their **ladder** **last summer**.  
   → Our neighbours ______ their **ladder** **last summer**.  
   key: let us use  
   why: The past of let is let: allowed us to use → let us use.

5. _Rewrite the sentence with let. Keep the same tense._  
   My sister never allows me to read her **messages**.  
   → My sister never ______ her **messages**.  
   key: lets me read  
   why: allows me to read → lets me read: no to after let.

6. _Correct the mistake._  
   **Uncle Bobur** lets me to drive his car in the **village**.  
   → **Uncle Bobur** lets me ______ his car in the **village**.  
   key: drive  
   why: After let + person there is no to: lets me drive.

7. _Correct the mistake._  
   My **grandparents** didn't let me going to the **match**.  
   → My **grandparents** didn't let me ______ to the **match**.  
   key: go  
   why: After let + person, use the base verb, not -ing: let me go.

8. _Correct the mistake._  
   The **PE teacher** let we play in the **big match**.  
   → The **PE teacher** ______ play in the **big match**.  
   key: let us  
   why: After let, use the object word us, not we: let us play.

9. _Correct the mistake._  
   Mum lets **Lola** watched cartoons in the **morning**.

   key: watch  
   why: After let + person, use the base verb, not the past form: lets … watch.

10. _Correct the mistake._
