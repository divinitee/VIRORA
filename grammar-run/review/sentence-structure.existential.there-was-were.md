## transform (15)

1. _Rewrite the sentence in the past._  
   There is a big **shop** in our **village** now.  
   → There ______ a big **shop** in our **village** ten years ago.  
   key: was  
   why: Ten years ago is the past, and a shop is one thing, so is becomes was.

2. _Rewrite the sentence in the past._  
   There are many cars on the **main road** near our **college** today.  
   → There ______ many cars on the **main road** near our **college** last night.  
   key: were  
   why: Last night is the past, and cars is plural, so are becomes were.

3. _Rewrite the sentence in the past._  
   There is no milk in the **café**.  
   → There ______ no milk in the **café** yesterday.  
   key: was  
   why: Yesterday is the past, and milk is uncountable, so is becomes was.

4. _Rewrite the sentence in the past._  
   There are a lot of tourists in the **bazaars** of **Tashkent** this summer.  
   → There ______ a lot of tourists in the **bazaars** of **Tashkent** last summer.  
   key: were  
   why: Last summer is the past, and tourists is plural, so are becomes were.

5. _Rewrite the sentence in the past._  
   There is a football match on TV tonight.  
   → There ______ a football match on TV last night, so my **uncle** stayed up late.  
   key: was  
   why: Last night is the past, and a match is one thing, so is becomes was.

6. _Make the sentence negative._  
   There were some free seats on the **minibus** this morning.  
   → There ______ any free seats on the **minibus** this morning.  
   key: weren't  
   why: Seats is plural, so the negative is there weren't, with any.

7. _Make the sentence negative._  
   There was some bread in the **cupboard** for **breakfast**.  
   → There ______ any bread in the **cupboard** for **breakfast**.  
   key: wasn't  
   why: Bread is uncountable, so the negative is there wasn't, with any.

8. _Make the sentence negative._  
   There were some good films at the cinema near our **school** in **Fergana** last week.  
   → There ______ any good films at the cinema near our **school** in **Fergana** last week.  
   key: weren't  
   why: Films is plural, so the negative is there weren't, with any.

9. _Make the sentence negative._  
   There was a doctor at the **school** in our **district** yesterday.  
   → There ______ a doctor at the **school** in our **district** yesterday.  
   key: wasn't  
   why: A doctor is one person, so the negative is there wasn't.

10. _Make the sentence negative._

   → There ______ many people at the **party** in **Bukhara** on Sunday.  
   key: weren't

   key: were

   key: there was

   Was a lot of traffic on the **bridge** near the **station** yesterday.  
   → ______ a lot of traffic on the **bridge** near the **station** yesterday.  
   key: There was

   There were a cat in our **yard** this morning. It had **brown** paws.
