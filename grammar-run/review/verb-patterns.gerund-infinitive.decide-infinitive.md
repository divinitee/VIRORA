## create (8)

1. Write one sentence about something you decided to do this week.  
   must use: decided to + verb · exactly one sentence · must be about you · must use decided to

2. Write one sentence about something you decided not to do.  
   must use: decided not to + verb · exactly one sentence · must use decided not to · must be about you

3. Write one sentence about what someone in your family decided to buy, sell or change.  
   must use: decided to + verb · exactly one sentence · must name a family member · must use decided to

4. Write one sentence about what you have decided to study or become.  
   must use: have decided to + verb · exactly one sentence · must use have decided to · must be about your future

5. Write a question to a friend: why did he or she decide to do something?  
   must use: Why did you decide to + verb · exactly one question · must use decide to · must end with a question mark

6. Write one sentence about what your class or your friends decided to do at the weekend.  
   must use: decided to + verb · exactly one sentence · must use decided to · must be about a group

7. Write two sentences: one thing you decided to do and one thing you decided not to do.  
   must use: decided to and decided not to · exactly two sentences · must use decided to · must use decided not to

8. A friend wrote: "We decided going by bus and not take a taxi." Write the sentence correctly.  
   must use: decided to go ... and not to take · exactly one sentence · must use decided to go · must use not to take

## express (5)

1. Tell me about a big decision you made this year. What did you decide to do, and why? Write three or four sentences.  
   target: decided to + verb · look for: decided to + base verb · no -ing after decided · a reason

2. What have you decided to do after school or university? Write three or four sentences.  
   target: have decided to + verb · look for: have decided to · to + base verb · a plan for the future

3. Describe a day when the weather changed your plans. What did you decide to do, and what did you decide not to do? Write three or four sentences.  
   target: decided to / decided not to · look for: decided to · decided not to · past tense for the story

4. Your family is planning a holiday. What did everyone decide? Write three or four sentences.  
   target: decided to + verb · look for: decided to go, stay or visit · to + base verb · at least two decisions

5. Why did you decide to learn English? What did you decide to do to learn it faster? Write three or four sentences.  
   target: decided to + verb · look for: decided to learn · another decided to · a reason
