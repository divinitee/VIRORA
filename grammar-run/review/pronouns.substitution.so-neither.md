## transform (15)

1. _Correct the answer._  
   **Aziz** says: 'I like **sweets**.' 'So am I.'  
   → **Aziz** says: 'I like **sweets**.' '______.'  
   key: So do I (also: Me too, I do too)  
   why: "I like": present simple, so do, not am.

2. _Correct the answer._  
   **Timur** says: 'I'm cold **outside**.' 'So do I.'  
   → **Timur** says: 'I'm cold **outside**.' '______.'  
   key: So am I (also: Me too, I am too)  
   why: "I'm cold": be, so am, not do.

3. _Correct the answer._  
   **Aziz** says: 'I can't **drive**.' 'Neither I can.'  
   → **Aziz** says: 'I can't **drive**.' '______.'  
   key: Neither can I (also: Nor can I, Me neither, I can't either)  
   why: Word order after Neither: verb, then I.

4. _Correct the answer._
