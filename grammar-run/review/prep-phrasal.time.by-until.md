# prep-phrasal.time.by-until — review sample (B1)

Each item shows one random filler combination (in bold). Mark anything a teacher wouldn't accept.

## choose (20)

1. Please send me the **photos** ______ **Thursday** at the latest, **Feruza**.  
   options: ✔ by · until · since · during  
   why: "at the latest" = a deadline: by.

2. We waited for the **bus** ______ **six o'clock**, but it didn't come.  
   options: ✔ until · since · during · by  
   why: Waiting continues up to that time: until.

3. I need to finish this **book** ______ **Monday**, **Sardor**.  
   options: since · during · ✔ by · until  
   why: finish happens once, before the deadline: by.

4. The **supermarket** is open ______ ten o'clock tonight, **Ulugbek**.  
   options: during · by · ✔ until · since  
   why: It stays open and then closes: until.

5. The guests will arrive ______ **six o'clock**, so let's start cooking now, **Gulnora**.  
   options: ✔ by · until · since · during  
   why: arrive happens at one point, not later than that: by.

6. My little brother slept ______ **noon** on Sunday, **Feruza**.  
   options: ✔ until · since · during · by  
   why: Sleeping continues and then stops: until.

7. Your passport will be ready ______ the end of **May**, **Akmal**.  
   options: since · during · ✔ by · until  
   why: be ready = the deadline for the job: by.

8. Let's stay at the **library** ______ the rain stops, **Feruza**.  
   options: during · by · ✔ until · since  
   why: We stay, and leave when the rain stops: until.

9. The homework must be on my desk ______ **Thursday** morning, **Nodira**.  
   options: ✔ by · until · since · during  
   why: A deadline for the teacher: by.

10. Grandpa worked at the **factory** ______ he was sixty-five, **Feruza**.  
   options: ✔ until · since · during · by  
   why: He kept working and then stopped: until.

11. Can you pay me back ______ the end of the **year**, **Gulnora**?  
   options: since · during · ✔ by · until  
   why: Pay back once, not later than that: by.

12. The road to **Chimgan** will be closed ______ **Tuesday**.  
   options: during · by · ✔ until · since  
   why: Closed the whole time, then open again: until.

13. If we leave now, we'll get to **Karshi** ______ midnight, **Feruza**.  
   options: ✔ by · until · since · during  
   why: get to happens at one point, not later than midnight: by.

14. We played **volleyball** in the yard ______ it got dark, **Sherzod**.  
   options: ✔ until · since · during · by  
   why: Playing continued and then stopped: until.

15. I'll have the **laptop** fixed ______ **six o'clock** tomorrow, **Feruza**.  
   options: since · during · ✔ by · until  
   why: Fixed at some point before that time: by.

16. You can borrow my **tent** ______ **Tuesday**, **Feruza**.  
   options: during · by · ✔ until · since  
   why: You keep it the whole time, then give it back: until.

17. Tickets for the **match** must be booked ______ **Tuesday**.  
   options: ✔ by · until · since · during  
   why: booked once, before the deadline: by.

18. The baby cried ______ **her sister** came home, **Feruza**.  
   options: ✔ until · since · during · by  
   why: Crying continued and stopped when mum came: until.

19. Most of the **cake** was gone ______ nine o'clock, **Nodira**.

## transform (15)

1. _Rewrite with by._  
   Please send the form no later than **Tuesday**, **Gulnora**.  
   → Please send the form ______ **Tuesday**, **Gulnora**.  
   key: by  
   why: no later than = by.

2. _Rewrite with by._  
   The **job** must be finished not later than **six o'clock**, **Malika**.  
   → The **job** must be finished ______ **six o'clock**, **Malika**.  
   key: by  
   why: not later than = by.

3. _Rewrite with by._  
   Be at the **station** at seven or a bit earlier, **Feruza**.  
   → Be at the **station** ______ seven, **Feruza**.  
   key: by  
   why: at seven or earlier = by seven.

4. _Rewrite with by._  
   The **bread** will be ready at six or before, **Nodira**.  
   → The **bread** will be ready ______ six, **Nodira**.  
   key: by  
   why: at six or before = by six.

5. _Rewrite with by._  
   Pay the bill not later than the 10th of **October**, **Malika**.  
   → Pay the bill ______ the 10th of **October**, **Malika**.  
   key: by

## express (5)
