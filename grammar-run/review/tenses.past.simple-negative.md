   must use: didn't + base form · exactly one sentence · must include yesterday · must use I

   must use: didn't go + because · exactly one sentence · must use because · must include last weekend

3. Write one sentence about food you didn't like as a child.  
   must use: didn't like · exactly one sentence · must use didn't like · must name the food

4. Write one sentence about a friend who didn't come to an event.  
   must use: didn't come · exactly one sentence · must name the friend · must name the event

5. Write one sentence about something you didn't understand in a lesson.  
   must use: didn't understand · exactly one sentence · must name the lesson · must include a past time

6. Write one sentence about two people who didn't have time for something.  
   must use: didn't have · exactly one sentence · must use a plural subject · must include a past time

8. Write two sentences: something you planned to do last week, and that you didn't do it.  
   must use: past simple, then didn't + base · exactly two sentences · second sentence negative · must include last week

## express (5)

1. Describe a day when nothing went right. Write three or four sentences.  
   target: past simple negative · look for: didn't + base form · at least two negatives · a finished time

2. Write about a trip where you didn't do what you planned. Write three or four sentences.  
   target: past simple positive and negative · look for: didn't + base form · what you did instead · a finished time

3. Explain to your teacher why you didn't do your homework. Write three or four sentences.  
   target: past simple negative with reasons · look for: didn't do · because · past simple verbs

4. Compare a good weekend and a bad one: what you did and what you didn't do. Write three or four sentences.  
   target: past simple positive and negative · look for: didn't + base form · but · last weekend or a date

5. Write about a time you were ill. What didn't you do that week? Write three or four sentences.  
   target: past simple negative · look for: didn't go, didn't eat, didn't sleep · a finished time · at least two negatives
