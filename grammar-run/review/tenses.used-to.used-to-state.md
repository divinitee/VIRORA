## transform (15)

1. _Rewrite the two sentences as one, with used to._  
   I lived in a small **village house** in **Samarkand** as a child. I don't live there now.  
   → As a child, I ______ in a small **village house** in **Samarkand**.  
   key: used to live  
   why: "I don't live there now": a past home → used to live.

2. _Rewrite the two sentences as one, with used to._  
   Ten years ago, my **uncle** had a **motorbike**. He doesn't have it now.  
   → Ten years ago, my **uncle** ______ a **motorbike**.  
   key: used to have  
   why: "He doesn't have it now": a past possession → used to have.

3. _Rewrite the two sentences as one, with used to._  
   This **building** was a **school**. It isn't a **school** now.  
   → This **building** ______ a **school**.  
   key: used to be  
   why: "It isn't … now": a past state → used to be.

4. _Rewrite the two sentences as one, with used to._  
   When she was young, **Kamola** liked **sport**. She doesn't like it now.  
   → When she was young, **Kamola** ______ **sport**.  
   key: used to like  
   why: "She doesn't like it now": a past liking → used to like.

5. _Rewrite the two sentences as one, with used to._  
   There was a big **park** near our **college**. It is not there now.  
   → There ______ a big **park** near our **college**.  
   key: used to be  
   why: "It is not there now": something that was there → there used to be.

   When I was little, I often played **football** in the **street**.

   why: "often" in the past is a habit → used to play.

## express (5)

1. Describe your life when you were ten. Where did you use to live, and what did you use to do every day? Write three or four sentences.  
   target: used to for past states and habits · look for: used to + base form · at least two different verbs · something that is different now

2. Describe how your town or neighbourhood has changed. What used to be there? Write three or four sentences.  
   target: there used to be · look for: there used to be · now for the present · a place named

3. Describe a person in your family: how they used to be and how they are now. Write three or four sentences.  
   target: used to be vs now · look for: used to be + adjective · present simple for now · he or she without -s after used to

4. Describe the games and hobbies you used to have as a child. Write three or four sentences.  
   target: used to for past habits · look for: used to play / used to go · no use to in positive sentences · a past time named

5. Describe your school days. What did you use to like and not like? Write three or four sentences.  
   target: used to for past likes · look for: used to like or used to love · no used to + -ing · school words
