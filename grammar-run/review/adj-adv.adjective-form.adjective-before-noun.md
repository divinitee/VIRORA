## transform (15)

1. _Correct the mistake: put the adjective in the right place._  
   **Bobur** lives in a house big near the **river**.  
   → **Bobur** lives in a ______ near the **river**.  
   key: big house  
   why: The adjective goes before the noun: a big house, not a house big.

2. _Correct the mistake: put the adjective in the right place._  
   We have a teacher nice for **maths**, **Dad**.  
   → We have a ______ for **maths**, **Dad**.  
   key: nice teacher  
   why: In English the adjective comes first: a nice teacher.

3. _Correct the mistake: put the adjective in the right place._  
   Take off your shoes dirty at the **door**, **Olga**.  
   → Take off your ______ at the **door**, **Olga**.  
   key: dirty shoes  
   why: The adjective goes before the noun: your dirty shoes.

4. _Correct the mistake: put the adjective in the right place._  
   There is a cat hungry in the **garden**. Give it some **bread**.

   why: The adjective goes before the noun: a hungry cat.

5. _Correct the mistake in the adjective._  
   **Aziz** has two bigs dogs in the **big yard**.

   key: big dogs

## express (5)

1. Describe your home to a new friend from another country.  
   target: adjective + noun · look for: a big / small / old house or flat · adjectives before nouns · no -s on adjectives

2. Tell a friend about your favourite place in your town.  
   target: adjective + noun · look for: a / an + adjective + noun · many + adjective + plural noun · three or four sentences

3. Describe the people in your family. Say one thing about each person.  
   target: adjective + noun · look for: a kind mother, a tall brother … · adjectives before nouns · is / are

4. Write a short message about a market or shop near your home. What can you buy there?  
   target: adjective + plural noun · look for: fresh, cheap, big … · plural nouns with -s · no -s on adjectives

5. Describe your school or your workplace for a new student.  
   target: adjective + noun · look for: a / an + adjective + noun · two or three adjectives · three or four sentences
