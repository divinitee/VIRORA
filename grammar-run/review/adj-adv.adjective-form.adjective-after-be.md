## transform (15)

1. _Correct the mistake._  
   You look tiredly, **Timur**. Sit down, **please**.  
   → You look ______, **Timur**. Sit down, **please**.  
   key: tired  
   why: When look means seem, use an adjective: tired, not tiredly.

2. _Correct the mistake._  
   The **plov** smells deliciously, **Grandma**.  
   → The **plov** smells ______, **Grandma**.  
   key: delicious  
   why: After smell, use an adjective: delicious, not deliciously.

3. _Correct the mistake._  
   Be carefully with the **knife**, **Olga**.  
   → Be ______ with the **knife**, **Olga**.  
   key: careful  
   why: After be, use an adjective: careful, not carefully.

4. _Correct the mistake._  
   **Madina** feels happily today. Her **cousin** is here.  
   → **Madina** feels ______ today. Her **cousin** is here.  
   key: happy  
   why: After feel, use an adjective: happy, not happily.

5. _Correct the mistake._  
   This **music** sounds strangely, **Lola**.  
   → This **music** sounds ______, **Lola**.  
   key: strange  
   why: After sound, use an adjective: strange, not strangely.
