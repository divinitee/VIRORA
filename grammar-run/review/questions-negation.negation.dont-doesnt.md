   target: doesn't + base verb · look for: He doesn't · She doesn't

3. Write about your town: what it has and what it doesn't have.  
   target: doesn't have · look for: doesn't have · It has

   target: don't / doesn't · look for: don't eat · doesn't eat

5. Write about your morning: what you do and what you don't do.  
   target: don't + base verb · look for: I don't · every morning
