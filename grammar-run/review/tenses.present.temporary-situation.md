## create (8)

1. Write one sentence about something you are doing this month that is different from usual.  
   must use: am + -ing with this month · exactly one sentence · must include this month · must use I

2. Write one sentence about where a family member is staying or living for a short time.  
   must use: is staying or is living · exactly one sentence · must name a family member · must say why it is temporary

3. Write one sentence about a course or lesson you are taking these days.  
   must use: am taking or am studying · exactly one sentence · must include these days · must name the course or subject

4. Write one sentence about a friend who has a summer job.  
   must use: is working · exactly one sentence · must include this summer · must name the place of work

5. Write one sentence about the weather this week.  
   must use: is raining, is snowing or is getting · exactly one sentence · must include this week · must use the present continuous

6. Write one sentence about something two or more people are doing this year.  
   must use: are + -ing · exactly one sentence · must include this year · must use a plural subject

7. Write one sentence about a book, series or game you are enjoying at the moment, but not at this second.  
   must use: am reading, am watching or am playing · exactly one sentence · must include at the moment · must not be happening at this second

8. Write two sentences: first your usual situation, then a temporary situation this month.  
   must use: present simple, then present continuous · exactly two sentences · first sentence present simple · second sentence with this month and -ing

## express (5)

1. Write to a friend you haven't seen for a long time. Tell them what is different in your life these days. Write three or four sentences.  
   target: present continuous for temporary situations · look for: these days or this month · am, is or are + -ing · reasons with because

2. Describe a family member who is doing something unusual this year. Write three or four sentences.  
   target: present continuous for a temporary situation · look for: is + -ing · this year · how it is different from usual

3. Your town or school is changing for a short time (repairs, a festival, exams). Describe what is happening this month. Write three or four sentences.  
   target: present continuous for temporary situations · look for: this month or at the moment · is or are + -ing · a sense that it will end

4. Describe a project or course you are working on at the moment. Write three or four sentences.  
   target: present continuous for situations around now · look for: at the moment or these days · am + -ing · what you do for it each week

5. Compare your usual life with your life this summer or this term. Write three or four sentences.  
   target: present simple for the usual, present continuous for the temporary · look for: usually + present simple · this summer or this term + -ing · but to show the contrast
