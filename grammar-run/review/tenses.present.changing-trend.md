## transform (15)

1. _Correct the mistake. Use the present continuous._  
   Prices at the **corner shop** is going up these days, **Dilnoza**.  
   → Prices at the **corner shop** ______ up these days, **Dilnoza**.  
   key: are going  
   why: Prices is plural, so are, not is.

2. _Correct the mistake. Use the present continuous._  
   Our town growing very fast, **Nodira**. Look at all the **new shops**.  
   → Our town ______ very fast, **Nodira**. Look at all the **new shops**.  
   key: is growing  
   why: be is missing: is growing.

3. _Correct the mistake. Use the present continuous._  
   More people are use bikes in our **town** now, **Nodira**.  
   → More people ______ bikes in our **town** now, **Nodira**.  
   key: are using  
   why: After are, the -ing form: using.

4. _Correct the mistake. Use the present continuous._  
   My **handwriting** get better and better, **Gulnora**.  
   → My **handwriting** ______ better and better, **Gulnora**.  
   key: is getting  
   why: A change in progress: is getting, not get.

5. _Correct the mistake. Use the present continuous._  
   The days are get longer now that it's **April**, **Jamshid**.  
   → The days ______ longer now that it's **April**, **Jamshid**.  
   key: are getting  
   why: are + -ing, never are + base: are getting.

6. _Say what is changing now. Use the verb in brackets in the present continuous._

   key: are buying

7. _Say what is changing now. Use the verb in brackets in the present continuous._

   key: is becoming

8. _Say what is changing now. Use the verb in brackets in the present continuous._  
   In **August** it was hot. In **early autumn** it is cooler, **Nodira**.  
   → The weather ______ (get) cooler, **Nodira**.  
   key: is getting

9. _Say what is changing now. Use the verb in brackets in the present continuous._

   → Our club ______ (grow), **Dilnoza**.  
   key: is growing  
   why: More members than last year: is growing.

   → The **market street** ______ ______ (busy), **Jamshid**.  
   key: is getting + busier and busier

   → The **bookshop** ______ ______ popular, **Dilnoza**.  
   key: is becoming + more and more

13. _Say it with the present continuous and a double comparative (-er and -er / more and more)._  
   My **laptop** was fast. Now it is slower every week, **Gulnora**.  
   → My **laptop** ______ ______ (slow), **Gulnora**.  
   key: is getting + slower and slower

   → Flights to the **capital** ______ ______ expensive, **Akmal**.  
   key: are getting + more and more (also: are becoming,more and more)  
   why: Plural flights: are getting more and more expensive.

15. _Say it with the present continuous and a double comparative (-er and -er / more and more)._  
   My grandpa's walks were long. Every week they are shorter, **Sardor**. He is **seventy-nine** now.

   key: are getting + shorter and shorter  
   why: Plural walks: are getting shorter and shorter.
