## create (8)

1. Write one sentence about a lesson or class you have on one day of the week.  
   must use: on + day · exactly one sentence · must use on + a day · about you

2. Write one sentence about what your family does on Sundays.  
   must use: on Sundays · exactly one sentence · must use on Sundays · about your family

3. Invite a friend to do something with you. Say the day.  
   must use: Let's … on … · exactly one sentence · must use on + a day · an activity

4. Write one question to a friend about what they do on Saturday.  
   must use: What do you do on …? · exactly one question · must use on + a day · must end with a question mark

5. Write one sentence about the day you like best and why.  
   must use: on + day · exactly one sentence · must use on + a day · must say why

6. Write one sentence about a shop, market or place in your town that is closed on one day.  
   must use: closed on + day · exactly one sentence · must use on + a day · a place in your town

7. Write one sentence about something you do on a day and a part of it, like Friday evening.  
   must use: on + day + morning / afternoon / evening · exactly one sentence · on + day + part of the day · about you

8. Write two sentences about two different days of your week.  
   must use: on + day (×2) · exactly two sentences · on in both sentences · two different days

## express (5)

1. Describe your week. Say what you do on three or four different days.  
   target: on + days · look for: on Monday … · on + three or four days · I have / I go / I play

2. A new classmate asks about your timetable. Tell them which lessons you have on which days.  
   target: on + days · look for: We have … on … · on + days · lesson names

3. Write a message to a friend and plan a trip for next weekend. Say the day and what you will do.  
   target: on Saturday / on Sunday · look for: Let's go … on … · a place · on + a day

4. Tell a friend about your family's weekend: what happens on Saturday and on Sunday.  
   target: on Saturday / on Sunday · look for: on Saturday … · on Sunday … · family words

5. Write about your favourite day of the week. What do you do on that day, and why do you like it?  
   target: on + day · look for: on + a day · because … · three or four sentences
