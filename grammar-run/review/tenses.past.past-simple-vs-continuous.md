   When I ______ (get) home, my brother ______ (cook) dinner, so I joined him, **Nodira**. We made **soup**.  
   key: got + was cooking  
   why: Arrival = event; cooking still going on (so I joined him).

   key: was walking + started  
   why: Walk in progress, rain began once.

   key: was having + phoned

   must use: while + past continuous, past simple · exactly one sentence · while · past simple

   must use: When I arrived, … were + -ing · exactly one sentence · When I arrived · were + -ing

   must use: it was + -ing when … · exactly one sentence · It was · when

5. Write one sentence where a sound interrupts someone sleeping.  
   must use: was sleeping when … heard / woke · exactly one sentence · was sleeping · past simple

6. Write one sentence about meeting someone while you were travelling.  
   must use: past simple event + while + past continuous · exactly one sentence · met · while

   must use: two past continuous clauses · exactly one sentence · while · was / were + -ing

## express (5)

1. Tell a short story about a day when something went wrong. Write three or four sentences.  
   target: past continuous background + past simple events · look for: was / were + -ing · when / while · three or four sentences

2. Describe what everyone in your family was doing when you got home yesterday.  
   target: past continuous for a scene · look for: were + -ing · When I got home · three or four sentences

3. Tell a friend how you met someone important in your life.  
   target: past simple event against a background · look for: was / were + -ing · met · three or four sentences

4. Write about a time the lights went out or the internet stopped. What were people doing?  
   target: background + sudden event · look for: were + -ing · went out / stopped · three or four sentences

5. Describe a funny moment from a holiday or a trip.  
   target: past continuous + past simple in a narrative · look for: while / when · past simple · three or four sentences
