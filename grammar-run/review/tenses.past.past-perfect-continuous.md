## transform (15)

1. _Join the facts. Use the verb in brackets in the past perfect continuous._  
   She started waiting at three. The **tram** came at five, **Dilnoza**.  
   → She ______ (wait) for two hours when the **tram** came, **Dilnoza**.  
   key: had been waiting  
   why: From three to five, up to a past moment: had been waiting.

2. _Join the facts. Use the verb in brackets in the past perfect continuous._  
   He started studying at eight. At midnight he was very tired, **Nodira**. The **maths exam** was the next day.  
   → He was tired because he ______ (study) since eight, **Nodira**. The **maths exam** was the next day.  
   key: had been studying  
   why: Eight to midnight explains the tiredness: had been studying.

3. _Join the facts. Use the verb in brackets in the past perfect continuous._  
   It started raining on Monday. On Wednesday the **river** was very high, **Nodira**.  
   → The **river** was very high because it ______ (rain) since Monday, **Nodira**.  
   key: had been raining  
   why: Monday to Wednesday, rain explains the high water.

4. _Join the facts. Use the verb in brackets in the past perfect continuous._  
   They began building the **stadium** in 2019. In 2023 it was still not ready, **Gulnora**.  
   → In 2023 they ______ (build) the **stadium** for four years, **Gulnora**.  
   key: had been building  
   why: Four years of building up to 2023: had been building.

5. _Join the facts. Use the verb in brackets in the past perfect continuous._  
   I started reading at six. My **friend** called at nine, **Jamshid**.  
   → I ______ (read) for three hours when my **friend** called, **Jamshid**.  
   key: had been reading  
   why: Six to nine, until the call: had been reading.

   → When I arrived, they ______ for an hour at the **café**, **Nodira**.  
   key: had been waiting  
   why: A past story needs had, not has: had been waiting.

   → She was tired because she ______ in the **office** all day, **Nodira**.  
   key: had been working  
   why: After had been, the -ing form: working.

8. _Correct the mistake. The story is in the past._

9. _Correct the mistake. The story is in the past._

   → We ______ for an hour when the **teacher** arrived, **Jamshid**.  
   key: had been talking

   It had been snowed all night, so the **yard** was white, **Nodira**.  
   → It ______ all night, so the **yard** was white, **Nodira**.  
   key: had been snowing  
   why: Duration of an activity: had been snowing, not snowed.

11. _Make the question. Use had … been + -ing._  
   You were tired. Ask how long you had worked that day, **Gulnora**. It was at the **shop**.  
   → How long ______ you ______ at the **shop** that day, **Gulnora**?  
   key: had + been working  
   why: Question order: had + subject + been + -ing.

12. _Make the question. Use had … been + -ing._

   → ______ it ______ in the **afternoon**, **Jamshid**?  
   key: Had + been raining

## express (5)

1. Tell the story of a long wait (at a station, a clinic or an office). Write three or four sentences.  
   target: past perfect continuous for duration · look for: had been waiting · when · three or four sentences

2. You came home very tired one day. Explain why: what had you been doing?  
   target: had been + -ing as a reason · look for: because · had been · three or four sentences

3. Describe a big change in your family's life and what you had been doing before it.  
   target: had been + -ing before a past event · look for: had been · before · three or four sentences

4. Write a short story that starts: 'When the lights went out, we had been …'  
   target: past perfect continuous in a narrative · look for: had been · past simple events · three or four sentences

5. Tell a friend about a skill you had been practising for a long time before a test or show.  
   target: had been practising / learning · look for: had been · for / since · three or four sentences
