   Washes the **apples**, please, **Aziz**.  
   → ______ the **apples**, please, **Aziz**.  
   key: wash  
   why: An order uses the base verb, with no -s: wash.

2. _Correct the mistake._  
   To sit down, please, **Olga**. The **lesson** is starting.  
   → ______ down, please, **Olga**. The **lesson** is starting.  
   key: sit  
   why: An order uses the base verb, with no to: sit.

3. _Correct the mistake._  
   Being careful on the **stairs**, **Olga**.

   key: be

   must use: cut / put / mix + … · exactly one sentence · start with the verb · a food word

5. Write one instruction a teacher gives in your English class.  
   must use: open / write / listen + … · exactly one sentence · start with the verb · a classroom word

## express (5)

1. Explain to a friend how to cook a simple dish from your home: three or four steps.  
   target: imperatives · look for: start each step with a verb · first / then · three or four sentences

2. Tell a new student the way from the school gate to your classroom.  
   target: imperatives · look for: go / turn / walk · a place word · three or four sentences

3. Write the rules for a guest in your home: shoes, food, the pet.  
   target: imperatives · look for: start with a verb · please · three or four sentences

4. Give a friend advice for a big test tomorrow.  
   target: imperatives · look for: start with a verb · be + adjective once · three or four sentences

5. Write a message to a friend who is coming to your town for the first time. What should they do?  
   target: imperatives · look for: visit / try / go · a place in your town · three or four sentences
