## create (8)

1. Write one sentence about something your mother or father can do well.  
   must use: He can … / She can … · exactly one sentence · he or she + can + verb · no -s and no to

2. Write one sentence about a friend and a sport he or she can play.  
   must use: can play · exactly one sentence · a friend's name + can play · no -s and no to

3. Write one sentence about a language a person in your family can speak.  
   must use: can speak · exactly one sentence · a family word + can speak · no -s and no to

4. Write one sentence about something your grandmother or grandfather can make.  
   must use: can make · exactly one sentence · he or she + can make · no -s and no to

5. Write one sentence about a little child you know and what he or she can do now.  
   must use: can + verb · exactly one sentence · he or she + can · no -s and no to

6. Write one sentence about a teacher and something he or she can do well.  
   must use: can + verb · exactly one sentence · a teacher + can · no -s and no to

7. Write one sentence about a famous person you like and what he or she can do.  
   must use: can + verb · exactly one sentence · he or she + can · no -s and no to

8. Write two sentences: one thing your brother or sister can do, one thing your friend can do.  
   must use: can + verb · exactly two sentences · he or she + can in both · no -s and no to

## express (5)

1. Tell a new friend about a person in your family and three things he or she can do.  
   target: he / she can + verb · look for: he can / she can · three different verbs · no -s and no to

2. Describe your best friend. What can he or she do well?  
   target: he / she can + verb · look for: he can / she can · well / very well · three or four sentences

3. Write about a grandparent. What can he or she make or do with his or her hands?  
   target: can make / can do · look for: can make or can sew or can fix · he or she · three or four sentences

4. Tell a visitor about a teacher at your school and what he or she can do.  
   target: he / she can + verb · look for: he can / she can · no -s and no to · three or four sentences

5. Write about a little child you know. What can he or she do now?  
   target: he / she can + verb · look for: he can / she can · now · three or four sentences
