# pronouns.substitution.the-other-others — review sample (B1)

Each item shows one random filler combination (in bold). Mark anything a teacher wouldn't accept.

## choose (20)

1. I have two brothers. One lives in **Bukhara** and ______ lives in **Nukus**.  
   options: ✔ the other · another · the others · other  
   why: Two brothers: one …, the second one: the other.

2. I've got two **cats**. One is black and ______ is white, **Aziz**.  
   options: another · the others · other · ✔ the other  
   why: Two pets: one black, the last one: the other.

3. There are two shops on our street. One is open, but ______ one is closed, **Olga**.  
   options: the others · other · ✔ the other · another  
   why: Two shops: one open, the second one: the other.

4. I can only find one **glove**. Where's ______ one, **Aziz**?  
   options: other · ✔ the other · another · the others  
   why: A pair: one found, the missing one: the other.

5. Hold the bag with one hand and open the **box** with ______, **Timur**.  
   options: ✔ the other · another · the others · other  
   why: Two hands: one holds, the second one: the other.

6. Our flat has two bedrooms. I sleep in this one, and my **brother** sleeps in ______, **Olga**.  
   options: another · the others · other · ✔ the other  
   why: Two bedrooms: this one and the second one: the other.

7. Two **guests** came to the **meeting**. One was my cousin; I didn't know ______.  
   options: the others · other · ✔ the other · another  
   why: Two people: one cousin, the second one: the other.

8. This road is closed. Let's cross to ______ side of the **river**, **Olga**.  
   options: other · ✔ the other · another · the others  
   why: A road has two sides: the opposite one: the other.

9. One of my parents is a **doctor**; ______ is a **builder**.  
   options: ✔ the other · another · the others · other  
   why: Two parents: one …, the second one: the other.

10. There are two cafés near the **station**. One is cheap, and ______ is very expensive.  
   options: another · the others · other · ✔ the other  
   why: Two cafés: one cheap, the second one: the other.

11. Where are ______, **Timur**? Only you are at the **station**.  
   options: the other · another · ✔ the others · others  
   why: "Only you are here": all the rest of the group: the others.

12. There were five **apples**. I ate one, and my brother ate all ______, **Aziz**.  
   options: another · ✔ the others · others · the other  
   why: Five, I ate one: all the rest: the others.

13. I kept one **card** and gave all ______ to my sister, **Lola**.  
   options: ✔ the others · others · the other · another  
   why: I kept one: all the rest: the others.

14. I have six **bags**. This one is new, but all ______ are old, **Olga**.  
   options: others · the other · another · ✔ the others  
   why: Six, this one is new: all the rest: the others.

15. Ten students took the **driving test**. Three failed, but all ______ passed, **Aziz**.  
   options: the other · another · ✔ the others · others  
   why: Three failed: all the rest of the ten: the others.

16. Only two of us are here. Let's wait for all ______ before we start the **film**, **Timur**.  
   options: another · ✔ the others · others · the other  
   why: Two of us here: all the rest: the others.

17. Only one of my friends came on time; all ______ were late for the **match**.  
   options: ✔ the others · others · the other · another  
   why: One came on time: all the rest of my friends: the others.

18. Our team was ready, but ______ teams were late for the **race**, **Aziz**.  
   options: the others · others · another · ✔ the other  
   why: Plural noun teams after it: the other, no -s.

19. **Lola** sits at the front of the **hall**, and ______ students sit at the back.  
   options: others · another · ✔ the other · the others  
   why: Plural noun students after it: the other, no -s.

20. Two of the **chairs** are broken, but ______ ones are fine, **Timur**.  
   options: another · ✔ the other · the others · others  
   why: Plural noun ones after it: the other.

## transform (15)

1. _Correct the mistake._  
   I have two sisters, **Timur**. One is a **driver** and another is a student.  
   → I have two sisters, **Timur**. One is a **driver** and the ______ is a student.  
   key: other  
   why: The second of two: the other, not another.

2. _Correct the mistake._  
   Where are others? Only **Timur** is at the **park**.  
   → Where are the ______? Only **Timur** is at the **park**.  
   key: others  
   why: No noun after it: the others, with the.

3. _Correct the mistake._  
   I lost one **glove**. Have you seen the another one, **Olga**?  
   → I lost one **glove**. Have you seen the ______ one, **Olga**?  
   key: other  
   why: After the: other, not another.

4. _Correct the mistake._  
   This bag on the **table** is mine; the others bags are yours, **Aziz**.  
   → This bag on the **table** is mine; the ______ bags are yours, **Aziz**.  
   key: other  
   why: Before a noun: the other bags, no -s.

5. _Correct the mistake._  
   Only **Olga** is ready. The other are still eating **breakfast**.  
   → Only **Olga** is ready. The ______ are still eating **breakfast**.  
   key: others  
   why: No noun after it: the others, with -s.

6. _Rewrite with the other._  
   I have two **rabbits**. One is black and the second one is white, **Olga**.  
   → I have two **rabbits**. One is black and the ______ is white, **Olga**.  
   key: other  
   why: The second of two = the other.

7. _Rewrite with the other._  
   There are two doors in the **shop**. Please use the second door, **Aziz**.  
   → There are two doors in the **shop**. Please use the ______ door, **Aziz**.  
   key: other  
   why: The second of two doors = the other door.

8. _Rewrite with the other._  
   We have two cars. Dad drives one, and Mum drives the second one **on Sundays**, **Timur**.  
   → We have two cars. Dad drives one, and Mum drives the ______ one **on Sundays**, **Timur**.  
   key: other  
   why: The second of two cars = the other.

9. _Rewrite with the other._  
   He has two phones: one for work and the second for **family**, **Aziz**.  
   → He has two phones: one for work and the ______ for **family**, **Aziz**.  
   key: other  
   why: The second of two phones = the other.

10. _Rewrite with the other._  
   Two girls came to the **wedding**. One was **Lola**; the second was her friend.  
   → Two girls came to the **wedding**. One was **Lola**; the ______ was her friend.  
   key: other  
   why: The second of two girls = the other.

11. _Rewrite with the others._  
   Five friends came to the **picnic**. **Timur** stayed, and the rest went home.  
   → Five friends came to the **picnic**. **Timur** stayed, and the ______ went home.  
   key: others  
   why: "the rest went home" = the others went home.

12. _Rewrite with the others._  
   I've got one **ticket**. Where are the rest, **Aziz**?  
   → I've got one **ticket**. Where are the ______, **Aziz**?  
   key: others  
   why: "Where are the rest?" = Where are the others?

13. _Rewrite with the others._  
   Ten people came. Three ate **manti**; the rest ate soup, **Lola**.  
   → Ten people came. Three ate **manti**; the ______ ate soup, **Lola**.  
   key: others  
   why: "the rest ate soup" = the others ate soup.

14. _Rewrite with the others._  
   This photo of **Bukhara** is good. The rest are dark, **Aziz**.  
   → This photo of **Bukhara** is good. The ______ are dark, **Aziz**.  
   key: others

15. _Rewrite with the others._

## create (8)

1. Write one sentence about two people in your family: one … and the other …  
   must use: One of my … and the other … · exactly one sentence · one … the other · two people only

2. Write one sentence about a pair of things (shoes, gloves, earrings) where one is missing.  
   must use: I can find one …, but not the other. · exactly one sentence · the other · a pair

3. Write one sentence about a group where one person is different from the rest.  
   must use: … is …, but the others … · exactly one sentence · the others · no noun after the others

4. Write one sentence about two places you have lived or visited.  
   must use: One was … and the other was … · exactly one sentence · the other · past verbs

5. Write one sentence about your class, with the other + a plural noun.  
   must use: I sit with …, and the other students … · exactly one sentence · the other + plural noun · no the others + noun

6. Write one sentence about your hands or eyes.  
   must use: I use one hand for … and the other for … · exactly one sentence · the other · no another

7. Write one sentence about friends who were late.  
   must use: … came on time, but the others were late. · exactly one sentence · the others · a past verb

8. Write two sentences: one with the other and one with the others.  
   must use: I have two … The other … / … the others … · exactly two sentences · the other for one of two · the others for the rest

## express (5)

1. Describe two friends who are very different.  
   target: I have two best friends. One … The other … · look for: the other · present verbs · three or four sentences

2. Describe your family: one person, then the others.  
   target: My dad is … The others … · look for: the others · the other + plural · three or four sentences

3. Write about a team or group you were part of.  
   target: One player … The other players … The others … · look for: the other + plural · the others · three or four sentences

4. Describe two jobs or two subjects you are choosing between.  
   target: One is … The other is … I like … · look for: one … the other · comparisons · three or four sentences

5. Write about a day when only one person in a group came on time.  
   target: Only … came. The others … The other students … · look for: the others · past verbs · three or four sentences
