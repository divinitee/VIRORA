# sentence-structure.relative-clauses.object-relative-omission — review sample (B1)

Each item shows one random filler combination (in bold). Mark anything a teacher wouldn't accept.

## choose (20)

1. Which option is NOT possible here? The **phone** ______ I bought **on Friday** is great, **Aziz**.  
   options: ✔ where · that · which · (nothing)  
   why: A thing I bought: that, which or nothing work; where is for places.

2. One option is wrong. Which one? The **film** ______ we watched last night was very long, **Olga**.  
   options: that · which · (nothing) · ✔ what  
   why: A film we watched: that, which or nothing work; never what after a noun.

3. Which word can't go in the gap? The **girl** ______ we met at the **party** was very kind.  
   options: that · (nothing) · ✔ which · who  
   why: A person we met: who, that or nothing work; which is for things.

4. Three options work. Find the one that doesn't. The **teapot** ______ my grandmother gave me is very old, **Timur**.  
   options: (nothing) · ✔ where · that · which  
   why: Something Grandma gave me: that, which or nothing; where is for places.

5. Pick the option that is NOT correct. The **plumber** ______ I called yesterday didn't answer, **Olga**.  
   options: ✔ which · who · that · (nothing)  
   why: A person I called: who, that or nothing; which is for things.

6. Only one option is wrong. Choose it. The **pasta** ______ Dad cooked tonight was delicious, **Timur**.  
   options: that · which · (nothing) · ✔ what

13. I love the **watch** ______, **Lola**.

## transform (15)

1. _Leave out the relative word._  
   The **match** that we watched was boring, **Timur**.  
   → The **match** ______ was boring, **Timur**.  
   key: we watched  
   why: that is the object (we watched it): drop it.

2. _Leave out the relative word._  
   The **girl** who I met at the **wedding** was **interesting**.  
   → The **girl** ______ at the **wedding** was **interesting**.  
   key: I met  
   why: who is the object (I met them): drop it.

3. _Leave out the relative word._  
   Is this the **bag** which you bought **last night**, **Timur**?  
   → Is this the **bag** ______ **last night**, **Timur**?  
   key: you bought  
   why: which is the object (you bought it): drop it.

4. _Leave out the relative word._  
   The **soup** that Grandma cooks is the best, **Aziz**.  
   → The **soup** ______ is the best, **Aziz**.  
   key: Grandma cooks  
   why: that is the object (Grandma cooks it): drop it.

5. _Leave out the relative word._  
   The people that we visited in **Termez** were very kind, **Olga**.  
   → The people ______ in **Termez** were very kind, **Olga**.  
   key: we visited  
   why: that is the object (we visited them): drop it.

6. _Join the two sentences. Leave out the relative word._  
   I bought a **bag** **on Friday**. It was cheap.  
   → The **bag** ______ **on Friday** was cheap.  
   key: I bought  
   why: Join the sentences: The … I bought.

7. _Join the two sentences. Leave out the relative word._  
   You recommended a **film**. I loved it, **Aziz**.  
   → I loved the **film** ______, **Aziz**.  
   key: you recommended  
   why: Join the sentences: the … you recommended.

8. _Join the two sentences. Leave out the relative word._  
   We met a **student** on the train. She was from **Termez**.  
   → The **student** ______ on the train was from **Termez**.  
   key: we met  
   why: Join the sentences: The … we met.

9. _Join the two sentences. Leave out the relative word._  
   My friend took some **pictures**. They are beautiful, **Aziz**.  
   → The **pictures** ______ are beautiful, **Aziz**.  
   key: my friend took  
   why: Join the sentences: The … my friend took.

10. _Join the two sentences. Leave out the relative word._  
   Dad told a **story**. Everyone laughed at it, **Timur**.  
   → Everyone laughed at the **story** ______, **Timur**.  
   key: Dad told  
   why: Join the sentences: the … Dad told.

11. _Correct the mistake._  
   The **jacket** I bought it **on Friday** was **great**.  
   → The **jacket** ______ **on Friday** was **great**.  
   key: I bought (also: that I bought, which I bought)  
   why: Don't repeat the object: I bought, not I bought it.

12. _Correct the mistake._  
   The **man** we met him at the **meeting** is a doctor.  
   → The **man** ______ at the **meeting** is a doctor.  
   key: we met (also: who we met, that we met)  
   why: Don't repeat the object: we met, not we met him.

13. _Correct the mistake._  
   The museum what we visited in **Samarkand** was closed, **Aziz**.  
   → The museum ______ in **Samarkand** was closed, **Aziz**.  
   key: we visited (also: that we visited, which we visited)  
   why: After a noun, never what: we visited.

14. _Correct the mistake._  
   The woman lives next door is a **doctor**, **Aziz**.  
   → The woman ______ next door is a **doctor**, **Aziz**.  
   key: who lives (also: that lives)  
   why: The woman is the subject: who lives can't be dropped.

## create (8)

1. Write one sentence about a film you saw. Leave out the relative word.  
   must use: The film I saw … was … · exactly one sentence · noun + subject + verb, no that / which · no it in the clause

2. Write one sentence about a present someone gave you. Leave out the relative word.  
   must use: The … my … gave me is … · exactly one sentence · no relative word · no it in the clause

3. Write one sentence about a person you met recently. Leave out the relative word.  
   must use: The … I met … was … · exactly one sentence · no who / that · no him / her in the clause

4. Write one sentence about food someone in your family cooks.  
   must use: The … my mum cooks is … · exactly one sentence · no relative word · present simple

5. Write one sentence about a neighbour, using who as the subject of the clause.  
   must use: The man who lives … is … · exactly one sentence · who + verb · who can't be left out here

6. Write one sentence about a place you visited. Leave out the relative word.  
   must use: The … we visited in … was … · exactly one sentence · no where · no it in the clause

7. Write one sentence about something you lost.  
   must use: The … I lost … was … · exactly one sentence · no relative word · a past verb

8. Write two sentences: one where the relative word is left out and one where it must stay.  
   must use: The book I read … / The girl who sits … · exactly two sentences · one object clause with no relative word · one subject clause with who or which

## express (5)

1. Describe the best trip you have had: the places you saw and the food you ate.  
   target: The food we ate in … was … The people we met … · look for: relative clauses with no relative word · no extra it / them · three or four sentences

2. Describe some presents you got for your last birthday.  
   target: The … my sister gave me is … The … I liked most … · look for: relative clauses with no relative word · past verbs · three or four sentences

3. Write a short review of a book or film you liked.  
   target: The film I watched … The actor who plays … · look for: object clauses with no relative word · one subject clause with who or which · three or four sentences

4. Describe the people you live near.  
   target: The man who lives next door … The woman I see every morning … · look for: who for subjects · no relative word for objects · three or four sentences

5. Write a message to a friend about things you bought in a new shop.  
   target: The jacket I bought … The shop which opened … · look for: object clauses with no relative word · which or that for subjects · three or four sentences
