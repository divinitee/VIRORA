## express (5)

1. Describe your last birthday. Write three or four sentences.  
   target: past simple with finished times · look for: last year or a date · past simple verbs · no present perfect with a finished time

   target: past simple with finished times · look for: in + year or ago · past simple verbs · what you did and felt

   target: past simple with finished times · look for: on Saturday, on Sunday · past simple verbs · different activities

   target: past simple with finished times · look for: in + year · moved, started, opened, met · past simple throughout
