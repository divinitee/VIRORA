## transform (15)

1. _The two facts are the same. Rewrite them as one sentence with as + the adjective in brackets + as._  
   Aziz is 170 cm and **Jasur** is 170 cm too. They play **handball**.  
   → Aziz is ______ (tall) **Jasur**. They play **handball**.  
   key: as tall as  
   why: Both are 170 cm, so they are equal: as tall as.

2. _The two facts are the same. Rewrite them as one sentence with as + the adjective in brackets + as._  
   For the trip to **Moscow**, my bag is 5 kilos and your **suitcase** is 5 kilos too.  
   → For the trip to **Moscow**, my bag is ______ (heavy) your **suitcase**.  
   key: as heavy as  
   why: Both weigh 5 kilos, so they are equal: as heavy as.

3. _The two facts are the same. Rewrite them as one sentence with as + the adjective in brackets + as._  
   In **Tashkent**, it was 35 degrees yesterday and it is 35 degrees today.  
   → In **Tashkent**, today is ______ (hot) yesterday.  
   key: as hot as  
   why: The temperature is the same, so today is as hot as yesterday.

4. _The two facts are the same. Rewrite them as one sentence with as + the adjective in brackets + as._
