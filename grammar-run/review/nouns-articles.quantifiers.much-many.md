## transform (15)

1. _Use the new noun. Write much or many._  
   I don't have many coins in my **wallet**, **Aziz**.  
   → I don't have ______ money in my **wallet**, **Aziz**.  
   key: much  
   why: Money is uncountable (coins are countable), so use much.

2. _Use the new noun. Write much or many._  
   There isn't much bread in the **bag**, **Olga**.  
   → There aren't ______ buns in the **bag**, **Olga**.  
   key: many  
   why: Buns are countable and plural (bread is not), so use many.

3. _Use the new noun. Write much or many._  
   **Aziz** doesn't have much homework in **English**.  
   → **Aziz** doesn't have ______ exercises in **English**.  
   key: many  
   why: Exercises are countable and plural (homework is not), so use many.

4. _Use the new noun. Write much or many._  
   We didn't buy many apples at the **shop**, **Timur**.  
   → We didn't buy ______ fruit at the **shop**, **Timur**.  
   key: much  
   why: Fruit here is uncountable (apples are countable), so use much.

5. _Use the new noun. Write much or many._  
   There aren't many cars in **Bukhara** in the **summer**.  
   → There isn't ______ traffic in **Bukhara** in the **summer**.  
   key: much  
   why: Traffic is uncountable (cars are countable), so use much.

6. _Make a question with How. Write much or many._  
   **Aziz** has three brothers in **Bukhara**.  
   → How ______ brothers does **Aziz** have in **Bukhara**?  
   key: many  
   why: Brothers are countable and plural, so How many.

7. _Make a question with How. Write much or many._  
   The ticket to **Fergana** costs fifty thousand som, **Olga**.  
   → How ______ does the ticket to **Fergana** cost, **Olga**?  
   key: much  
   why: We ask about money (uncountable), so How much.

8. _Make a question with How. Write much or many._  
   We need two kilos of rice for the **plov**, **Malika**.  
   → How ______ rice do we need for the **plov**, **Malika**?  
   key: much  
   why: Rice is uncountable, so How much.

9. _Make a question with How. Write much or many._  
   There are twenty students in the **group**, **Malika**.  
   → How ______ students are there in the **group**, **Malika**?  
   key: many  
   why: Students are countable and plural, so How many.

10. _Make a question with How. Write much or many._  
   I drink two glasses of water every **evening**, **Olga**.  
   → How ______ water do you drink every **evening**, **Olga**?  
   key: much  
   why: Water is uncountable, so How much.

11. _Make the sentence negative. Write much or many in each gap._  
   We have a lot of eggs and a lot of milk for the **cake**, **Malika**.  
   → We don't have ______ eggs or ______ milk for the **cake**, **Malika**.  
   key: many + much  
   why: Eggs are countable plural → many; milk is uncountable → much.

12. _Make the sentence negative. Write much or many in each gap._  
   **Malika** has a lot of time and a lot of friends in **Bukhara**.  
   → **Malika** doesn't have ______ time or ______ friends in **Bukhara**.  
   key: much + many  
   why: Time is uncountable → much; friends are countable plural → many.

13. _Make the sentence negative. Write much or many in each gap._  
   Our **town** has a lot of shops and a lot of traffic, **Aziz**.  
   → Our **town** doesn't have ______ shops or ______ traffic, **Aziz**.  
   key: many + much  
   why: Shops are countable plural → many; traffic is uncountable → much.

14. _Make the sentence negative. Write much or many in each gap._

   → I don't have ______ homework or ______ tests this **term**, **Timur**.
