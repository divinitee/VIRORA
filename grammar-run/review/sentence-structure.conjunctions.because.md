## transform (15)

1. _Say the same thing with because. Start with the result._  
   Aziz was hungry **at midnight**, so he made a **sandwich**.  
   → Aziz made a **sandwich** **at midnight** ______ (hungry).  
   key: because he was hungry  
   why: The reason (he was hungry) comes after because. So gave the result; because gives the reason.

2. _Say the same thing with because. Start with the result._  
   It was cold, so the children played **in the hall** **after school**.  
   → The children played **in the hall** **after school** ______ (cold).  
   key: because it was cold  
   why: Put the reason after because: because it was cold.

3. _Say the same thing with because. Start with the result._  
   Lola was tired after **school**, so she went to bed **before ten**.  
   → Lola went to bed **before ten** ______ (tired).  
   key: because she was tired  
   why: Put the reason after because: because she was tired.

4. _Say the same thing with because. Start with the result._  
   The bus didn't come, so we walked to **the market** **again**.  
   → We walked to **the market** **again** ______ (come).  
   key: because the bus didn't come  
   why: Put the reason after because: because the bus didn't come.

5. _Say the same thing with because. Start with the result._  
   My phone was broken, so I didn't answer your **calls**, **Mum**.  
   → **Mum**, I didn't answer your **calls** ______ (broken).  
   key: because my phone was broken  
   why: Put the reason after because: because my phone was broken.

6. _Change because of + noun into because + a full sentence._  
   **We** stayed at home **on Sunday** because of the rain.  
   → **We** stayed at home **on Sunday** ______ (rain).  
   key: because it was raining (also: because it rained)  
   why: Because of + noun (the rain) becomes because + a sentence: because it was raining.

7. _Change because of + noun into because + a full sentence._  
   The **game** stopped **early** because of the snow.  
   → The **game** stopped **early** ______ (snow).  
   key: because it was snowing (also: because it snowed)  
   why: Because of the snow becomes because it was snowing.

8. _Change because of + noun into because + a full sentence._  
   **Malika** couldn't sleep **at all** because of the noise.  
   → **Malika** couldn't sleep **at all** ______ (noisy).  
   key: because it was noisy (also: because it was so noisy, because it was too noisy, because it was very noisy)  
   why: Because of the noise becomes because it was noisy.

9. _Change because of + noun into because + a full sentence._  
   Malika stayed **in her room** **yesterday** because of her headache.  
   → Malika stayed **in her room** **yesterday** ______ (headache).  
   key: because she had a headache (also: because her head hurt)  
   why: Because of her headache becomes because she had a headache.

10. _Change because of + noun into because + a full sentence._  
   They cancelled our **picnic** **yesterday** because of the fog.  
   → They cancelled our **picnic** **yesterday** ______ (foggy).  
   key: because it was foggy (also: because it was very foggy)  
   why: Because of the fog becomes because it was foggy.

11. _Join the two sentences with because._  
   Bobur is happy. He passed his **driving test** **on Monday**.  
   → Bobur is happy ______ (pass) his **driving test** **on Monday**.  
   key: because he passed  
   why: The second sentence is the reason, so join with because: because he passed…

12. _Join the two sentences with because._  
   **My parents** love **Samarkand**. It's very beautiful.  
   → **My parents** love **Samarkand** ______ (beautiful).  
   key: because it's very beautiful (also: because it is very beautiful, because it's beautiful, because it is beautiful)  
   why: The second sentence is the reason: because it's very beautiful.

13. _Join the two sentences with because._  
   My brother is learning English. He wants to travel **to America**.  
   → My brother is learning English ______ (travel) **to America**.  
   key: because he wants to travel  
   why: The second sentence is the reason: because he wants to travel.

14. _Join the two sentences with because._  
   The children are tired. They played all **afternoon** in the **snow**.  
   → The children are tired ______ (play) all **afternoon** in the **snow**.  
   key: because they played  
   why: The second sentence is the reason: because they played…

15. _Join the two sentences with because._  
   Dad is angry. I broke his **camera** **this morning**.  
   → Dad is angry ______ (break) his **camera** **this morning**.  
   key: because I broke

   target: because for reasons · look for: because + a full sentence · three different reasons · no because of + sentence

   target: because / because of for an excuse · look for: because + reason · because of + noun · polite words like sorry

   target: because to support an opinion · look for: I prefer … because … · two or more reasons · full sentences after because

   target: because / because of in the past · look for: because + past sentence · because of + noun · past tense verbs
