## create (8)

1. Write about yourself: your age or your city. Use I am.  
   must use: I am · exactly one sentence · must use am

2. Write about a person in your family. Use is.  
   must use: is · exactly one sentence · must use is

3. Write where your friends are now. Use are.  
   must use: are · exactly one sentence · must use are

4. Write about you and a friend. Use … and I are.  
   must use: and I are · exactly one sentence · must use and I are

5. Write about your town. Use It is.  
   must use: It is · exactly one sentence · must use is

6. Write one sentence with am, one with is and one with are.  
   must use: am / is / are · exactly three sentences · must use am, is and are

7. Write about two things in your bag. Use are.  
   must use: are · exactly one sentence · plural subject + are

8. Write about your teacher. Use is.  
   must use: is · exactly one sentence · must use is

## express (5)

1. Introduce yourself: your name, age and city.  
   target: am · look for: I am · I'm

2. Describe your family: who they are and where they are now.  
   target: is / are · look for: is · are

3. Describe your classroom: what is in it and where things are.  
   target: is / are · look for: is · are

4. Write about you and your best friend.  
   target: and I are · look for: and I are · is

5. Describe your town for a visitor.  
   target: is / are · look for: It is · are
