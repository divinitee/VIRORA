## transform (15)

1. _Rewrite the sentence with enjoy. Change the form of the second verb._  
   I like to paint **people** **after school**.  
   → I enjoy ______ (paint) **people** **after school**.  
   key: painting  
   why: Like can take to + verb, but enjoy can't: enjoy painting.

2. _Rewrite the sentence with enjoy. Change the form of the second verb._  
   My brother likes to play **cards** with his **friends**.  
   → My brother enjoys ______ (play) **cards** with his **friends**.  
   key: playing  
   why: After enjoys, the next verb takes -ing: enjoys playing.

3. _Rewrite the sentence with enjoy. Change the form of the second verb._  
   We love to walk in the **mountains** **on Sundays**.  
   → We enjoy ______ (walk) in the **mountains** **on Sundays**.  
   key: walking  
   why: Love can take to + verb, but enjoy takes only -ing: enjoy walking.

4. _Rewrite the sentence with enjoy. Change the form of the second verb._  
   Nodira likes to bake **cakes** for her **family**.  
   → Nodira enjoys ______ (bake) **cakes** for her **family**.  
   key: baking  
   why: Enjoys + -ing: baking. Drop the final e before -ing.

5. _Rewrite the sentence with enjoy. Change the form of the second verb._  
   They love to sing **pop songs** at **family dinners**.  
   → They enjoy ______ (sing) **pop songs** at **family dinners**.  
   key: singing  
   why: Enjoy is followed by the -ing form: enjoy singing.

6. _Rewrite the sentence in the negative._  
   Lola enjoys getting up early on **Sundays** to **walk the dog**.  
   → Lola ______ (get) up early on **Sundays** to **walk the dog**.  
   key: doesn't enjoy getting  
   why: Lola is one person, so the negative is doesn't enjoy. The next verb keeps -ing: getting.

7. _Rewrite the sentence in the negative._  
   I enjoy doing **homework** **in the evening**.  
   → I ______ (do) **homework** **in the evening**.  
   key: don't enjoy doing  
   why: With I, the negative is don't enjoy, and the next verb keeps -ing: doing.

8. _Rewrite the sentence in the negative._  
   My parents enjoy watching **talent shows** **at weekends**.  
   → My parents ______ (watch) **talent shows** **at weekends**.  
   key: don't enjoy watching  
   why: My parents is plural, so use don't enjoy + watching.

9. _Rewrite the sentence in the negative._  
   Rustam enjoys answering **questions** in **history**.  
   → Rustam ______ (answer) **questions** in **history**.  
   key: doesn't enjoy answering  
   why: Rustam is one person, so use doesn't enjoy (no -s on enjoy), then answering.

10. _Rewrite the sentence in the negative._  
   We enjoy sitting in the **back** row of the **theatre**.  
   → We ______ (sit) in the **back** row of the **theatre**.  
   key: don't enjoy sitting  
   why: With we, the negative is don't enjoy, then sitting (double the t).

11. _Say the same thing with enjoy + -ing._  
   I take photos of **birds** **in the park**. It's fun for me.  
   → I enjoy ______ (take) photos of **birds** **in the park**.  
   key: taking  
   why: Enjoy + -ing: taking. Drop the final e of take.

12. _Say the same thing with enjoy + -ing._  
   Bekzod plays football with his **friends** **on Sundays**. He loves it.  
   → Bekzod enjoys ______ (play) football with his **friends** **on Sundays**.  
   key: playing  
   why: Enjoys + -ing: playing.

13. _Say the same thing with enjoy + -ing._  
   My mum makes **dinner** for the **guests**. It makes her happy.  
   → My mum enjoys ______ (make) **dinner** for the **guests**.  
   key: making  
   why: Enjoys + -ing: making. Drop the final e of make.

14. _Say the same thing with enjoy + -ing._  
   Dilnoza writes in her **diary** **every evening**. It relaxes her.  
   → Dilnoza enjoys ______ (write) in her **diary** **every evening**.  
   key: writing  
   why: Enjoys + -ing: writing, with one t.

15. _Say the same thing with enjoy + -ing._  
   My friends and I ride our bikes **around the city** **in summer**. It's great fun.  
   → My friends and I enjoy ______ (ride) our bikes **around the city** **in summer**.  
   key: riding  
   why: Enjoy + -ing: riding. Drop the final e of ride.

## create (8)

1. Write one sentence about something you enjoy doing at the weekend.  
   must use: enjoy + -ing · exactly one sentence · must use I enjoy · must include at the weekend

2. Write one sentence about something you don't enjoy doing at home.  
   must use: don't enjoy + -ing · exactly one sentence · must use don't enjoy · must name a job at home

3. Write one sentence about what a friend enjoys doing.  
   must use: enjoys + -ing · exactly one sentence · must name the friend · must use enjoys + -ing

4. Ask a new classmate a question about their free time.  
   must use: Do you enjoy + -ing … ? · exactly one question · must use Do you enjoy · must end with a question mark

5. Write one sentence about something you loved doing when you were a child.  
   must use: loved + -ing · exactly one sentence · must use loved · must use an -ing form

6. Write one sentence about something you hate doing in the morning.  
   must use: hate + -ing · exactly one sentence · must use hate · must include in the morning

7. Write one sentence about something your family enjoys doing together.  
   must use: enjoy + -ing · exactly one sentence · must use together · must use enjoy + -ing

8. Write two sentences: one thing you enjoy doing in summer and one thing you don't enjoy doing in winter.  
   must use: enjoy / don't enjoy + -ing · exactly two sentences · one positive, one negative · -ing form after enjoy in both

## express (5)

1. Tell a new friend about your free time. What do you enjoy doing, and what don't you enjoy? Write three or four sentences.  
   target: enjoy / don't enjoy + -ing · look for: enjoy + -ing · don't enjoy + -ing · no enjoy to + verb

2. Describe a person in your family and the things they love and hate doing. Write three or four sentences.  
   target: love / hate / enjoy + -ing · look for: loves or enjoys + -ing · hates + -ing · no bare verb after love or hate

3. Describe your last holiday or a special day. What did you enjoy doing? Write three or four sentences.  
   target: enjoyed + -ing · look for: enjoyed + -ing · two or more different -ing forms · past time words like last summer

4. Compare yourself and your best friend: what do you both enjoy doing, and what is different? Write three or four sentences.  
   target: enjoy / like + -ing for comparing · look for: we both enjoy + -ing · he / she enjoys + -ing · but for a difference

5. Your teacher asks: "What do you enjoy doing in English lessons?" Answer in three or four sentences.  
   target: enjoy + -ing with study activities · look for: enjoy + -ing · don't enjoy + -ing · a reason with because
