## create (8)

1. Write one sentence about a food you like.  
   must use: I + like + food · exactly one sentence · subject + verb + object · the verb is not at the end

2. Write one sentence about what your mother or father does at home.  
   must use: subject + verb + object · exactly one sentence · start with My mother / My father · one object after the verb

3. Write one sentence about a sport or game you play.  
   must use: I play + object · exactly one sentence · must use play · object after the verb

4. Write one sentence about a language someone in your family speaks.  
   must use: subject + speaks + language · exactly one sentence · must use speaks or speak · the language after the verb

5. Write one sentence about what you and your friends watch or read.  
   must use: We + verb + object · exactly one sentence · must start with We · object after the verb

6. Write one sentence about something you need for school.  
   must use: I need + object · exactly one sentence · must use need · object after the verb

7. Write one sentence about a shop or market in your town and what it sells.  
   must use: subject + sells + object · exactly one sentence · must use sells · object after the verb

8. Write three short sentences about three people you know. Each one: who + verb + what.  
   must use: subject + verb + object · exactly three sentences · a different verb in each · no verb at the end of a sentence

## express (5)

1. Write about your family: what does each person like, eat or do? Write four short sentences.  
   target: subject + verb + object · look for: a subject at the start of each sentence · the verb before the object · four different verbs

2. Describe a normal Sunday at your home. Who cooks, cleans, plays or watches what?  
   target: subject + verb + object · look for: subject + verb + object · names or family words as subjects · no verb at the end

3. Tell a new friend about the things you like and the things you don't like.  
   target: I like / I love / I don't like + object · look for: I + verb + object · three or more things · the object after the verb

4. Write about your best friend: what languages, sports or hobbies do they have?  
   target: he / she + verb + object · look for: he or she + verb + object · speaks / plays / likes · three sentences

5. Describe your town: what do people buy, sell, eat or visit there?  
   target: subject + verb + object · look for: people / tourists + verb + object · a place name · three or four sentences
