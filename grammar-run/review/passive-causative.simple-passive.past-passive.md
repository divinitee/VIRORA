## transform (15)

1. _Correct the mistake._  
   Our school were built in **2012**, **Aziz**.  
   → Our school ______ in **2012**, **Aziz**.  
   key: was built  
   why: One school → was built.

2. _Correct the mistake._  
   These photos was taken in **Bukhara**, **Timur**.  
   → These photos ______ in **Bukhara**, **Timur**.  
   key: were taken  
   why: More than one photo → were taken.

3. _Correct the mistake._  
   The cake was make **an hour ago**, **Aziz**.  
   → The cake ______ **an hour ago**, **Aziz**.  
   key: was made  
   why: After was, use the past participle: made.

4. _Correct the mistake._  
   The letters was sent **two days ago**, **Olga**.  
   → The letters ______ **two days ago**, **Olga**.  
   key: were sent  
   why: More than one letter → were sent.

5. _Correct the mistake._  
   This book wrote in **2010**, **Timur**.  
   → This book ______ in **2010**, **Timur**.  
   key: was written  
   why: A book doesn't write; it was written.
