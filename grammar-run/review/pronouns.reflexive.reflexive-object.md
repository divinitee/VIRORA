# pronouns.reflexive.reflexive-object — review sample (B1)

Each item shows one random filler combination (in bold). Mark anything a teacher wouldn't accept.

## choose (20)

1. Be careful with that **saw**, **Madina**! You might cut ______.  
   options: ✔ yourself · you · yours · your  
   why: You might cut you: the same person, so yourself.

2. I taught ______ to play the **guitar** from videos, **Olga**.  
   options: me · mine · I · ✔ myself  
   why: I taught me: the same person, so myself.

3. I hurt ______ when I fell on the **stairs** **on Friday**.  
   options: mine · I · ✔ myself · me  
   why: I hurt me: the same person, so myself.

4. Help ______ to some **plov**, **Kamila**!  
   options: your · ✔ yourself · you · yours  
   why: Help yourself = take what you want: yourself.

5. Did you enjoy ______ at the **concert**, **Lola**?  
   options: ✔ yourself · you · yours · your  
   why: enjoy needs an object: you enjoy yourself.

6. Let me introduce ______. I'm **Timur**, your new **driver**.  
   options: me · mine · I · ✔ myself  
   why: I introduce me: the same person, so myself.

7. We really enjoyed ______ at the **concert** **yesterday**.  
   options: ourself · our · ✔ ourselves · us  
   why: We enjoyed us: the same people, so ourselves.

8. **Timur** made ______ a cup of **tea** and sat down.  
   options: he · ✔ himself · hisself · his  
   why: He made a cup for him: the same person, so himself.

9. My **sister** bought ______ a new **bike** with her first salary.  
   options: ✔ herself · sheself · hers · she  
   why: She bought it for her: the same person, so herself.

10. The **printer** turns ______ off after **five** minutes.  
   options: its · itselves · it's · ✔ itself  
   why: The machine turns the machine off: itself.

11. The **children** can dress ______ now; they're **five**.  
   options: their · theirs · ✔ themselves · theirselves  
   why: The children dress the children: themselves.

12. Look after ______ in **Termez**, **Kamila**!  
   options: your · ✔ yourself · you · yours  
   why: Look after you: the same person, so yourself.

13. I talk to ______ when I'm **nervous**, **Lola**.  
   options: ✔ myself · me · mine · I  
   why: I talk to me: the same person, so myself.

14. We built ______ a small **shed** in the **garden**.  
   options: ourself · our · ours · ✔ ourselves  
   why: We built it for us: the same people, so ourselves.

15. Don't blame ______, **Lola**. It wasn't your fault.  
   options: yours · your · ✔ yourself · you  
   why: You blame you: the same person, so yourself.

16. My mum looked at ______ and **waved**, **Madina**.  
   options: I · ✔ me · myself · mine  
   why: Mum looked at a different person: me.

17. Can you help ______ with this **box**, **Olga**?  
   options: ✔ me · myself · mine · I  
   why: You help a different person: me.

18. The teacher asked ______ a **strange** question **last week**.  
   options: myself · mine · I · ✔ me  
   why: The teacher asked a different person: me.

19. Our **neighbours** invited ______ to dinner **on Friday**.  
   options: our · we · ✔ us · ourselves  
   why: Our … invited different people: us.

20. I'll call ______ **tomorrow**, **Lola**.  
   options: your · ✔ you · yourself · yours  
   why: I call a different person: you.

## transform (15)

1. _Correct the mistake._  
   I cut me on the broken **bottle** **on Friday**.  
   → I cut ______ on the broken **bottle** **on Friday**.  
   key: myself  
   why: Same person as I: myself, not me.

2. _Correct the mistake._  
   Did you hurt you on the **ice**, **Lola**?  
   → Did you hurt ______ on the **ice**, **Lola**?  
   key: yourself  
   why: Same person as you: yourself, not you.

3. _Correct the mistake._  
   We enjoyed us at the **wedding** **yesterday**.  
   → We enjoyed ______ at the **wedding** **yesterday**.  
   key: ourselves  
   why: Same people as we: ourselves, not us.

4. _Correct the mistake._  
   **Timur** taught hisself to **drive**.  
   → **Timur** taught ______ to **drive**.  
   key: himself  
   why: The word is himself, never hisself.

5. _Correct the mistake._  
   The kids made theirselves some **eggs** **last night**.  
   → The kids made ______ some **eggs** **last night**.  
   key: themselves  
   why: The word is themselves, never theirselves.

6. _Correct the mistake._  
   My mum helped myself with the **letter**, **Olga**.  
   → My mum helped ______ with the **letter**, **Olga**.  
   key: me  
   why: Mum helped someone else: me, not myself.

7. _Correct the mistake._  
   Can you call myself **tomorrow**, **Madina**?  
   → Can you call ______ **tomorrow**, **Madina**?  
   key: me  
   why: You call someone else: me, not myself.

8. _Correct the mistake._  
   Our **cousins** invited ourselves to the **party**.  
   → Our **cousins** invited ______ to the **party**.  
   key: us  
   why: Our … invited other people: us, not ourselves.

9. _Correct the mistake._  
   I'll text yourself the **plan**, **Lola**.  
   → I'll text ______ the **plan**, **Lola**.  
   key: you  
   why: I text someone else: you, not yourself.

10. _Correct the mistake._  
   The **neighbour** gave myself a present **on Friday**.  
   → The **neighbour** gave ______ a present **on Friday**.  
   key: me  
   why: The … gave to someone else: me, not myself.

11. _Start the sentence with I._  
   You can look after yourself in **Bukhara**, **Olga**.  
   → I can look after ______ in **Bukhara**, **Olga**.  
   key: myself  
   why: New subject I, looking after me: myself.

12. _Start the sentence with We._  
   I enjoyed myself at the **festival**, **Kamila**.  
   → We enjoyed ______ at the **festival**, **Kamila**.  
   key: ourselves  
   why: New subject we: ourselves.

13. _Start the sentence with I._  
   We introduced ourselves to the new **teacher**, **Olga**.  
   → I introduced ______ to the new **teacher**, **Olga**.  
   key: myself  
   why: New subject I, introducing me: myself.

14. _Start the sentence with My brother._  
   I taught myself to **swim**.  
   → My **cousin** taught ______ to **swim**.  
   key: himself  
   why: New subject he: himself.

15. _Start the sentence with My sister._  
   I bought myself a new **bike**.  
   → My **cousin** bought ______ a new **bike**.  
   key: herself  
   why: New subject she: herself.

## create (8)

1. Write one sentence about something you taught yourself.  
   must use: I taught myself to … · exactly one sentence · myself · taught

2. Write one sentence telling a friend to be careful.  
   must use: Be careful! Don't hurt yourself. · one or two short sentences · yourself · an imperative

3. Write one sentence about a party where you had a good time.  
   must use: We enjoyed ourselves at … · exactly one sentence · ourselves · a past verb

4. Write one sentence introducing yourself to a new class.  
   must use: Let me introduce myself. I'm … · one or two short sentences · myself · introduce

5. Write one sentence about someone who helped you.  
   must use: My … helped me with … · exactly one sentence · me, not myself · a different subject

6. Write one sentence about a small accident at home.  
   must use: I burned myself on … · exactly one sentence · a -self word · a past verb

7. Write one sentence about a person in your family who bought something for themselves.  
   must use: My … bought himself / herself … · exactly one sentence · himself or herself · no hisself

8. Write two sentences: one with myself and one with me.  
   must use: I hurt myself. / My friend helped me. · exactly two sentences · myself with I · me with another subject
