# reported-speech.statements.backshift-present — review sample (B1)

Each item shows one random filler combination (in bold). Mark anything a teacher wouldn't accept.

## choose (20)

1. Yesterday **Timur** said, 'I'm **busy**.' He said that he ______ **busy**.  
   options: ✔ was · is · were · has been  
   why: 'I'm' moves back a step: am → was.

2. Last week my **friends** said, 'We're at the **station**.' They said they ______ at the **station**.  
   options: are · was · have been · ✔ were  
   why: 'We're' moves back: are → were.

3. On the phone last night, **Lola** said, 'I'm at the **hotel**.' She said she ______ at the **hotel**.  
   options: were · has been · ✔ was · is  
   why: 'I'm at the …' moves back: am → was.

4. Yesterday **Aziz** said, 'I'm working on my **car**.' He said he ______ on his **car**.  
   options: were working · ✔ was working · is working · worked  
   why: 'I'm working' moves back: was working.

5. Last night the **girls** said, 'We're watching a **match**.' They said they ______ a **match**.  
   options: ✔ were watching · are watching · watched · was watching  
   why: 'We're watching' moves back: were watching.

6. This morning **Lola** said, 'I'm waiting for the **metro**.' She said she ______ for the **metro**.  
   options: is waiting · waited · were waiting · ✔ was waiting  
   why: 'I'm waiting' moves back: was waiting.

7. Last week **Bekzod** said, 'I have a **cold**.' He said he ______ a **cold**.  
   options: have · had had · ✔ had · has  
   why: 'I have' moves back: have → had.

8. On Monday **Lola** said, 'I have a lot of **homework**.' She said she ______ a lot of **homework**.  
   options: had had · ✔ had · has · have  
   why: 'I have a lot of' moves back: had.

9. At the café yesterday, **Bekzod** said, 'I want some **tea**.' He said he ______ some **tea**.  
   options: ✔ wanted · wants · want · had want  
   why: 'I want' moves back: want → wanted.

10. Yesterday **Madina** said, 'I need your **car**.' She said she ______ my **car**.  
   options: needs · need · was need · ✔ needed  
   why: 'I need' moves back: need → needed.

11. Last Friday **Aziz** said, 'I can't come to the **party**.' He said he ______ come to the **party**.  
   options: can't to · didn't can · ✔ couldn't · can't  
   why: 'I can't' moves back: can't → couldn't.

12. On Sunday **Sevara** said, 'I can help you with the **cooking**.' She said she ______ help me with the **cooking**.  
   options: could to · ✔ could · can · can to  
   why: 'I can help' moves back: can → could.

13. Last night the **waiter** said, 'The **room** is ready.' He said the **room** ______ ready.  
   options: ✔ was · is · were · has been  
   why: 'The … is ready' moves back: is → was.

14. At lunch yesterday, **Rustam** said, 'I'm not **hungry**.' He said he ______ **hungry**.  
   options: isn't · weren't · didn't · ✔ wasn't  
   why: 'I'm not' moves back: wasn't.

15. At the **concert** last night, **Lola** said, 'I'm not feeling well.' She said she ______ feeling well.  
   options: didn't · weren't · ✔ wasn't · isn't  
   why: 'I'm not feeling' moves back: wasn't feeling.

16. After the concert, my **neighbours** said, 'We're very **happy**.' They said they ______ very **happy**.  
   options: have been · ✔ were · are · was  
   why: 'We're very …' moves back: were.

17. Last week **Aziz** said, 'My **fridge** doesn't work.' He said his **fridge** ______ work, but it works now.  
   options: ✔ didn't · doesn't · don't · wasn't  
   why: 'doesn't work' moves back: didn't work.

18. Yesterday **Lola** called and said, 'I'm lost in **Samarkand**.' She said she ______ lost in **Samarkand**.  
   options: is · were · has been · ✔ was  
   why: 'I'm lost' moves back: was lost.

19. Last night **Bekzod** said, 'I'm cooking **soup**.' He said he ______ **soup**.  
   options: cooked · were cooking · ✔ was cooking · is cooking  
   why: 'I'm cooking' moves back: was cooking.

20. Last week the **teacher** said, 'The **door** is broken.' She said the **door** ______ broken. They fixed it on Friday.  
   options: has been · ✔ was · is · were  
   why: 'is broken', and it's fixed now: was.

## create (8)

1. A friend said yesterday: 'I'm tired.' Report it in one sentence.  
   must use: He / She said (that) he / she was tired · exactly one sentence · said · was

2. Your mum said last night: 'I'm cooking plov.' Report it.  
   must use: She said she was cooking plov · exactly one sentence · was + -ing · she

3. Your classmates said last week: 'We're at the museum.' Report it.  
   must use: They said they were at the museum · exactly one sentence · they · were

4. Someone said: 'I can't come.' Report it in one sentence.  
   must use: … said he / she couldn't come · exactly one sentence · couldn't · past

5. Report something a friend said on the phone yesterday. Use said that.  
   must use: said that + past · exactly one sentence · said that · one step into the past

6. Someone said: 'My car doesn't work.' Report it.  
   must use: … said his / her car didn't work · exactly one sentence · his / her · didn't

7. Write the direct words and then the reported sentence.  
   must use: direct → reported · exactly two sentences · quotation marks in the first · backshift in the second

8. A tourist said: 'I want a map.' Report it.  
   must use: He / She said he / she wanted a map · exactly one sentence · wanted · pronoun change
