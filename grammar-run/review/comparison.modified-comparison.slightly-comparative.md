# comparison.modified-comparison.slightly-comparative — review sample (B1)

Each item shows one random filler combination (in bold). Mark anything a teacher wouldn't accept.

## choose (20)

   options: ✔ slightly · much · far · very  
   why: 100,000 against 103,000 is a tiny change: slightly.

   options: much · far · very · ✔ slightly  
   why: 20 against 21 degrees: a small difference: slightly.
