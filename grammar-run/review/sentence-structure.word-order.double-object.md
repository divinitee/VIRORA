## create (8)

1. Write one sentence about a present someone gave you. Put the person before the thing.  
   must use: … gave me … · exactly one sentence · gave me + a thing · no to

2. Write one sentence about something you sent to a friend. Use sent + person + thing.  
   must use: I sent my friend … · exactly one sentence · sent + person + thing · no to

3. Write one sentence about a meal a relative cooked for your family.  
   must use: My … cooked us … · exactly one sentence · cooked us · a food word

4. Write one sentence about a teacher who taught you something.  
   must use: … teaches / taught us … · exactly one sentence · teach + us · a subject word

5. Ask a friend to pass you something at the table.  
   must use: Can you pass me …? · exactly one question · pass me · a food word

6. Write one sentence about a story someone told you.  
   must use: … told me a story about … · exactly one sentence · told me · a story word

7. Write one sentence about something you bought for your mum or dad.  
   must use: I bought my mum … · exactly one sentence · bought + person + thing · no for

8. Write two sentences: something you gave someone, and something someone showed you.  
   must use: I gave … / … showed me … · exactly two sentences · gave + person + thing · showed me + thing

## express (5)

1. Describe your last birthday: what did people give you, and what did they make you?  
   target: gave me / made me · look for: gave me · a second verb + me · three or four sentences

2. Write about a kind neighbour or relative: what does he or she bring you, tell you or show you?  
   target: brings us / tells us / shows us · look for: two of bring, tell, show · person + thing · three or four sentences

3. Describe a family holiday: who bought or cooked what for whom?  
   target: Dad bought us … / Mum cooked us … · look for: bought or cooked + person + thing · a food word · three or four sentences

4. Write a thank-you message to a teacher: what did he or she teach you and give you?  
   target: You taught us … / You gave us … · look for: taught us · gave us · three or four sentences

5. You are planning presents for your family. Write what you will give or buy each person.  
   target: I'll give my … / I'll buy my … · look for: give + person + thing · buy + person + thing · three or four sentences
