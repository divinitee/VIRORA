   key: likes

   target: Everyone + verb-s / Nobody + verb-s · look for: Everyone · Nobody · three or four sentences

   target: Everybody has / likes … · look for: Everybody · a singular verb · three or four sentences

   target: Everyone was … Nobody was … · look for: was twice · Everyone or Nobody · three or four sentences

   target: Everyone knows … No one lives … · look for: Everyone · No one · three or four sentences

   target: Someone was … Nobody knows … · look for: Someone · Nobody knows · three or four sentences
