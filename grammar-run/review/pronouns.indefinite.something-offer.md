## create (8)

1. A guest comes to your home. Offer them something to drink.  
   must use: Would you like something to drink? · exactly one sentence · something to · a question mark

2. Your friend looks hungry. Offer food.  
   must use: Can I get you something to eat? · exactly one sentence · something · a question mark

3. It's very hot. Offer something cold.  
   must use: Would you like something cold …? · exactly one sentence · something cold · a question mark

4. Your little brother is bored. Offer him something to do or read.  
   must use: Do you want something to …? · exactly one sentence · something to · a question mark

5. You are in a café. Ask for something sweet.  
   must use: Could I have something sweet …? · exactly one sentence · something + adjective · please

6. Your grandma is cold. Offer her something to wear.  
   must use: Would you like something to wear? · exactly one sentence · something to wear · a question mark

7. You are going to the shop. Ask your mum if she wants something.  
   must use: Do you want something from the shop? · exactly one sentence · something · shop

8. Write two offers: one with something to + verb, one with something + adjective.  
   must use: something to eat / something hot · exactly two questions · something to once · something + adjective once

## express (5)

   target: Would you like something to …? · look for: something twice · a question · three or four sentences

2. Write a short dialogue in a café between you and a waiter.  
   target: Can I get you something …? / Could I have something …? · look for: something · please · three or four lines

3. Your cousin arrives after a long train trip. Write what you say to him or her.  
   target: Would you like something to eat / drink? · look for: something to · a question · three or four sentences

4. A new student is in your class. Write how you help: offer things he or she needs.  
   target: Do you want something to write with? · look for: something · a school word · three or four sentences

5. It's a very hot day at the beach. Offer things to your family.  
   target: Would you like something cold …? · look for: something + adjective · a question · three or four sentences
