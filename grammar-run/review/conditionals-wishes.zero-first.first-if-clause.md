## transform (15)

   Maybe it will rain on **Friday**. Then we will watch a film at **Olga's** house.  
   → If it ______ on **Friday**, we will watch a film at **Olga's** house.  
   key: rains  
   why: Maybe ... then ... becomes a first conditional. After if, use the present simple, not will: if it rains.
