## transform (15)

1. _Make one sentence with between._  
   The bank is next to the **pharmacy**. It's also next to the **café**.  
   → The bank is ______ the **pharmacy** and the **café**.  
   key: between  
   why: Next to A and next to B → between A and B.

2. _Make one sentence with between._  
   On my left is **Timur**. On my right is **Olga**.  
   → I'm sitting ______ **Timur** and **Olga**.  
   key: between  
   why: One person on each side → between.

3. _Make one sentence with between._  
   Our house is next to a **supermarket**. It's also next to a **phone shop**.  
   → Our house is ______ a **supermarket** and a **phone shop**.  
   key: between  
   why: Next to A and next to B → between A and B.

4. _Make one sentence with between._  
   My bed is next to the window. It's also next to the **desk**.  
   → My bed is ______ the window and the **desk**.  
   key: between  
   why: Next to A and next to B → between A and B.

5. _Make one sentence with between._  
   **Bobur** is next to his **grandma**. He's also next to his **brother**.  
   → **Bobur** is ______ his **grandma** and his **brother**.  
   key: between  
   why: One person on each side → between.

6. _Correct the mistake._  
   The bank is between the **supermarket** with the **school**.  
   → The bank is between the **supermarket** ______ the **school**.  
   key: and  
   why: Between A and B, not between A with B.

7. _Correct the mistake._  
   I sit between **Bobur** or **Olga** in class.  
   → I sit between **Bobur** ______ **Olga** in class.  
   key: and  
   why: Between A and B, not between A or B.

8. _Correct the mistake._  
   The café is in the middle of the **supermarket** and the **shoe shop**.  
   → The café is ______ the **supermarket** and the **shoe shop**.  
   key: between  
   why: Two places, one on each side → between, not in the middle of.

9. _Correct the mistake._  
   The pharmacy is between the **phone shop** to the **hospital**.  
   → The pharmacy is between the **phone shop** ______ the **hospital**.

10. _Correct the mistake._  
   Stand between of **Bobur** and **Olga**, **Mum**.

   key: between
