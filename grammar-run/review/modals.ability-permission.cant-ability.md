## transform (15)

1. _Rewrite the sentence in the negative._  
   Aziz can swim **far** in the **pool**.  
   → Aziz ______ (swim) **far** in the **pool**.  
   key: can't swim (also: can not swim)  
   why: Add n't to can: can't swim. Don't use doesn't.

2. _Rewrite the sentence in the negative._  
   My sister can drive **a van** **on long trips**.  
   → My sister ______ (drive) **a van** **on long trips**.  
   key: can't drive (also: can not drive)  
   why: The negative of can is can't (cannot): can't drive. Not doesn't can.

3. _Rewrite the sentence in the negative._  
   We can see the **mountains** from **the window**.  
   → We ______ (see) the **mountains** from **the window**.  
   key: can't see (also: can not see)  
   why: The negative is can't + base verb: can't see. Not don't can.

4. _Rewrite the sentence in the negative._  
   I can understand **the film** **without help**.  
   → I ______ (understand) **the film** **without help**.  
   key: can't understand (also: can not understand)  
   why: With I, the negative is still can't: can't understand.

5. _Rewrite the sentence in the negative._

## express (5)

1. Write about three things you can't do but would like to learn. Write three or four sentences.  
   target: can't + base verb · look for: I can't + base verb · three different verbs · no don't can

2. A friend invites you to play football, but you can't. Write a short reply and explain why.  
   target: can't for inability · look for: I can't + base verb · a reason with because · a polite word like sorry

3. Describe a family member: what can they do, and what can't they do? Write three or four sentences.  
   target: can / can't + base verb · look for: he / she can … · he / she can't … · no doesn't can

4. Compare a baby and a ten-year-old child. What can't a baby do yet? Write three or four sentences.  
   target: can't … yet · look for: can't + base verb · yet · can for the older child

5. Write about an old phone or computer you have. What can't it do? Write three or four sentences.  
   target: it can't + base verb · look for: it can't · different verbs · base verb after can't
