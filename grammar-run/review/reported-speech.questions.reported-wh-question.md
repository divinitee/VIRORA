   must use: She asked me why I was late · exactly one sentence · why + subject + verb · past

3. Report a question a tourist asked you.  
   must use: … asked me how / where … · exactly one sentence · statement order · no do / did

4. Write the direct question and then the reported question.  
   must use: direct → reported wh-question · exactly two sentences · a question mark in the first · statement order in the second

5. Your mum asked: 'What are you doing?' Report it.  
   must use: She asked me what I was doing · exactly one sentence · was + -ing · statement order

6. Report a question with wanted to know.  
   must use: … wanted to know … · exactly one sentence · wanted to know · wh-word + subject + verb

7. A friend asked: 'When did you come back?' Report it.  
   must use: … asked me when I had come back · exactly one sentence · had + past participle · no did

8. Report a question someone asked you in a job interview.  
   must use: … asked me why / what … · exactly one sentence · statement order · past
