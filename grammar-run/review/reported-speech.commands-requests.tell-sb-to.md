## create (8)

1. Your teacher said: 'Open your books.' Report it.  
   must use: The teacher told us to open our books · exactly one sentence · told + person + to · past

   must use: My mum told me not to come home late · exactly one sentence · told me not to · base verb

3. A friend said: 'Please help me.' Report it with asked.  
   must use: He / She asked me to help him / her · exactly one sentence · asked + person + to · pronoun change

4. Report an order a coach gave a team.  
   must use: The coach told the players to … · exactly one sentence · told · to + base verb

   must use: … asked the assistant to … · exactly one sentence · asked · to + base verb

   must use: command → told + person + to · exactly two sentences · quotation marks · told + person + to

   must use: My parents told me not to … · exactly one sentence · not to · past

   must use: The doctor told me to … · exactly one sentence · told me to · base verb
