   Aziz walks to **work** with his **cousin** every day.  
   → This week Aziz ______ (walk) to **work** alone. His **cousin** has a cold.  
   key: is walking  
   why: This week is a temporary change, so use the present continuous: is walking.

   My mother cooks **plov** at home every evening.

   key: is cooking  
   why: This week is different from the routine, so use the present continuous: is cooking.

## create (8)

2. Write one sentence about how a family member usually travels to work and how they are travelling today.  
   must use: present simple + present continuous · exactly one sentence · must include today · must name a family member

3. Write one sentence about where a friend usually lives and where they are staying this month.  
   must use: lives or live + is staying or are staying · exactly one sentence · must include this month · must use but

4. Write one sentence about what you usually eat for lunch and what you are eating this week.  
   must use: eat + am eating · exactly one sentence · must use I · must include this week

5. Write one sentence about a teacher's normal routine and a temporary change.  
   must use: present simple + present continuous · exactly one sentence · must be about a teacher · must include normally or usually

6. Write one sentence about a sport or hobby you usually do and what you are doing instead these days.  
   must use: present simple + present continuous · exactly one sentence · must include these days · must use but

7. Write one sentence about a shop, bus or lesson that is different from usual this week.  
   must use: present simple + present continuous · exactly one sentence · must be about a thing, not a person · must include this week

8. Write two sentences: first your usual weekend routine, then what you are doing differently this weekend.  
   must use: present simple in the first sentence, present continuous in the second · exactly two sentences · first sentence present simple · second sentence present continuous

## express (5)

1. Your life is different this month. Describe your usual routine and what is different now. Write three or four sentences.  
   target: present simple for routine, present continuous for temporary change · look for: usually or every day · this month or these days · both tenses used correctly

2. Write a message to a friend: say what you normally do on Saturdays and why this Saturday is different. Write three or four sentences.  
   target: present simple + present continuous · look for: normally or usually · this Saturday · but to show the contrast

3. Describe a family member who has a temporary job or project. What do they usually do, and what are they doing now? Write three or four sentences.  
   target: present simple + present continuous · look for: -s with he or she · is + -ing for the change · time words for each tense

4. Describe how your school or work is different during exam time. Write three or four sentences.  
   target: present simple for normal days, present continuous for exam time · look for: normally or usually · this week or at the moment · both tenses

5. Imagine you are on holiday. Compare your normal day with your holiday day. Write three or four sentences.  
   target: present simple + present continuous · look for: usual habits in the present simple · holiday activities in the present continuous · but or however
