## transform (15)

1. _Rewrite the sentence about the past._  
   I can **ride a bike**, **Olga**.  
   → When I was six, I ______ **ride a bike**, **Olga**.  
   key: could  
   why: Ability in the past: can → could.

2. _Rewrite the sentence about the past._  
   My **uncle** can speak **English** very well.  
   → Ten years ago, my **uncle** ______ speak **English** very well.  
   key: could  
   why: Ability in the past: can → could.

3. _Rewrite the sentence about the past._  
   **Timur** can draw beautiful **horses**.  
   → As a child, **Timur** ______ draw beautiful **horses**.  
   key: could  
   why: Ability in the past: can → could.

4. _Rewrite the sentence about the past._  
   We can play **tag** in the yard every evening, **Lola**.  
   → When we were small, we ______ play **tag** in the yard every evening, **Lola**.  
   key: could  
   why: Ability in the past: can → could.

5. _Rewrite the sentence about the past._  
   My **brother** can lift very heavy **sacks of rice**.  
   → At thirty, my **brother** ______ lift very heavy **sacks of rice**.  
   key: could  
   why: Ability in the past: can → could.

6. _Make the sentence negative._

7. _Make the sentence negative._

## express (5)

   target: could / couldn't · look for: could · couldn't · three or four sentences

   target: he / she could … · look for: could twice · a family word · three or four sentences

3. Write about a bad day. What couldn't you do, and why?  
   target: I couldn't … because … · look for: couldn't · because · three or four sentences

4. Compare your English now and one year ago.  
   target: I couldn't … Now I can … · look for: could or couldn't · can · three or four sentences

5. Describe a friend from your first school. What could he or she do well?  
   target: he / she could … · look for: could · a school word · three or four sentences
