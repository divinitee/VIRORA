## transform (15)

1. _Say the same thing with a verb and an adverb._  
   **Madina** is a good singer, **Dilnoza**.  
   → **Madina** sings ______, **Dilnoza**.  
   key: well  
   why: A good singer sings well. Good describes the person, well describes the action.

2. _Say the same thing with a verb and an adverb._  
   My **father** is a hard worker, **Olga**.

## create (8)

1. Write one sentence about something you do well.  
   must use: an action verb + well · exactly one sentence · must be about you · must use well, not good

2. Write one sentence about a person who works or studies hard.  
   must use: works hard or studies hard · exactly one sentence · must name a person · must use hard, not hardly

3. Write one sentence about someone who runs, talks or drives fast.  
   must use: an action verb + fast · exactly one sentence · must use fast · must not use fastly

4. Write one sentence about a day when you got up early or went to bed late.  
   must use: got up early or went to bed late · exactly one sentence · must use early or late · past tense

5. Write one sentence about how well you speak another language.  
   must use: speak + a language + well · exactly one sentence · must name a language · must use well

6. Write one sentence with good and one with well about the same person.  
   must use: good as an adjective, well as an adverb · exactly two sentences · good before a noun or after be · well after an action verb

7. A friend wrote: "My brother drives very good but too fastly." Write the sentence correctly.  
   must use: drives very well but too fast · exactly one sentence · must use well · must use fast

8. Write one sentence of advice for a student before an exam. Use hard or early.  
   must use: study hard or go to bed early · exactly one sentence · must be advice · must use hard or early

## express (5)

1. What can you do well, and what can't you do well yet? Write three or four sentences.  
   target: action verbs with well · look for: well after the verb or object · no good in place of well · at least two different verbs

2. Describe a person you know who works very hard. What do they do? Write three or four sentences.  
   target: work hard, start early, finish late · look for: hard, not hardly · early or late · the adverb after the verb

3. Describe your school day or work day. When do you get up, how do you work, when do you finish? Write three or four sentences.  
   target: early, late, hard, fast, well · look for: early or late · hard or fast · no fastly or hardly

4. Who is the best sports player you know? How do they play, run or train? Write three or four sentences.  
   target: action verbs with well, fast and hard · look for: plays well · runs fast or trains hard · adverbs, not adjectives

5. Give advice to a new student who wants to learn English well. Write three or four sentences.  
   target: well, hard, early · look for: speak or learn well · study or work hard · no good in place of well
