## transform (15)

   My **cousin** doesn't often cook **plov**.

   key: rarely cooks  
   why: Rarely means not often, and it is already negative in meaning, so the verb is positive: rarely cooks.

## express (5)

1. Describe things you rarely do. When do you do them, and why not more often? Write three or four sentences.  
   target: rarely = not often · look for: rarely + positive verb · a reason for each · no fixed rule like once a week = rarely

2. Describe your family's food. What do you often eat, and what do you rarely eat? Write three or four sentences.  
   target: often vs rarely with food · look for: often for many times · rarely for not often · food named

3. Describe a relative or friend you rarely see. Where do they live, and when do you meet? Write three or four sentences.  
   target: rarely with people · look for: rarely see or rarely meet · a place named · when you meet

4. Describe your weekends. What do you never do, rarely do and often do? Write three or four sentences.  
   target: never, rarely and often · look for: never for not at all · rarely for not often · often for many times

5. Describe the weather in your city in different seasons. What rarely happens there? Write three or four sentences.  
   target: rarely with weather · look for: rarely + rains or snows · seasons or months named · it with the -s form
