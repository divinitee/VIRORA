## transform (15)

1. _Rewrite with despite. Keep the meaning._  
   Although it was raining, we played **volleyball**, **Timur**.  
   → Despite ______, we played **volleyball**, **Timur**.  
   key: the rain  
   why: Despite + noun: it was raining → the rain.

2. _Rewrite with despite. Keep the meaning._  
   Although he was ill, **Bekzod** went to **school**.  
   → Despite ______, **Bekzod** went to **school**.  
   key: being ill (also: his illness, feeling ill)  
   why: Despite + -ing: he was ill → being ill.

3. _Rewrite with despite. Keep the meaning._  
   Although it was cold, we swam every **morning**, **Olga**.  
   → Despite ______, we swam every **morning**, **Olga**.
