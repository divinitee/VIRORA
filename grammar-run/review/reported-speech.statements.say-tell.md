# reported-speech.statements.say-tell — review sample (B1)

Each item shows one random filler combination (in bold). Mark anything a teacher wouldn't accept.

## choose (20)

1. **Timur** ______ that the **meeting** had been cancelled.  
   options: ✔ said · told · said me · told to  
   why: No listener after the gap, just that: said.

2. **Olga** ______ she was tired after the **flight**.  
   options: told · said me · told to · ✔ said  
   why: No listener after the gap: said she was tired.

3. The **guard** ______ that the **pharmacy** was closed.  
   options: said me · told to · ✔ said · told  
   why: No listener, just that: said.

4. My **dad** ______ it would rain **in the afternoon**.  
   options: told to · ✔ said · told · said me  
   why: No listener, just it would rain: said.

5. **Bekzod** ______, 'I'm hungry,' and opened the **bag**.  
   options: ✔ said · told · said me · told to  
   why: Before the exact words 'I'm hungry': said.

6. Everyone ______ the **cake** was delicious, **Rustam**.  
   options: told · said me · told to · ✔ said  
   why: No listener after the gap: said the … was.

7. The teacher ______ that we had a test on **Tuesday**, **Olga**.  
   options: said me · told to · ✔ said · told  
   why: "The teacher … that": no listener named: said.

8. **Lola** ______ hello and sat down next to me on the **bus**.  
   options: told to · ✔ said · told · said me  
   why: Fixed phrase say hello: said hello.

9. What did the **boss** ______ about the **test**?  
   options: ✔ say · tell · say me · tell to  
   why: After did, base form, no listener: say.

10. **Bekzod** ______ goodbye and left the **office**.  
   options: told · said me · told to · ✔ said  
   why: Fixed phrase say goodbye: said goodbye.

11. **Olga** ______ me that she was moving to **Tashkent**.  
   options: told to · talked · ✔ told · said  
   why: A listener (me) after the gap: told me.

12. **Rustam** ______ us a funny story about his **boss**.  
   options: talked · ✔ told · said · told to  
   why: tell a story: told us a story.

13. The doctor ______ my **dad** that he was fine, **Lola**.  
   options: ✔ told · said · told to · talked  
   why: A listener (my …) after the gap: told.

14. Who ______ you about the **trip**, **Aziz**?  
   options: said · told to · talked · ✔ told  
   why: A listener (you) after the gap: told you.

15. **Lola** ______ her **teacher** the truth.  
   options: told to · talked · ✔ told · said  
   why: She told her … the truth: tell + person + the truth.

16. My **cousin** ______ me a secret **last night**.  
   options: talked · ✔ told · said · told to  
   why: tell a secret, with a listener (me): told.

17. The **teacher** ______ the children a **legend** before bed.  
   options: ✔ told · said · told to · talked  
   why: tell a story, with a listener (the children): told.

18. **Timur** ______ me he would be late for the **meeting**.  
   options: said · told to · talked · ✔ told  
   why: "… me he would be late": a listener: told.

19. Did you ______ **the teacher** about the party, **Lola**?  
   options: tell to · talk · ✔ tell · say  
   why: After did, base form, a listener follows: tell.

20. I ______ him the way to the **station**, **Rustam**.  
   options: talked · ✔ told · said · told to  
   why: tell someone the way: told him the way.

## transform (15)

1. _Correct the mistake._  
   **Madina** said me that she was tired after the **flight**.  
   → **Madina** ______ me that she was tired after the **flight**.  
   key: told  
   why: say has no listener after it: told me.

2. _Correct the mistake._  
   **Timur** told that he was busy on **Friday**.  
   → **Timur** ______ that he was busy on **Friday**.  
   key: said  
   why: tell needs a listener: said that.

3. _Correct the mistake._  
   The **teacher** said us a story about the **mountains**.  
   → The **teacher** ______ us a story about the **mountains**.  
   key: told  
   why: A story with us: told us a story.

4. _Correct the mistake._  
   **Madina** told to me the truth about the **test**.  
   → **Madina** ______ me the truth about the **test**.  
   key: told  
   why: tell + person, no to: told me.

5. _Correct the mistake._  
   My **mum** told it was going to rain **in the afternoon**.  
   → My **mum** ______ it was going to rain **in the afternoon**.  
   key: said  
   why: No listener after the gap: said.

6. _Rewrite with told._  
   **Aziz** said to me that he was hungry after the **lesson**.  
   → **Aziz** ______ that he was hungry after the **lesson**.  
   key: told me  
   why: He said it to me: so told me, no to.

7. _Rewrite with told._  
   **Madina** said to us that the **meeting** was at six.  
   → **Madina** ______ that the **meeting** was at six.  
   key: told us  
   why: said to us = told us.

8. _Rewrite with told._  
   The **teacher** said to the students that the test was easy.  
   → The **teacher** ______ the students that the test was easy.  
   key: told  
   why: said to the students = told the students.

9. _Rewrite with told._  
   **Rustam** said to his **boss** that he was sorry.  
   → **Rustam** ______ his **boss** that he was sorry.  
   key: told  
   why: said to his … = told his ….

10. _Rewrite with told._  
   My **grandmother** said to me, 'Dinner is ready, **Bekzod**.'  
   → My **grandmother** ______ that dinner was ready.  
   key: told me  
   why: 'Dinner is ready' was said to me: told me.

11. _Rewrite with said._

12. _Rewrite with said._

   key: said

13. _Rewrite with said._  
   The **mechanic** told me the **table** was ready.  
   → The **mechanic** ______ the **table** was ready.  
   key: said  
   why: Without me after the verb: said.

14. _Rewrite with said._

15. _Rewrite with said._

## create (8)

   must use: … said that … · exactly one sentence · said · no person after said

2. Write one sentence with told me.  
   must use: … told me (that) … · exactly one sentence · told + person · past

3. Write one sentence about a story someone told you.  
   must use: … told me a story about … · exactly one sentence · told · a person after told

4. Write two sentences about the same news: one with said, one with told.  
   must use: said vs told · exactly two sentences · said that · told + person

5. Write one question with tell.  
   must use: Did you tell …? · exactly one question · tell + person · a question mark

6. Write one sentence with said hello or said goodbye.  
   must use: said hello / goodbye · exactly one sentence · said · hello or goodbye

7. Write one sentence with told the truth or told a lie.  
   must use: told the truth / a lie · exactly one sentence · told · the truth / a lie

8. Write one sentence about what a doctor or teacher said.  
   must use: … said (that) … · exactly one sentence · said · reported speech

## express (5)

1. Report a conversation with a friend. Write three or four sentences with said and told.  
   target: said vs told · look for: said · told + person · three or four sentences

2. What did your teacher say and tell your class this week? Write three or four sentences.  
   target: say / tell in reported speech · look for: said · told us · three or four sentences

3. Report the news a family member told you. Write three or four sentences.  
   target: told me / said · look for: told me · said · three or four sentences

4. Write three or four sentences about a time someone told you a secret.  
   target: told + person · look for: told · said · three or four sentences

5. A tourist asked you for help. Report what he said and what you told him. Write three or four sentences.  
   target: said / told · look for: he said · I told him · three or four sentences
