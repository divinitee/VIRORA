## transform (15)

1. _Make the sentence negative._  
   I saw someone in the **office**, **Olga**.  
   → I ______ in the **office**, **Olga**.  
   key: didn't see anyone (also: didn't see anybody, did not see anybody, saw no one, saw nobody)  
   why: didn't + see, and someone → anyone.

2. _Make the sentence negative._  
   We met someone at the **bazaar** **on Sunday**.  
   → We ______ at the **bazaar** **on Sunday**.  
   key: didn't meet anyone (also: didn't meet anybody, did not meet anybody, met no one, met nobody)  
   why: didn't + meet, and someone → anyone.

3. _Make the sentence negative._  
   **Aziz** knows someone in **Moscow**.  
   → **Aziz** ______ in **Moscow**.  
   key: doesn't know anyone (also: doesn't know anybody, does not know anybody, knows no one, knows nobody)  
   why: doesn't + know, and someone → anyone.

## create (8)

1. Write one sentence about someone you met last week.  
   must use: I met someone … · exactly one sentence · someone · a place word

2. Write one sentence about a place where you don't know anyone.  
   must use: I don't know anyone in / at … · exactly one sentence · don't know anyone · a place word

3. You hear a noise at home. Write one sentence about it with someone.  
   must use: There's someone in the … · exactly one sentence · someone · a room word

4. Write one sentence about a secret. Tell a friend not to tell anyone.  
   must use: Don't tell anyone … · exactly one sentence · anyone · don't

5. Write one sentence about a time you needed someone to help you.  
   must use: I needed someone to … · exactly one sentence · someone · a verb

6. Write one sentence about an empty place you saw.  
   must use: There wasn't anyone in / at … · exactly one sentence · wasn't anyone or didn't see anyone · a place word

7. Write one sentence about a phone call or a message from someone.  
   must use: Someone called / sent … · exactly one sentence · someone · call or message

8. Write two sentences: one with someone and one with anyone.  
   must use: … someone … / … not … anyone … · exactly two sentences · someone once · anyone once with a negative
