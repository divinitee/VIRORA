## create (8)

   must use: There's a … on my desk. · exactly one sentence · on my desk · a thing word

2. Write one sentence about a picture or poster on a wall at home.  
   must use: There's a … on the wall. · exactly one sentence · on the wall · a room word

3. Write one sentence about food on your plate today.  
   must use: There's … on my plate. · exactly one sentence · on my plate · a food word

4. Write one sentence about where your pet or a friend's pet likes to sleep.  
   must use: It sleeps on the … · exactly one sentence · on the · an animal word

5. Write one sentence about something on a shelf in your room.  
   must use: There are … on the shelf. · exactly one sentence · on the shelf · a thing word

6. Write one sentence about what is on the board in your classroom.  
   must use: There are … on the board. · exactly one sentence · on the board · a school word

7. A friend can't find her keys. Write one sentence to help her.  
   must use: They're on the … · exactly one sentence · on the · a furniture word

8. Write two sentences: one about a thing on a table and one about a thing on a wall.  
   must use: … on the table. / … on the wall. · exactly two sentences · on the table · on the wall
