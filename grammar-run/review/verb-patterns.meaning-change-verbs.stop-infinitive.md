   I was thirsty, so I stopped drinking some water at the **shop**, **Zarina**.

   key: to drink  
   why: Thirsty = stopped in order to drink: to drink.

2. _Correct the mistake._  
   We were lost, so we stopped ask a man in **Bukhara**, **Jamshid**.

   why: Lost = stopped in order to ask: to ask.

   must use: … so I stopped to … · exactly one sentence · stopped to · so

3. Write one sentence about stopping to talk to someone.  
   must use: I stopped to talk to … · exactly one sentence · stopped to talk · a person

4. Write one sentence with stopped to and one with stopped + -ing.  
   must use: purpose vs end · exactly two sentences · stopped to + base · stopped + -ing

5. Write one sentence about stopping to take a photo.  
   must use: We stopped to take a photo of … · exactly one sentence · stopped to take · a place

6. Write one sentence about a time you stopped to help someone.  
   must use: I stopped to help … · exactly one sentence · stopped to help · past

   must use: I stopped to buy / look … · exactly one sentence · stopped to · your town

   must use: Why did you stop to …? · exactly one question · stop to · a question mark

   target: stopped to · look for: stopped to · past simple · three or four sentences

3. Write three or four sentences about a hike in the mountains with stopped to.  
   target: stopped + to + base · look for: stopped to · so · three or four sentences

   target: stop + -ing vs stop + to · look for: stopped + -ing · stopped to · three or four sentences

   target: stopped to help · look for: stopped to · past · three or four sentences
