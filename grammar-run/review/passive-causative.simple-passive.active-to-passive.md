2. Write one passive sentence about a book or song and who wrote it.  
   must use: … was written by … · exactly one sentence · was written by · a name

3. Write one passive sentence about a meal and who cooks it in your family.  
   must use: … is cooked by … · exactly one sentence · is cooked by · a food word

4. Write one passive sentence about a photo and who took it.  
   must use: This photo was taken by … · exactly one sentence · was taken by · a person word

5. Write one passive sentence about a subject and who teaches it.  
   must use: … is taught by … · exactly one sentence · is taught by · a subject word

6. Write one active sentence, then the same idea in the passive.  
   must use: My dad built … / … was built by my dad. · exactly two sentences · the same tense in both · by

7. Write one passive sentence about something that is made or grown by people in your region.  
   must use: … are grown by … · exactly one sentence · is or are + past participle + by · a place word

8. Write two passive sentences with by: one in the present and one in the past.  
   must use: … is … by … / … was … by … · exactly two sentences · is or are + by · was or were + by

## express (5)

1. Describe your home: who built it, who painted it and who cleans it.  
   target: It was built by … / It is cleaned by … · look for: was + past participle + by · is + past participle + by · three or four sentences

2. Describe a family celebration: what was made or cooked, and by whom?  
   target: The plov was cooked by … / The cake was made by … · look for: was + by twice · a food word · three or four sentences

3. Describe your favourite book, film or song: who wrote or made it, and who it is loved by.  
   target: It was written by … · look for: was written by or was made by · is + past participle + by · three or four sentences

4. Describe your school: who teaches which subjects and who looks after the building.  
   target: English is taught by … · look for: is taught by · are + past participle + by · three or four sentences

5. Describe a famous place in your country: who built it and who visits it now.  
   target: It was built by … / It is visited by … · look for: was built by · is visited by · three or four sentences
