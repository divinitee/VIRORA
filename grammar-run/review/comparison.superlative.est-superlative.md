   → Aziz is ______ (tall) boy in our **year**. He plays **basketball**.  
   key: the tallest  
   why: Taller than all the others means the tallest. Tall is a short adjective, so the superlative is the + -est: the tallest.

## express (5)

1. Describe your family. Who is the oldest, the youngest, the tallest and the funniest? Write three or four sentences.  
   target: the + -est superlatives about people · look for: the before every superlative · -est forms · y changed to i: funniest

   target: the + -est superlatives about time · look for: the busiest, the longest, the easiest · of my week or in my week · y changed to i

   target: the + -est superlatives about weather · look for: the hottest, the coldest, the nicest · month names · no most before short adjectives

   target: the + -est superlatives about a group · look for: the before every superlative · in our class or in my group · no comparative where a superlative is needed
