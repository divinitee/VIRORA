## create (8)

1. Write one sentence about something you want right now.  
   must use: I want (not I am wanting) · exactly one sentence · must include right now · must use want

2. Write one sentence about a food you like and why.  
   must use: like or likes in the present simple · exactly one sentence · must use because · no -ing with like

3. Write one sentence about something that belongs to a family member.  
   must use: belongs to · exactly one sentence · must name a family member · must use belong

4. Write one sentence about something you need for school or work this week.  
   must use: need in the present simple · exactly one sentence · must include this week · no -ing with need

5. Write one sentence about a person you know well and what they know about you.  
   must use: knows or know · exactly one sentence · must use know · present simple only

6. Write one sentence saying how much something costs in your town.  
   must use: costs or cost · exactly one sentence · must include a price · must use cost

7. Write one sentence agreeing with a friend's idea.  
   must use: I agree (not I am agree) · exactly one sentence · must use agree · must say what the idea is

8. Write two sentences: one with something you are doing now, and one with a state verb about how you feel about it.  
   must use: present continuous for the action, present simple for the state verb · exactly two sentences · first sentence present continuous · second sentence with like, love, hate or want

## express (5)

1. Describe your favourite place in your town: what you like about it and what you don't like. Write three or four sentences.  
   target: state verbs in the present simple · look for: like, love or hate without -ing · present simple · because to give reasons

2. You are in a shop. Write what you want, what you need and how much things cost. Write three or four sentences.  
   target: want, need and cost in the present simple · look for: no -ing with want, need or cost · -s with singular subjects · prices or amounts

3. Describe a friend: what they like, what they know a lot about and what they want in the future. Write three or four sentences.  
   target: state verbs with he or she · look for: likes, knows, wants with -s · no is + -ing with state verbs · present simple

4. Write a short message to a teacher saying what you understand and what you don't understand in the lesson. Write three or four sentences.  
   target: understand and know in the present simple · look for: I understand / I don't understand · no am understanding · a clear question

5. Describe what you are doing right now and how you feel about it. Write three or four sentences.  
   target: present continuous for actions, present simple for state verbs · look for: am + -ing for actions · like, want or need in the present simple · right now or at the moment
