## express (5)

1. What are you good at doing and bad at doing? Write three or four sentences.  
   target: at + -ing · look for: good at + -ing · bad at + -ing · no bare verb after at

2. Describe your evening. What do you do after coming home and before going to bed? Write three or four sentences.  
   target: after / before + -ing · look for: after + -ing · before + -ing · no to + verb after the preposition

3. How do you learn English words? Write three or four sentences with by + -ing.  
   target: by + -ing · look for: by + -ing at least twice · an -ing form after every preposition · different verbs

4. Write a short thank-you message to a friend or teacher who helped you. Three or four sentences.  
   target: Thank you for + -ing · look for: for + -ing · another preposition + -ing · polite tone

5. What are you tired of doing, and what are you thinking about doing this year? Write three or four sentences.  
   target: tired of / thinking about + -ing · look for: tired of + -ing · thinking about + -ing · no bare verb after the preposition
