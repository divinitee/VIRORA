# adj-adv.adverb-form.adverb-vs-adjective-sense — review sample (B1)

Each item shows one random filler combination (in bold). Mark anything a teacher wouldn't accept.

## choose (20)

1. I haven't seen **your brother** much ______. Is everything OK, **Akmal**?  
   options: ✔ lately · late · later · latest  
   why: "haven't … much" + a recent period: lately = recently.

2. Have you read any good **books** ______, **Otabek**?  
   options: late · later · latest · ✔ lately  
   why: a question about the recent past: lately = recently.

3. Prices at the **supermarket** have gone up a lot ______, **Akmal**.  
   options: later · latest · ✔ lately · late  
   why: "have gone up" in the recent past: lately = recently.

4. The **bus** came twenty minutes ______, so I missed the lesson, **Dilnoza**.  
   options: lated · ✔ late · lately · latest  
   why: twenty minutes after the right time: late = not on time / at a late hour.

5. We stayed up ______ to watch the **film**, **Zarina**.  
   options: ✔ late · lately · latest · lated  
   why: until a late hour: late = not on time / at a late hour.

6. I can ______ hear you. Please speak louder, **Feruza**.  
   options: hard · harder · hardest · ✔ hardly  
   why: "speak louder" = I almost can't hear: hardly = almost not.

7. There was ______ any food left at the **dinner**, **Ulugbek**.  
   options: harder · hardest · ✔ hardly · hard  
   why: "any food left" = almost no food: hardly = almost not.

8. My grandpa ______ ever uses his **phone**, **Feruza**.  
   options: hardest · ✔ hardly · hard · harder  
   why: "ever" = almost never: hardly = almost not.

9. I was so tired that I could ______ walk after the **match**, **Akmal**.  
   options: ✔ hardly · hard · harder · hardest  
   why: so tired = almost couldn't walk: hardly = almost not.

10. My sister studies very ______ for her **exams**, **Akmal**.  
   options: hardly · hardest · hardness · ✔ hard  
   why: "very" + with a lot of effort: hard = with a lot of effort.

11. It rained ______ all night in **Fergana**, **Sherzod**.  
   options: hardest · hardness · ✔ hard · hardly  
   why: heavy rain = rained hard: hard = with a lot of effort.

12. I ______ fell off my **bike** on the ice, **Gulnora**.  
   options: nearest · ✔ nearly · near · nearer  
   why: almost fell, but didn't: nearly = almost.

13. We ______ missed the **start**. We ran the last hundred metres, **Otabek**!  
   options: ✔ nearly · near · nearer · nearest  
   why: almost missed it: nearly = almost.

14. I've ______ finished my **homework**, **Dilnoza**. Just one page left.  
   options: near · nearer · nearest · ✔ nearly  
   why: "Just one page left" = almost finished: nearly = almost.

15. We live ______ the **park**, so we walk there every day, **Feruza**.  
   options: nearness · neared · ✔ near · nearly  
   why: close to the park: near = close to (a place).

16. Is there a pharmacy ______ here, **Ulugbek**?  
   options: neared · ✔ near · nearly · nearness  
   why: close to this place: near = close to (a place).

17. This **museum** is ______ recommended by the guidebook, **Feruza**.  
   options: ✔ highly · high · higher · height  
   why: very strongly recommended: highly = very / very well.

18. Everyone at the **school** speaks ______ of our new **manager**.  
   options: high · higher · height · ✔ highly  
   why: speak highly of = say very good things about: highly = very / very well.

19. The plane flew very ______ over the **clouds**, **Sherzod**.  
   options: height · highness · ✔ high · highly  
   why: far up in the sky: high = far up.

20. My cousin can jump really ______, **Ulugbek**. He plays basketball.  
   options: highness · ✔ high · highly · height  
   why: far up: high = far up.
