## transform (15)

1. _The first sentence is about now. Complete the second sentence as a fact that is always true._  
   The water in the **teapot** on the **stove** is boiling now.  
   → Water ______ (boil) at 100 degrees, so the **teapot** on the **stove** gets very hot.  
   key: boils  
   why: A fact about water in general takes the present simple: boils, not is boiling.

2. _The first sentence is about now. Complete the second sentence as a fact that is always true._  
   Look, the ice on the **path** is melting in the **sunshine**.  
   → Ice ______ (melt) when it gets warm, so the **path** is wet in the **sunshine**.  
   key: melts  
   why: A rule about ice in general takes the present simple: melts.

3. _The first sentence is about now. Complete the second sentence as a fact that is always true._  
   Our cat is sleeping on the **sofa** in the **bedroom** right now.  
   → Cats ______ (sleep) a lot, so you often find ours on the **sofa** in the **bedroom**.  
   key: sleep  
   why: Cats in general is plural, and a general fact takes the present simple: sleep.

4. _The first sentence is about now. Complete the second sentence as a fact that is always true._  
   The sun is going down behind the **trees** now. We can see it from the **kitchen**.  
   → The sun ______ (set) in the west, so we see it go down behind the **trees** from the **kitchen**.  
   key: sets  
   why: Where the sun goes down is always true, so use the present simple: sets.

5. _The first sentence is about now. Complete the second sentence as a fact that is always true._

   key: grow  
   why: Plants is plural, and how plants grow is a general fact: grow.
