## transform (15)

1. _Join the two sentences with when. Don't use will after when._  
   Aziz will arrive at the **bus station**. Then he'll **take a taxi**.  
   → Aziz will **take a taxi** when he ______ (arrive) at the **bus station**.  
   key: arrives  
   why: After when, use the present simple (arrives), not will arrive.

2. _Join the two sentences with when. Don't use will after when._  
   The film will finish. Then we'll go for **a walk**.  
   → We'll go for **a walk** when the film ______ (finish), **Lola**.  
   key: finishes  
   why: After when, use the present simple: when the film finishes.

3. _Join the two sentences with when. Don't use will after when._  
   Mum will come home. Then we'll have **dinner**.  
   → We'll have **dinner** when Mum ______ (come) home, **Aziz**.  
   key: comes  
   why: After when, use the present simple: when Mum comes.

4. _Join the two sentences with when. Don't use will after when._  
   It will stop raining. Then the children will play **in the yard**.  
   → The children will play **in the yard** when it ______ (stop) raining, **Mum**.  
   key: stops  
   why: After when, use the present simple: when it stops.

5. _Join the two sentences with when. Don't use will after when._  
   The shop will open. Then I'll buy **some milk**.  
   → I'll buy **some milk** when the shop ______ (open), **Olga**.  
   key: opens  
   why: After when, use the present simple: when the shop opens.

6. _Rewrite with when. Don't use will after when._  
   First I'll finish my **essay**. Then I'll **watch a film**.

7. _Rewrite with when. Don't use will after when._

## create (8)

1. Write one sentence about what you will do when you finish school or university.  
   must use: When I + present simple, I'll … · exactly one sentence · must start with When I · no will after when

2. Write one sentence about what you will do when you get home today.  
   must use: … when I get home · exactly one sentence · must use when I get home · will in the other part

3. Write one sentence about a friend: what will they do when they arrive?  
   must use: when he / she arrives · exactly one sentence · must name the friend · present simple with -s after when

4. Write one sentence about what you will buy when you have enough money.  
   must use: when I have enough money · exactly one sentence · must use when · no will after when

5. Write one sentence asking a friend to text you when they arrive.  
   must use: Text me when you … · exactly one sentence · must use when you · present simple after when

6. Write one sentence about what you will do when the weather gets warm.  
   must use: when it gets warm · exactly one sentence · must use when it · present simple with -s after when

7. Write one sentence about your plans when the holidays start.  
   must use: when the holidays start · exactly one sentence · must use when · no will after when

8. Write two sentences about your future: one starting with When, one with when in the middle.  
   must use: when + present simple · exactly two sentences · when at the start of one · no will after when

## express (5)

1. What will you do when you finish school or university? Write three or four sentences.  
   target: when + present simple for future time · look for: When I + present simple · I'll / I want to in the other part · no will after when

2. Your friend is visiting your city. Write a message: what will you do when they arrive?  
   target: when + present simple · look for: when you arrive / get here · we'll + verb · no will after when

3. Describe your plans for the summer holidays. Use when two or three times.  
   target: future time clauses with when · look for: when the holidays start · when I + present simple · will in the main part

4. Write instructions for a friend who will look after your pet while you travel.  
   target: when + present simple in instructions · look for: when you + present simple · imperatives like feed / give · no will after when

5. Imagine your life in ten years. What will you do when you have a job, money and free time?  
   target: when + present simple · look for: when I have … · I'll + verb · two or more when clauses
