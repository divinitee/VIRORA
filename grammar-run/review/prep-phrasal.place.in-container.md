## transform (15)

1. _Answer the question. Use in and the fridge._  
   Where's the **cheese**?  
   → It's ______ fridge, **Timur**.  
   key: in the  
   why: The fridge is a container: in the fridge.

2. _Answer the question. Use in and the garden._  
   Where are the **children**?  
   → They're ______ garden, **Olga**.  
   key: in the  
   why: A garden has walls or a fence around it: in the garden.

3. _Answer the question. Use in and your bag._  
   Where's my **pen**?  
   → It's ______ bag, **Aziz**.  
   key: in your  
   why: A bag is a container: in your bag.

4. _Answer the question. Use in and the kitchen._  
   Where's **Grandma**?  
   → She's ______ kitchen, **Timur**.  
   key: in the  
   why: A kitchen is a room: in the kitchen.

5. _Answer the question. Use in and the drawer._  
   Where are the **knives**?  
   → They're ______ drawer, **Olga**.  
   key: in the  
   why: A drawer is a container: in the drawer.
