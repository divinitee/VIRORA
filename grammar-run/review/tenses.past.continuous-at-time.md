2. Write one sentence about what your family was doing at 7 this morning.  
   must use: was or were + -ing · exactly one sentence · must include at 7 this morning · must name a family member

   must use: was raining, was snowing or was shining · exactly one sentence · must include a clock time · must use it or the sun

   must use: was + -ing · exactly one sentence · must include a time · must use the past continuous

   must use: was + -ing · exactly one sentence · must include at midnight · must name a place

8. Write two sentences: what you do at 7 pm every day, and what you were doing at 7 pm yesterday.  
   must use: present simple, then past continuous · exactly two sentences · first sentence present simple · second sentence with was + -ing

## express (5)

1. Describe your evening yesterday. Say what you were doing at three different times. Write three or four sentences.  
   target: past continuous at specific past times · look for: at + a clock time · was or were + -ing · three different actions

2. A friend asks where everyone in your family was at 8 pm last Saturday. Answer in three or four sentences.  
   target: past continuous for actions in progress at a time · look for: at 8 pm last Saturday · different family members · was or were + -ing

3. Describe what was happening in your school or office at 10 o'clock yesterday morning. Write three or four sentences.  
   target: past continuous for scenes at a past moment · look for: at 10 o'clock yesterday morning · several people doing things · were + -ing

4. Think of a special day (a holiday or a wedding). What were people doing at one moment of that day? Write three or four sentences.  
   target: past continuous to describe a scene · look for: a clear time · was or were + -ing · who, where and what

5. Compare a normal Sunday with last Sunday: what do you usually do at noon, and what were you doing at noon last Sunday? Write three or four sentences.  
   target: present simple for routine, past continuous for a past moment · look for: usually + present simple · at noon last Sunday + was + -ing · but to show the contrast
