2. Write one sentence about when a train, bus or flight you know leaves.  
   must use: leaves at + time · exactly one sentence · leaves or departs · a future time word

3. Write one question about when a shop or museum opens tomorrow.  
   must use: What time does / do … open? · exactly one question · does or do · tomorrow

4. Write one sentence about when your holidays begin or end.  
   must use: present simple for a fixed date · exactly one sentence · begin / end · next or a date

5. Write one sentence about a film or match on TV this week and when it starts.  
   must use: starts at + time · exactly one sentence · present simple · a day and a time

6. Write one sentence with doesn't about a bus or shop timetable.  
   must use: doesn't + base verb · exactly one sentence · doesn't + base form · a time or day

7. Write one question to a friend about when their exams or course start.  
   must use: When do your … start? · exactly one question · do + your · base verb

8. Write two sentences about tomorrow: one fixed timetable and one plan of your own.  
   must use: present simple vs going to · exactly two sentences · present simple for the timetable · going to for your plan

## express (5)

1. Describe your timetable for tomorrow: when your lessons or work start and finish. Write three or four sentences.  
   target: present simple for a timetable · look for: starts / finishes · at + time · three or four sentences

2. You are going on a trip by train or plane. Tell a friend the times. Write three or four sentences.  
   target: leaves / arrives / lands · look for: present simple for times · tomorrow or next · a place

3. Write a short message to a friend about a concert or match: when it starts, when it ends, when the last bus leaves.  
   target: present simple for events · look for: starts / ends · leaves · times

4. Ask a friend three questions about a trip timetable.  
   target: What time does …? · look for: does / do · base verb after does · three questions

5. Describe the opening hours of a shop, museum or café near you. Write three or four sentences.  
   target: opens / closes · look for: opens / closes · doesn't open until · days and times
