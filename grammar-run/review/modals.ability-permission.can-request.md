## transform (15)

   Close the **window**, **Lola**.  
   → ______ (close) the **window**, please, **Lola**?  
   key: can you close  
   why: A polite request: Can you + base verb … , please?

2. _Make it a polite request with Can you … , please?_  
   Turn down the **volume** **a lot**.  
   → ______ (turn) down the **volume** **a lot**, please?  
   key: can you turn  
   why: Make the order polite: Can you turn … , please?

## create (8)

1. You are in class and you forgot your pen. Ask a classmate.  
   must use: Can I borrow … ? · exactly one question · must use Can I · must use please

2. You are at a friend's house and you are thirsty. Ask politely.  
   must use: Can I have … , please? · exactly one question · must use Can I have · must name a drink

3. Ask your teacher to explain something again.  
   must use: Can you + base verb … , please? · exactly one question · must use Can you · must use again

4. Ask a stranger to take a photo of you.  
   must use: Can you take a photo … ? · exactly one question · must use Can you · must start with Excuse me

5. Ask your parents for permission to go somewhere this weekend.  
   must use: Can I go … ? · exactly one question · must use Can I · must name a place

6. Ask a shop assistant to help you find something.  
   must use: Can you help me … ? · exactly one question · must use Can you help · must name the thing

7. Ask if your friend can come with you to a party.  
   must use: Can my friend come … ? · exactly one question · must start with Can my friend · base verb after the person

8. Write two requests: one asking to do something yourself, one asking someone else to do something.  
   must use: Can I … ? and Can you … ? · exactly two questions · one with Can I, one with Can you · please in at least one

## express (5)

1. You are at a café. Write a short dialogue with the waiter: ask for the menu, order, and ask for the bill.  
   target: Can I … ? / Can you … ? requests · look for: Can I have … , please? · Can you bring … ? · please and thank you

2. You are staying with a host family abroad. Write four polite requests you might need on the first day.  
   target: Can I … ? and Can you … ? · look for: Can I + base verb · Can you + base verb · please

3. Your friend is helping you move to a new flat. Write four requests to your friend.  
   target: Can you … , please? · look for: Can you + base verb · four different verbs · no Do you … ? for requests

4. Write a short message to your teacher asking for two things (for example, to leave early and to send you the homework).  
   target: Can I … ? and Can you … ? · look for: Can I + base verb · Can you + base verb · a polite greeting and thank you

5. You are lost in a new city. Write a short dialogue asking a person on the street for help.  
   target: Can you … ? requests · look for: Excuse me · Can you tell / show me … ? · please and thank you
