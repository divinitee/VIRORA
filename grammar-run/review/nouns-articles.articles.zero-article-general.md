   **Our teacher** says the money can't buy the happiness, **Lola**.  
   → **Our teacher** says ______ can't buy ______, **Lola**.  
   key: money + happiness  
   why: General statement about money and happiness: no article on either.

## create (8)

1. Write one general sentence about an animal you like.  
   must use: … (plural animal) are / can … · exactly one sentence · a plural noun with no article · a present verb

2. Write one sentence about what money can or can't buy.  
   must use: Money can't buy … · exactly one sentence · money with no article · can or can't

3. Write one general sentence about what is good for your health.  
   must use: … is / are good for … · exactly one sentence · a general noun with no article · good for

4. Write one general sentence about teenagers or children.  
   must use: Teenagers / Children often … · exactly one sentence · a plural noun with no article · often or usually

5. Write one sentence about a school subject you like and why.  
   must use: I like … because … · exactly one sentence · a subject with no article · because

6. Write two sentences: one about cats in general, one about a specific cat or cats you know.  
   must use: Cats are … The cats in my … are … · exactly two sentences · no article for the general one · the for the specific one

7. Write one general sentence about food or drink in your family.  
   must use: In my family we drink / eat … · exactly one sentence · a food or drink with no article · a present verb

8. Write one sentence about what you think about music or sport.  
   must use: I think music / sport … · exactly one sentence · no article · I think

## express (5)

1. What makes people happy? Write your opinion.  
   target: Money / Friends / Health … make people happy · look for: general nouns with no article · I think · three or four sentences

2. Describe dogs and cats in general. Which do you prefer, and why?  
   target: Dogs are … Cats are … · look for: plural nouns with no article · prefer · three or four sentences

3. Give advice to a younger student about healthy food and sport.  
   target: Vegetables are … Sport is … · look for: general nouns with no article · should or good for · three or four sentences

4. Write about a school subject you love and a subject you find hard.  
   target: I love history … Maths is … · look for: subjects with no article · because · three or four sentences

5. Compare young people and old people in your family: what do they like?  
   target: Young people like … Old people … · look for: general plurals with no article · a contrast word (but) · three or four sentences
