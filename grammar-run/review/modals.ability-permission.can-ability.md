## transform (15)

1. _Rewrite the sentence with can._  
   My sister is able to speak **Turkish** **well**.  
   → My sister ______ (speak) **Turkish** **well**.  
   key: can speak  
   why: Is able to means can. Use can + base verb: can speak.

2. _Rewrite the sentence with can._  
   We are able to see **the airport** from **the tenth floor**.  
   → We ______ (see) **the airport** from **the tenth floor**.  
   key: can see  
   why: Are able to means can: can see. Can is the same for all people.

3. _Rewrite the sentence with can._  
   Tigers are able to swim **a long way** in **cold water**.  
   → Tigers ______ (swim) **a long way** in **cold water**.  
   key: can swim  
   why: Are able to means can: can swim.

4. _Rewrite the sentence with can._  
   I'm able to finish the **crossword** in **an hour**.  
   → I ______ (finish) the **crossword** in **an hour**.  
   key: can finish  
   why: Am able to means can: can finish. No am before can.

5. _Rewrite the sentence with can._  
   Our son is able to tie his **shoes** now.  
   → Our son ______ (tie) his **shoes** now.  
   key: can tie  
   why: Is able to means can: can tie. No is before can.

6. _Say the same thing with can._  
   **My uncle** knows how to drive a **bus**.

7. _Say the same thing with can._

## create (8)

1. Write one sentence about a sport you can play.  
   must use: I can + base verb · exactly one sentence · must use I can · must name a sport

2. Write one sentence about a language you can speak and how well.  
   must use: can speak · exactly one sentence · must name a language · must say how well (a little, very well…)

3. Write one sentence about something a family member can do very well.  
   must use: he / she can + base verb · exactly one sentence · must name a family member · no -s on can or on the verb

4. Write one sentence about something an animal can do.  
   must use: can + base verb · exactly one sentence · must name an animal · must use can

5. Write one sentence about something you can cook.  
   must use: I can cook · exactly one sentence · must name a dish · must use can + cook

6. Write one sentence about something your phone or computer can do.  
   must use: it can + base verb · exactly one sentence · must use it can or my phone can · no to after can

7. Write one sentence about something your best friend can do and you can do too.  
   must use: can + base verb for two people · exactly one sentence · must name your friend · must use can twice or both … can

8. Write two sentences about two different things you can do with your hands.  
   must use: I can + base verb · exactly two sentences · two different verbs · no -ing after can

## express (5)

1. You want to join a summer camp. Write about the things you can do (sports, music, languages, cooking). Write three or four sentences.  
   target: can + base verb for abilities · look for: I can + base verb · three or more different verbs · words like very well or a little

2. Describe a talented person you know. What can they do? Write three or four sentences.  
   target: he / she can + base verb · look for: he can / she can · no -s on the verb after can · a detail like very well or fast

3. Write a short profile for a job website: say what you can do and what languages you can speak.  
   target: can for skills · look for: I can + base verb · can speak + language · no to after can

4. Describe your favourite animal. What can it do? Write three or four sentences.  
   target: it can / they can + base verb · look for: it can or they can · different action verbs · no -ing after can

5. Write about the members of your family and one special thing each person can do.  
   target: can + base verb with different subjects · look for: my mother can, my brother can… · the same form of can for everyone · base verb after can
