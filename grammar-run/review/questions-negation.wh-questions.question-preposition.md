## transform (15)

1. _Make the question everyday English: put the preposition at the end._  
   For whom are you waiting at the **bus stop**, **Timur**?  
   → ______ are you waiting ______ at the **bus stop**, **Timur**?  
   key: Who + for  
   why: Everyday English ends with the preposition: Who … for?

2. _Make the question everyday English: put the preposition at the end._  
   About what are you and **your sister** talking, **Lola**?  
   → ______ are you and **your sister** talking ______, **Lola**?  
   key: What + about  
   why: The verb is talk about. In a wh-question the preposition stays: it goes at the end of the question.

3. _Make the question everyday English: put the preposition at the end._  
   With whom does your sister live in **Bukhara**, **Aziz**?  
   → ______ does your sister live ______ in **Bukhara**, **Aziz**?  
   key: Who + with  
   why: The verb is live with. In a wh-question the preposition stays: it goes at the end of the question.

4. _Make the question everyday English: put the preposition at the end._  
   To whom does this **bike** belong, **Timur**?

   why: Not To whom: Who does this … belong to?
