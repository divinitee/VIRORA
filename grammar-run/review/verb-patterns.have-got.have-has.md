## transform (15)

1. _Rewrite the sentence about one person. Use has._  
   I have a new **bag**.  
   → **Aziz** ______ a new **bag**.  
   key: has  
   why: One person (he or she) → has.

2. _Rewrite the sentence about one person. Use has._  
   We have a small **car**.  
   → Our **teacher** ______ a small **car**.  
   key: has  
   why: One person → has, not have.

3. _Rewrite the sentence about one person. Use has._  
   My brothers have a **guitar** in **the flat**.  
   → My brother ______ a **guitar** in **the flat**.  
   key: has  
   why: One brother → has, not have.

4. _Rewrite the sentence about one person. Use has._  
   They have got a **blue** car.  
   → Our **uncle** ______ got a **blue** car.  
   key: has  
   why: One person → has got, not have got.

5. _Rewrite the sentence about one person. Use has._  
   My friends have a **cat** called **Snow**.  
   → My friend ______ a **cat** called **Snow**.  
   key: has  
   why: One friend → has, not have.

6. _Rewrite the sentence about more people. Use have._  
   She has two **cats**, **Olga**.  
   → They ______ two **cats**, **Olga**.  
   key: have  
   why: With they, use have: they have.

7. _Rewrite the sentence about more people. Use have._  
   My sister has long **brown** hair and **green** eyes.  
   → My sisters ______ long **brown** hair and **green** eyes.  
   key: have  
   why: More than one sister → have.

8. _Rewrite the sentence about more people. Use have._  
   He has got **two** cousins in **Fergana**.  
   → We ______ got **two** cousins in **Fergana**.  
   key: have  
   why: With we, use have got.

9. _Rewrite the sentence about more people. Use have._  
   My cousin has a flat near the **bazaar** in **Fergana**.  
   → My cousins ______ a flat near the **bazaar** in **Fergana**.  
   key: have  
   why: More than one cousin → have.

10. _Rewrite the sentence about more people. Use have._  
   Lola has a new **phone**.  
   → Lola and **Kamila** ______ a new **phone**.  
   key: have  
   why: Two people → have, not has.

11. _Correct the mistake._  
   She have a **dog** called **Snow**.  
   → She ______ a **dog** called **Snow**.  
   key: has  
   why: She → has, not have.

12. _Correct the mistake._  
   I has two **aunts** in **Bukhara**.  
   → I ______ two **aunts** in **Bukhara**.  
   key: have  
   why: I → have, not has.

13. _Correct the mistake._  
   My **parents** has a **white** car.  
   → My **parents** ______ a **white** car.  
   key: have  
   why: More than one person → have, not has.

14. _Correct the mistake._  
   **Timur** have got a new **watch**.  
   → **Timur** ______ got a new **watch**.  
   key: has  
   why: One person → has got.

15. _Correct the mistake._  
   Our **new flat** have **five** rooms.  
   → Our **new flat** ______ **five** rooms.  
   key: has  
   why: Our flat or house is one thing → has.

## create (8)

1. Write one sentence about a thing you have in your room.  
   must use: I have / I've got · exactly one sentence · start with I have or I've got · a thing word

2. Write one sentence about a pet you or a friend has.  
   must use: have / has + a pet · exactly one sentence · have or has · an animal word

3. Write one sentence about your brother, sister or cousin. What has he or she got?  
   must use: he / she has · exactly one sentence · has or has got · a family word

4. Write one sentence about your flat or house. How many rooms has it got?  
   must use: it has / our flat has · exactly one sentence · has · a number

5. Write one sentence about your best friend's hair or eyes.  
   must use: he / she has · exactly one sentence · has · a colour word

6. Write one sentence about your school. What has it got?  
   must use: our school has · exactly one sentence · has · a place word

7. Write one sentence about your grandparents' home.  
   must use: they have · exactly one sentence · have · a home word

8. Write two sentences: one thing you have and one thing your friend has.  
   must use: I have / he or she has · exactly two sentences · have once · has once

## express (5)

1. Describe your family. Who is in it, and what have they got?  
   target: have / has · look for: have · has · three or four sentences

2. Describe your room. What have you got in it?  
   target: I have / I've got · look for: I have · a furniture word · three or four sentences

3. Describe your flat or house for a new friend.  
   target: it has / we have · look for: has · have · three or four sentences

4. Describe your best friend: hair, eyes, pets and things.  
   target: he / she has · look for: has twice · a colour word · three or four sentences

5. Describe a relative in another town or village. What has he or she got there?  
   target: he / she has · look for: has · a place word · three or four sentences
