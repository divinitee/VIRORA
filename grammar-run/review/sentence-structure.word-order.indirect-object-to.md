## transform (15)

1. _Rewrite with it or them and to or for._  
   I gave my sister the **book**, **Olga**.  
   → I gave ______ ______ my sister, **Olga**.  
   key: it + to  
   why: With it at the end: gave it to.

2. _Rewrite with it or them and to or for._  
   Dad bought Mum the **flowers**, **Olga**.  
   → Dad bought ______ ______ Mum, **Olga**.  
   key: them + for  
   why: With them, buy uses for: bought them for.

3. _Rewrite with it or them and to or for._  
   Can you show me the **videos**, **Timur**?  
   → Can you show ______ ______ me, **Timur**?  
   key: them + to  
   why: With them, show uses to: show them to.

## create (8)

1. Write one sentence asking a teacher to explain something to you.  
   must use: Could you explain … to me? · exactly one sentence · explain + thing + to me · no explain me

2. Write one sentence about a present you bought for someone.  
   must use: I bought … for my … · exactly one sentence · bought + thing + for + person · a past verb

3. Write one sentence about something you lent to a friend. Use it.  
   must use: I lent it to … · exactly one sentence · lent it to · a person

4. Write one sentence about introducing a friend to your family.  
   must use: I introduced … to my … · exactly one sentence · introduce + person + to · a past verb

5. Write one sentence about food someone cooked for you.  
   must use: My … cooked … for me. · exactly one sentence · cooked + food + for · a person

6. Write one sentence describing your town to a new friend.  
   must use: Let me describe my town to you: … · exactly one sentence · describe + thing + to · a place

7. Write one sentence about photos you showed someone. Use them.  
   must use: I showed them to … · exactly one sentence · showed them to · a person

8. Write two sentences: one with to + person and one with for + person.  
   must use: I gave … to … / I made … for … · exactly two sentences · to + person · for + person

## express (5)

1. Describe a family birthday: what people bought, made or gave for the birthday person.  
   target: We bought … for her. My brother gave … to her. · look for: to and for · past verbs · three or four sentences

2. Write a message to a classmate asking them to explain a lesson to you.  
   target: Could you explain … to me? Can you send … to me? · look for: explain … to me · to + person · three or four sentences

3. Describe how you helped a guest who came to your town.  
   target: I showed the old town to her. I found a hotel for her. · look for: to and for · past verbs · three or four sentences

4. Describe things you lent or gave to friends this year.  
   target: I lent my … to … / I gave it to … · look for: to + person · it or them · three or four sentences

5. Write about a meal you cooked for your family or friends.  
   target: I cooked … for … and gave … to … · look for: to and for · past verbs · three or four sentences
