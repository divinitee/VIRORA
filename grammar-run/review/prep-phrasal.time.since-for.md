## transform (15)

1. _Say the same thing with for and the length of time._  
   It is Friday. **Lola** has been ill since Monday, **Grandma**.  
   → **Lola** has been ill ______ four days, **Grandma**.  
   key: for  
   why: Monday to Friday is four days. A length of time takes for.

2. _Say the same thing with for and the length of time._  
   It is 2026. We have lived in this **street** since 2021, **Olga**.  
   → We have lived in this **street** ______ five years, **Olga**.  
   key: for  
   why: 2021 to 2026 is five years. A length of time takes for.

3. _Say the same thing with for and the length of time._  
   It is noon. The **twins** have played **football** since ten o'clock.  
   → The **twins** have played **football** ______ two hours.  
   key: for  
   why: Ten o'clock to noon is two hours. A length of time takes for.

4. _Say the same thing with for and the length of time._

   why: March to June is three months. A length of time takes for.

5. _Say the same thing with for and the length of time._
